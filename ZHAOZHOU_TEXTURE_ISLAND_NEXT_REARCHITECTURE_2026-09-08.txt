0. DECISION IN ONE PAGE
===============================================================================

KEEP V3. Do not restart the owner, undo T2, or replace the retained arithmetic.
The next productive architecture is:

  accepted fragment + reserved owner
      -> explicitly owned front-end preparation
      -> tagged coordinate/descriptor delivery
      -> expander with no independent allocation authority
      -> planner consuming a captured, resolved descriptor
      -> cache
      -> ONE synchronous metadata join, with space reserved for reads in flight
      -> independent class queues carrying the complete response packet
      -> decode/filter/palette
      -> existing V3 capture / snapshot / claim / write / publish machinery
      -> aligned material + sample packet
      -> COMBINE, with actual acceptance still authorizing FINAL
      -> required post-material/post-toon Fog in the appropriate render closure
      -> ordered external retirement, after all owner-scoped obligations end.

The principal area experiment is NOT "put every array into RAM". It is removing
several independent metadata read sites by moving the join to the common cache-
response stream BEFORE it splits into CLUT, nearest and bilinear paths. That
makes a real one-writer/one-reader metadata bank possible without serializing
several downstream readers or duplicating the bank for each class. [R03, R08]

The principal timing experiment, conditional on the fresh V3 path report, is
NOT another indiscriminate RCP output register. Stage A already inserted the
RCP-to-PERSPUV boundary. The newest measured legacy-island gate instead runs
from raw fragment depth into RCP context state. Split context reservation,
normalization and seed preparation while preserving the exact reciprocal law
and four-job scheduler. [R06, R12]

Before either experiment becomes the production baseline, close the remaining
contract holes. The green V3 source still contains old FRAGROB signals feeding
fault latches and an invalid-class counter. Zero-required-source owners can
become COMBINE-ready at admission even though the top sends their front-end work
through RCP/PERSPUV/expansion. The first is a source-confirmed observability bug;
the second is an unclosed lifetime obligation, not a claimed reproduced data-
corruption trace. Address them with focused tests and an explicit completion
contract, not by treating 119 green checks as universal correctness. [R03, R04]

The first V3 fit is useful even if it misses historical subsystem allocations.
Let it finish on its frozen specimen. Record its failures honestly. Do not
change the source under that run, and do not throw away the measurement because
it characterizes a compatibility profile rather than the eventual full renderer.

After that receipt, choose the next patch by evidence:

  Long raw-depth / normalization / seed path -> section 6.
  Large expanded metadata / multi-read glue -> section 7.
  Owner request-to-issue control dominates -> section 9.
  Material-join or expander bubbles limit workload -> sections 8 and 10.
  Different measured family -> investigate that family; do not force this list.

An approximately 11,000-ALM texture island at a genuine 100 MHz could be a good
outcome. This review does NOT establish the whole console's remaining budget,
so it cannot approve that allocation unconditionally. Conversely, missing the
old 7,500-ALM or 9,000-register subsystem rule does not prove the FPGA is full.
Keep the old variance visible and obtain a count-once whole-console allocation
before deciding how much additional area reduction is actually necessary.

2. THE GOAL AND RESOURCE CONTRACT
===============================================================================

2.1 Hard requirements versus an allocation decision
---------------------------------------------------

Hard functional requirements:
  exact required arithmetic and output order; no lost or duplicated accepted
  work; no dead-owner authority; bounded storage and progress under the agreed
  responder contracts; all required features live in the accepted profile.

Timing requirement:
  100 MHz under the real clock/interface contract, with required setup, hold,
  recovery/removal and pulse-width checks passing at required corners. A raw
  Fmax benchmark is not signoff. [V02]

Historical allocation:
  texture nominal 6,600 ALMs, redline 7,500; 9,000 registers; 64 M10Ks; 14 DSPs.
  These are recorded subsystem rules. The latest fit registration keeps them.
  They are not identical to whole-device capacity or an irrevocable allocation.
  [R11, R12, R17]

The provisional fit device provides 41,910 ALMs, 553 RAM blocks and 112 DSP
blocks in the fitter's resource units. The correct whole-console question is:

  texture allowance = admitted whole-device logic budget
                    - counted-once non-texture logic
                    - explicitly retained integration/routing reserve.

Neither the historical sum of leaf fits nor the space left after subtracting
texture alone answers that question. Overlapping cores, alternative versions,
wrappers, counters and duplicate production-manifest entries must not be added
as though they were independent simultaneous instances. [R11]

For illustration ONLY: if other admitted logic were 24,000 ALMs and the reserve
were 4,000, the physical 41,910 ceiling would leave 13,910. If other admitted
logic were 30,000 with the same reserve, it would leave 7,910. These are not
estimates of this console. They show why 11,000 cannot be declared either a
disaster or safely affordable without the other terms.

2.2 Register reduction is a means, not the objective function
------------------------------------------------------------

A large indexed register store can bring expensive selection logic and routing.
Eliminating that structure is useful even when total register capacity is not
close to exhaustion. Conversely, adding a few hundred pipeline registers to
remove a long cone can be exactly right. Stage A demonstrated a good storage
trade; it did not establish that every future register reduction saves ALMs.

Track fitted registers beside ALMs, inferred memories, control sharing, fan-out,
and timing. Do not sacrifice a correct registered interface to make the old
9,000-register subsystem number green. Do not describe every small register
FIFO as architectural waste.

2.3 Admission of a revised budget
--------------------------------

Keep two judgments in the report:

  Historical-rule result: pass/fail against the unchanged old allocation.
  Product-allocation decision: accepted/not accepted, with the new whole-console
  accounting and explicit owner approval attached.

A revised 11-12k logic allocation, for example, must be a stated decision rather
than an edit that erases the previous failure. A correctly measured 11k/100-MHz
candidate need not be redesigned again merely because it is not 7.5k. Equally,
this brief does not promise that the fully enabled V3 island will remain 11k.

DSP and M10K headroom must be handled the same way. A logical multiplication
site is not one physical DSP block. A 64x64 memory is not automatically one
M10K because it contains fewer than 10,240 bits: port width matters. [V01, V04]

4. TYPED IDENTITY AND ACCEPTED-BEAT OWNERSHIP
===============================================================================

4.1 Freeze the existing wire encodings; stop rediscovering their slices
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The P0-C migration has repeatedly broken a field whose width remained legal
while its meaning changed. The cure is a small shared encoding vocabulary, not
another namespace or translation RAM. Keep these external encodings:

  public owner:  {slot[5:0], generation[7:0]}                 14 bits
  sample handle: {slot[5:0], sample_index[1:0], generation}    16 bits
  route token:   {class[1:0], sample_handle[15:0]}             18 bits
  T2 ticket:    {generation[7:0], slot[5:0]}                  14 bits

The public owner and the arithmetic ticket are NOT interchangeable integers.
Use named pack/unpack functions or packed types for owner_slot, owner_generation,
owner_to_ticket, make_sample_handle, sample_to_owner and route_class. Restrict
raw slicing to these definitions and the explicit external adapters. [R03-R06]

This must be a bit-identical migration. It must not widen, truncate or reorder
existing wires, alter array depths, or introduce a 64-entry generation table.
The independent encoding check in this bundle covers all 262,144 combinations
of slot, generation, sample code and class code, including code 3 as a losslessly
represented but invalid TMU sample. The real RTL test must exercise slot bits
4 and 5, generation 0/255, and every adapter in the actual path.

The current top mixes generic-looking parameters with fixed 14/16/18-bit
interfaces. State the supported parameter profile explicitly and reject other
combinations at elaboration until tested. Do not claim that changing GENW or
owner depth works merely because the file has a parameter declaration.

4.2 Each captured record has one event and one stage
--------------------------------------------------

Use this review table for every stored field:

  field / width / writer event / writer stage / owner key /
  reader stage(s) / final legal read / reset or reuse rule.

Examples from the actual integration:

  Input material and palette fields belong to the input admission beat.
  UVW belongs to the owner returned by RCP, not its generation bits.
  AUX coordinates belong to the expander's current fragment, not output context.
  Sample decode metadata belongs to an accepted planned sample.
  Caller tag belongs to opaque context, not to the internal owner identifier.

The repaired top had mixed these stages while preserving plausible widths. A
record-level contract prevents the next field, such as fog amount, repeating
the same error. A stage-valid bit without its matching record is insufficient.

At the ingress fork the existing corrected law is:

  external_fire = frag_valid && owner_ready && rcp_ready
  owner_fire    = external_fire
  rcp_fire      = external_fire.

Later front-end changes must preserve ONE acceptance event, even if 'rcp_ready'
becomes 'preparation reservation available'. Never allocate an owner on the
caller merely offering valid while the other branch refuses. The independent
8-case truth check catches that exact phantom-admission mutant. [R03]

4.3 Stability and adapter inventory
-----------------------------------

At each elastic boundary require the complete offered record to remain stable
while valid is high and ready is low. If a legacy service intentionally permits
selection to change before acceptance, state that exception and capture it
before exposing a stable-valid contract; do not assume a generic interface name
proves either behavior.

The inventory must include internal cache c1_src/c2_src registers, not only
ports; the AUX sheet request AND response; RCP token storage; PERSPUV tag;
expander queue and current record; class queues; material alignment identity;
COMBINE tag; FINAL; and external caller context. 'Six sites, not four' was the
actual cache-width lesson. [R03, R05, R16]

A declaration-presence checker is useful, but it cannot establish record
identity, stage alignment, or meaning. Keep the data-dependent tests.

5. FRONT-END LIFETIME, ZERO-WORK OWNERS, RESET AND NAMESPACE REUSE
===============================================================================

5.1 The unclosed zero-work obligation
-------------------------------------

The owner intentionally creates initial ready work when adm_req_i == 0. That is
correct for its leaf contract: it owes no TMU or AUX source. The composed top,
however, admits the same fragment into RCP and later PERSPUV and the expander.
Those stages still hold its owner token and can still read owner-keyed sidecars.
The leaf owner's readiness does not prove that this front-end obligation ended.
[R03, R04: admission initialization and q0i_v_q; R05, R06]

Therefore this implication is not established by the inspected composition:

  owner may externally retire -> no legitimate front-end reader for it remains.

A zero-sample expander unit test proves that no TMU requests are emitted. It does
not establish the above implication for a separately completing owner. Nor does
392-record ordinary parity explore all prolonged stalls and wrap schedules.

Evidence classification: SOURCE-PROVEN mismatch between two completion domains;
REACHABILITY/IMPACT of a destructive same-slot reuse schedule NOT independently
proved here. The supplied abstract model demonstrates why the implication must
be specified, not that the repository already produces that exact trace.

5.2 Selected contract: a real front-end completion lease
-------------------------------------------------------

Before production cutover, require the following invariant:

  actual COMBINE acceptance for owner H requires both:
    - the owner's existing sample/AUX readiness;
    - completion of every front-end operation that may still read H's sidecars.

A compact initial implementation is one FRONT_COMPLETE bit per owner slot,
cleared on accepted admission and set on a tagged expander-done event. 'Done'
means all required requests were accepted and no front-end stage still needs
that fragment's descriptor. For the zero-work case it means the fragment really
passed through and left the current expansion record, not merely that its
sample count was zero at admission.

Gate both halves of the actual COMBINE handshake with the same completion term.
Do not pop the owner COMBINE packet early into an adapter and thereby mark cbi
before the real combiner takes it. That would repeat the reservation-versus-
acceptance defect already repaired by M6.

This 64-bit phase plane is NOT another live-owner counter, allocator or
namespace. It records an obligation the owner leaf currently cannot see. The
owner window remains the allocation authority. The full handle travels with
the completion event. Assert, against the owner window in verification, that a
legitimate completion belongs to a live instance and arrives once. Admission
clear has defined precedence over any colliding stale event.

The no-reuse proof is inductive: an admitted slot starts incomplete; its owner
cannot combine/retire until the front-end event; the event is emitted only when
that stage releases the last sidecar read; therefore a later legitimate front-
end event cannot belong to an already-reused slot. This proof also requires all
local front-end valid state to be reset/cancelled under the same reset contract.
Do not 'prove' the event's identity from a second copied generation table.

A later deliberate owner-interface extension can move FRONT_COMPLETE into the
owner's narrow phase state if that produces a cleaner implementation. It must
not encode 'front-end done' as a fake required TMU or AUX sample. Preserve the
real resource and error meanings of those lanes.

Alternative: bypass unnecessary RCP/PERSPUV work for a zero-source fragment.
That may be a good throughput optimization, but it changes the accepted work
and counter contract. It is NOT the initial compatibility fix. It needs its own
reference, counter and ordering decision, including any live Mosaic/Fog work.

5.3 The tests that decide whether the lease is sufficient
--------------------------------------------------------

Use zero-source, no-AUX owners interleaved with one-, two- and three-source
owners; zero-source WITH AUX must remain a distinct case. Give every owner
distinct material, context and descriptor values. Keep the output ready while
stalling front-end acceptance, then reverse the stalls. Exercise over 64
admissions and a full handle-namespace cycle in a bounded stress test.

Instrument:
  input admission; RCP acceptance and completion; PERSPUV completion;
  expander acceptance and done; actual COMBINE acceptance; owner retirement;
  descriptor reads and writes by full logical owner.

Require no final acceptance before FRONT_COMPLETE, no stale sidecar read after
retirement, no duplicate completion, and no leak after all responders resume.
Deliberately remove the lease qualifier: the new test must detect the violated
ordering, even if the final colours still happen to match.

If an alternative structural proof establishes that the existing implementation
already prevents retirement-before-front-completion for every legal schedule,
record that proof and keep the detector; do not add redundant phase state just
to follow this document. The current source and test evidence does not yet
supply that proof.

5.4 Owner quiet is not island quiescence
----------------------------------------

The owner's quiet_c covers its own pipeline, queues, live count and reserved
output space. It does not include RCP, PERSPUV, the expander, cache/fill
transport, class queues, palette lookup returns or external sheet responders.
Its own fence comments explicitly leave producer quiesce and residual transport
acknowledgment unimplemented. This is not fixed by connecting another local
'idle' signal to the existing reduction. [R04: quiet_c and FN_* states]

The product barrier must be a coordinated phase machine:

  STOP_NEW_ADMISSIONS
    -> FINISH_ACCEPTED_WORK (allow its requests to issue and complete)
    -> QUIESCE_PRODUCERS / CLOSE_OLD_EPOCH
    -> DRAIN_RESIDUAL_TRANSPORT
    -> VERIFY_ALL_ACKNOWLEDGMENTS_AND_LOCAL_EMPTY
    -> REOPEN once, with the new namespace permission registered.

Stopping new fragments must not prevent already accepted fragments issuing the
requests needed to drain. An ACK means the producer can no longer deliver an
old-epoch response, not that its visible output happened to be idle that cycle.
Track request ownership where the real transport adapter knows it; do not
subtract an arbitrary malformed response from an outstanding-work count.

The current transitional owner fence permits individual wrapping admissions.
When replacing it, use the existing allocation ticket to detect the namespace
boundary and define one acknowledged epoch barrier, not an accidental drain
for every slot of the wrapping generation. This is a controlled extension of
the current fence, not a second independent epoch allocator in the top.
Measure wrap overhead under sustained traffic once the safety contract exists.

Reset needs the same treatment. Resetting local generations to their initial
value does not make an old external packet with that same value distinguishable
from a new owner. Either the reset protocol guarantees transport cancellation/
drain before re-admission, or a persistent/versioned epoch must cross the real
external interface. Widening a local counter that the responder never echoes
cannot solve that ambiguity.

Required falsifier: withhold exactly one external adapter's ACK while all local
queues are empty. Admission must remain closed. Then deliver late old traffic,
complete the agreed cancellation/drain, acknowledge, and prove fresh traffic
works. No timeout may silently authorize reuse; a timeout can raise an explicit
fatal/recovery condition instead.

6. THE NEXT TIMING ARCHITECTURE: RESERVED RCP PREPARATION
===============================================================================

6.1 Why this is the next candidate, not a new reciprocal algorithm
----------------------------------------------------------------

Stage A's reported gate is raw depth -> RCP c_x. The actual source takes d_i
through a 24-position leading-zero search, variable shift, ROM-index formation,
seed lookup and selected-context write. The four-job Newton engine is separate
and its issue-selection boundary has already been registered. [R06, R12]

First check the fresh V3 setup report and the real launch/capture context. A
virtual-input path can include significant boundary/skew effects, so inspect
data delay as well as slack and the upstream register contract. Do not dismiss
it because it is a port path, or redesign it solely because it is a port path.

If that preparation family remains significant, implement the following split.
Keep the original RCP service as the arithmetic oracle; keep the current ROM,
normalization convention, rounding, overflow and truncation exactly.

6.2 Proposed stage schedule
---------------------------

  Stage    Action                          Reservation / visibility
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  N0       Accept raw d, token, context     Context becomes occupied NOW.
           identity into registers.        No micro-job is eligible yet.

  N1       Compute leading-zero count,     Operands and identity travel as
           normalized mantissa, exponent,  one record. Still not selectable.
           and zero classification.

  N2       Read/capture the unchanged      Initialize context operands;
           seed; publish prepared state.   only now assert micro-job eligibility.

  S1       Existing registered selection.  Eligibility surrendered at selection.

  M1/M2    Existing multiply/update law.   Existing phase/identity forwarding.

  DONE     Existing or separately tested  Completion and egress ownership are
           registered completion path.     explicitly accounted.

N0 reservation is essential. Reserving only when N2 completes admits more
preparation records than the context store owns, or lets the same free context
be chosen repeatedly while older preparation is in flight. Treat occupied-but-
not-prepared as a real state. In the present organization, c_val=1 with
c_pend=0 and a non-final phase can represent it, provided every completion and
reset predicate is audited for that state.

No stage may derive its token, zero flag, phase or selected context from the
current input after the beat was accepted. The original ingress fork must
still allocate exactly one global owner and one local reservation on the same
accepted external beat.

6.3 Normalization implementation and arithmetic limits
------------------------------------------------------

A bounded group leading-zero encoder is an appropriate implementation candidate:
classify the three eight-bit groups, select the first nonzero group, then encode
within that group. The all-zero input retains the old convention and separate
zero flag. Register the normalized mantissa before the ROM/seed publication.
Whether N1 needs another cut is a physical result, not a stylistic preference.

The seed index's low eight bits are equivalent to normalized mantissa bits
[22:15] for this fixed 24-bit expression, including the existing zero convention.
The independent check exhaustively compared both forms over all 2^24 inputs.
This licenses a candidate exact expression simplification, not an arbitrary
change to ROM contents or reciprocal approximation. [R06; check N1]

Preserve, in particular:
  the two Newton updates and four dependent multiply jobs;
  uint64-style wrapping of the 2^31 - w expression and product;
  the specified round-half-up shifts;
  uint32 iterate truncation;
  final rescale/clamp and zero result/exponent behavior.

Do not replace exact integer division behavior, use floating point, or reduce
iterations to buy a clock. Do not replace the current service with the separate
RCP-V3 successor simply because that successor once had a flattering leaf Fmax.
A successor decision requires the same workload, arithmetic and composed test.

6.4 Completion side: a separate conditional change
--------------------------------------------------

The current RCP completion selector searches c_val/c_pend/phase and in-flight
identities, then dynamically selects result, token and exponent. That structure
is real, but Stage A now captures the outgoing beat before PERSPUV. The old
cross-module completion path must not be cited as though Stage A never existed.

If a fresh report or a stall-stability test still implicates completion, use an
explicit terminal-state bit and a reserved result-holding stage. Capture the
whole result and identity together, prevent selecting the same completed context
twice, and keep it stable under backpressure. A conservative first variant keeps
the context reserved until the held result is accepted. Releasing it earlier is
allowed only with a separately counted egress reservation and a proof that no
remaining read accesses the reused context.

Do not stack two almost-identical skids around an unchanged expensive selector
without deciding which one owns the queued result. Do not add a hierarchical
arbiter just because an older report recommended it for a path already removed.

6.5 Acceptance and expected physical effects
--------------------------------------------

Structural prediction: raw depth no longer feeds normalization and ROM logic
all the way into a context operand register in one combinational stage. A
normalization or ROM family may become the new local limit; name it.

Resource expectation: additional bounded preparation registers and possibly
some narrow phase state. DSP arithmetic count is not intentionally increased.
ALM direction and Fmax gain are NOT predicted.

Functional gates: exhaustive/reference normalization checks; reciprocal RTL
pairing over boundary and random denominators; eight contexts under stalls;
identity preservation; reset with every preparation stage occupied; exactly
four multiply launches per ordinary reciprocal; no premature DONE; unchanged
retired colours and ordering in the island. Measure sustained launches and
cycles per result, not only correctness on a drained burst.

If the first fit merely moves delay into one long N1 stage, split the measured
N1 cone. If input timing was mostly virtual-pin skew and the actual embedded
core already meets its interface budget, do not spend area gratuitously: choose
the measured internal family instead.

7. THE NEXT STORAGE ARCHITECTURE: ONE METADATA JOIN BEFORE CLASS FAN-OUT
===============================================================================

7.1 Why the old 'P0-E closed' conclusion does not close this work
---------------------------------------------------------------

The old-island async-read census correctly found uvw_m as its largest immediate
convertible target. The new V3 top is a DIFFERENT storage graph: sample metadata
expanded from 16 to 64 owners; a material plane was added; context acquired a
new expander consumer; old arrays still have mixed live/dead consumers. An old
MAP census is not an inventory of this composition. [R03, R05, R12, R15]

Current source contains sampmeta_m[64][3] at 21 bits per sample, or 4,032
declared bits, written at accepted planner output and read independently by
CLUT, nearest and bilinear logic. The first V3 MAP/entity report must establish
its actual mapping and cost before an area claim is made. [R03: 1330 onward]

The wrong repair is three replicated RAMs, or one RAM whose new arbiter silently
serializes all decoding lanes. The selected candidate relocates the join to the
one stream those readers all share: accepted cache responses.

7.2 Proposed dataflow
---------------------

  planner/cache request acceptance
        | writes immutable SAMPLE_METADATA[address={slot,sample}]
        v
  cache response stream -- one accepted response per cycle maximum
        v
  R0: reserve joined-response capacity; capture data64 + full route token
        | initiate synchronous metadata read
        v
  R1: capture returned metadata + corresponding token/data/valid
        | check alignment/range/metadata generation; no owner payload write
        v
  joined-response elastic storage
        v
  existing raw dispatcher -> existing per-class queues
        | complete immutable response record travels with each queue entry
        v
  CLUT / nearest / bilinear / error consumers
        v
  existing terminal completion merger -> V3 owner R0.

This is an architecture of the metadata access, not a replacement of the
cache, dispatcher, palette or bilerp arithmetic.

The dispatcher already has a DATAW parameter. One candidate is DATAW=104,
carrying the existing data64 plus metadata40, while TOKW remains the correctly
encoded 18-bit routing identity. Update every queue/internal signal governed by
DATAW and verify the payload, not merely the port declaration. Do not put these
metadata bits into the identity token or opaque caller context. [R08]

7.3 Candidate 40-bit metadata row
---------------------------------

For the current four-palette-slot profile, a useful row budget is:

  owner generation       8
  palette slot           2
  palette generation     8
  resolved format        3
  fraction U             8
  fraction V             8
  byte selection         1
  CLUT4 nibble selection  1
  reserved               1
                         --
                         40 bits

Address {owner_slot[5:0], sample_index[1:0]} is eight bits: 256x40. Sample index
3 is invalid and must be rejected before any returned row is used. The reserved
bit is not an implicit resettable valid array. Generation here is an immutable
alignment check for the payload, not a second generation allocator or authority
table. V3's live window still determines write authority.

If palette-slot count or format payload changes, derive the row width explicitly
and refit it. Do not silently truncate to preserve the attractive 40-bit shape.

Cyclone V M10K supports the 256x40 simple-dual-port organization; wider or true-
dual-port arrangements have different limits. This is why that access shape is
worth targeting. It is not a guarantee of one physical block after extra ports,
byte enables or control logic are added. [V01]

7.4 Writer and publication contract
------------------------------------

Write exactly the metadata belonging to the accepted planned sample, at the
same transaction event that gives that sample to the cache. Capture palette
binding from the same accepted descriptor, not from a current ingress or another
pipeline stage. Format/fractions/nibble come from that sample's planner result.

Where the bank contract requires a registered write enable, stage the entire
{address,data,write_enable} record together. Establish a lower bound between
request acceptance and earliest response metadata use. If that lower bound is
not guaranteed, delay request release until metadata publication or implement a
bounded, explicitly compared forwarding path. Do not depend accidentally on
read-during-write old/new-data behavior. [R09, V03, V06]

Reset only validity/ownership/reservations; the persistent payload bank does not
need to be cleared. No combinational decode or arithmetic belongs between its
read port and the receiving data register. Keep record validation after capture.

7.5 Credits: memory latency is already using space
-------------------------------------------------

Let Q be joined-response destination capacity, including its defined holding
registers. Maintain:

  reserved = returned-but-unconsumed records + reads in flight
  0 <= reserved <= Q.

Reserve before initiating the read. Release only at the defined downstream
transfer. The downstream may withdraw ready immediately after a read launches;
that event must never overwrite an existing record. Full-but-popping bypass is
optional; the conservative no-bypass form is easier to prove and may be enough.

The attached abstract three-cycle join model survived 32 randomized 10,000-cycle
schedules, including a long initial consumer stall. The mutant that ignored
in-flight reservations overflowed. That validates the reservation equation,
not the proposed RTL's implementation. The RTL test must reproduce the same
adversarial schedules and check exact-once full-record transport.

7.6 Cost and deletion ledger
----------------------------

Potentially deleted after consumers migrate:
  sampmeta_m's separate asynchronous class-side selections;
  per-owner palette slot/generation lookups on the response paths;
  class-specific copies of decode metadata and duplicated binding reconstruction.

Definitely retained unless separately justified:
  the cache data path;
  per-class independence and error terminal path;
  the palette response's cannot-wait contract or an explicitly credited replacement;
  V3 return snapshot/claim/write/publication stages;
  the owner identity carried end to end.

Declared costs, not fit predictions:
  256x40 bank: 10,240 declared memory bits, versus 4,032 bits in current sample
  metadata alone, with additional fields replacing other lookups;
  metadata carried through RAWN=4 plus four CHN=4 queues: up to 20x40=800
  extra declared queue payload bits at the present dispatcher depths;
  bounded join stages and credits; possibly wider shallow-queue implementations.

This can increase total stored bits while reducing expensive access logic. It
can also fail to save area. Accept only after named inference, all functional
checks, and a composed comparison. If the first V3 MAP already shows inexpensive
properly banked metadata and this family is neither large nor slow, defer the
conversion. A better-looking block diagram is not sufficient justification.

7.7 Staged implementation without a false comparison
-----------------------------------------------------

First establish the joined record in simulation using the existing metadata
storage, carrying exactly the same values. Then replace the common read with
the synchronous bank and reservation pipeline. Finally delete the superseded
class-side reads and stores. Keep commits separable for review; the physical
candidate must be the completed replacement, not the temporary design carrying
both implementations.

Test all formats with distinct neighboring texels, nontrivial fractions and
alternating palette identities. Include warm hits, misses, full queues, delayed
fills, concurrent class traffic, repeated slots with different generations,
errors and reset/drain. Retain strict palette priority until a credited holding
stage makes another arbitration policy safe.

8. MATERIAL AND FRONT-END SIDECARS: RETAIN ONLY JUSTIFIED COPIES
===============================================================================

8.1 The material plane is 46 bits, not the original 45
------------------------------------------------------

The actual record is {base_rgb24, base_a8, weight8, recipe3, sample_count2,
has_aux1}: 46x64 = 2,944 declared bits. The extra bit controls whether COMBINE's
third lane is AUX or sample 2. It cannot be inferred from an arbitrary result
value. The synchronous read is aligned with a registered full owner handle,
and the same mat_aligned_c gates COMBINE valid and the owner's ready. Preserve
that repaired symmetry. [R03: approximately 2240-2395]

Do not promise one M10K for this 46-bit-wide parallel read merely because 2,944
is small. A simple-dual-port M10K's relevant width is at most 40; a direct
parallel implementation needs width banking unless another implementation is
chosen. Forcing a narrower multi-beat read changes bandwidth and scheduling.
The actual fit must decide the physical cost. [V01]

8.2 The head-only material read has a throughput cost
----------------------------------------------------

When owner A is accepted by COMBINE and the owner queue offers B, mat_rd_q still
belongs to A until the next read completes. The current identity comparison
correctly inserts a bubble. That is better than combining B with A's material.
It also means this interface cannot be advertised as unconditional one-packet-
per-cycle COMBINE admission. [R03]

Do not optimize this before showing it limits the workload. RCP currently
requires four multiplier jobs per ordinary fragment, so a two-cycle material
admission interface may have adequate sustained capacity while still limiting
bursts.

If measurement makes it significant, join material alongside the owner's
reserved gather stage, so the packet and its 46-bit material reach the COMBINE
holding queue together. This is a controlled packet/interface extension. It
must keep 'captured for COMBINE' separate from 'accepted by COMBINE'. An outside
FIFO that acknowledges the owner's cmb_ready merely because it accepted a
packet would mark actual-COMBINE state too early under the present contract.

8.3 fctx_m cannot currently be deleted by decree
------------------------------------------------

The new owner stores opaque context for retirement. The top ALSO still stores
fctx_m, and the expander now consumes that early context for the legacy AUX
coordinate convention. Those are two different read times. The latest AUX
repair is why deleting the top copy without replacing its reader is wrong.
[R03, R05, R16]

Two coherent alternatives exist:

  Compatibility profile: retain an explicitly named front-end AUX payload
  sidecar, prove its lifetime through expansion, and price its storage. Do not
  claim that fctx_m has been eliminated in this profile.

  Production profile: expose/capture real AUX geometry as typed fields and stop
  deriving world coordinates from the caller's opaque context. Carry those
  fields through the front-end record; let the owner context bank serve only
  the caller's retirement payload. The new field list and geometry width need
  their own ABI and reference tests.

Do not repack recipe, fog, binding or position into caller-owned context bits.
Do not add a second read port to OWNER_CONTEXT casually: it changes the physical
memory organization and may duplicate RAM or reintroduce a large mux.

8.4 Actual reader census before another storage sweep
-----------------------------------------------------

The top still declares/writes fbase_m, fbind_m, frec_m, fwt_m, fsc_m and other
sidecars in addition to mat_m. Some consumers are dead legacy nets; some feed
Mosaic or expansion. A declaration is not a fitted store, and an intended
'merge' is not a deletion until the old live consumers are gone. [R03]

Produce a small V3-only reader census from the first MAP/entity report:
  each field's live consumer; read address count; capture stage; inferred RAM or
  registers; and whether a proposed replacement changes the port count.

Then make narrow deletions. An independent front descriptor and a late material
record can be legitimate even when some bits overlap, because they serve
separate timing/port requirements. Optimize that trade with evidence rather
than requiring one physical copy of every repeated bit at any cost.

9. CONDITIONAL OWNER-CONTROL LOCALIZATION
===============================================================================

9.1 Target the measured structure, not 'the scoreboard' in general
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The post-T2 owner report names req_q -> iss_q as its internal limiting family.
The source computes a selected-row required/issued predicate, then broadcasts
the accepted event back into a loop over 64 owner rows. That can create the
undesirable route:

  one row's request bits -> global selected-row predicate -> another row's
  update gating / wide decoder distribution.

This is a reasonable candidate after the first V3 fit identifies its composed
importance. It is not permission to replace the entire proven owner. [R04, R13]

9.2 First experiment: exact Boolean localization, no extra event latency
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Keep the common full-identity/range validation. Decode the event's destination
once, hierarchically if the physical report justifies it. At each destination
row, test that row's required/issued bits locally and form its update locally.
The global rejection/count result may reduce local event outcomes, but it must
not become a control path back through all row updates.

Conceptually, for row i and source bit b:

  local_issue_i = event_valid && identity_live && source_in_range
               && destination_is_i && req_i[b] && !issued_i[b]

  issued_next_i = issued_i OR accepted_TMU_mask_i OR accepted_AUX_mask_i.

Admission/reset/release precedence remains exactly the existing one. Do not
replace that OR merge with two competing nonblocking writes. Do not delay an
ISSUE event by a new stage without reopening the snapshot/claim forwarding
proof; the first experiment does NOT add such a delay.

The independent 4,096-case scalar truth check covers source index, required and
issued masks, liveness and a simultaneous AUX event. It establishes the local
Boolean equivalence for that expression. It does not prove whole-owner event
ordering or Quartus placement. The full 541-check RTL suite, mutations and new
composed tests remain mandatory.

9.3 What is specifically forbidden in this experiment
-----------------------------------------------------

Do not restore per-slot generation RAM/FF state after T2 removed it. Do not put
all narrow scoreboard planes into one RAM requiring impossible simultaneous
reads/writes. Do not serialize independent TMU/AUX/final writes to obtain a
smaller block without changing and proving the throughput contract. Do not
remove claimed, committed or combine-reserved distinctions because two bits
look similar in one successful trace.

A later bank-local control design is possible, but it needs an explicit event
port schedule and reservations for collisions. It is not the first change.

9.4 Acceptance
--------------

Physical hypothesis: reduced global selection/update fan-out and elimination
or shortening of the measured req-to-iss family. Resource direction is unknown
until fitted: duplicated local decode can cost more than a shared mux. Use the
same source/parameter/constraint profile and first compare matched seeds.

Keep the variant only if it improves the relevant composed constraint without
breaking functional throughput or increasing another pressured resource beyond
the admitted budget. A better owner leaf number alone is insufficient.

10. THROUGHPUT AND SMALL QUEUES: WHAT NOT TO OPTIMIZE BLINDLY
===============================================================================

10.1 One fragment per clock is not the current end-to-end contract
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The RCP source performs four dependent multiplier jobs per ordinary reciprocal
with at most one launch per cycle. The arithmetic throughput ceiling is therefore
one ordinary reciprocal per four clocks before other stalls. At 100 MHz and
60 frames/s that is about 416,667 reciprocals per frame as an idealized ceiling,
NOT a guaranteed workload capacity. Raising owner depth cannot exceed that
arithmetic limit. [R06; calculation]

The owner can retire consecutive ready finals at one per clock; that is a valid
LOCAL burst capability. It must not be repeated as a sustained full-island
fragment rate. Similarly, a queue of four complete fragments does not turn a
single sample expander into a one-fragment-per-clock machine for three-sample
materials.

Write the workload contract in useful units:
  accepted fragments/frame; distribution of 0/1/2/3 samples; AUX fraction;
  nearest/CLUT/bilinear mix; hit/miss pattern; response latency envelope;
  allowed output stalls; and longest permitted end-to-end completion time.

Measure useful outputs per unit time, not just MHz. A smaller 100-MHz block
that now spends twice as many cycles per fragment can be a regression.

10.2 The expander has an intentional but measurable refill bubble
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The inspected expander loads a queued fragment only when cur_v_q is false; its
sequential branch clears cur_v_q on done and does not simultaneously load the
next record. Under ready sinks and queued work, an n-sample fragment therefore
has a refill bubble in addition to its n request cycles. A zero-request fragment
still consumes control cycles. [R05]

Do not automatically fix this. With a four-job reciprocal upstream, the bubble
may be hidden. Measure request utilization and head-of-line stalls first.

If it is a real bottleneck, allow a completed current fragment to be replaced
by the next queued record on the same edge. Preserve the original fragment's
final request/ISSUE event and the new fragment's initial index independently.
Include simultaneous final TMU, final AUX, queue push, queue pop, and current-
record replacement. The current opaque AUX payload must remain paired with its
owner. No field may be taken from a live input during a stalled current record.

10.3 Shallow owner packet queues are a trade, not a free prize
-------------------------------------------------------------

The owner CQ declares four records of owner14 plus four result40 lanes: 696
raw bits. OQ declares four records of owner14 + result40 + context64: 472 raw
bits. Total declared payload is 1,168 bits. The historical physical attribution
of seven M10Ks holding 1,056 bits is a DIFFERENT, optimized fitted accounting;
do not pretend those two counts are identical. [R04, R24]

Concatenating fields into a wide RAM does not automatically reduce blocks:
width banking and read-port organization still apply. Replacing RAM with a
small register FIFO can save M10Ks and improve a selected output path while
increasing ALMs. Since ALMs/timing are the current concerns, do not spend an
implementation cycle optimizing RAM utilization percentages in isolation.

Only experiment here when a fresh resource/timing attribution justifies it:
  packed synchronous body plus reserved registered head/spare;
  or a truly shallow register implementation of the same logical capacity.

Preserve the distinction between physical body storage, head/spare, reads in
flight and total logical reserved capacity. Keep the same payload/credit
contract. The 135.37-MHz ready-queue probe does not characterize these wider
packet queues, and does not remove their need for a composed test. [R14]

10.4 Material prefetch and queue depth are decisions from a measured bottleneck
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Do not expand NCTX, NTOK, owner count or every FIFO depth together. Record why
one capacity is limiting, sweep that parameter independently, and inspect its
resource/timing consequences. The global 64-owner window, local RCP contexts,
PERSPUV tokens, sample transport credits and output reservations are separate
resources even when their counters happen to coincide in one test.

11. FEATURE-LIVE BINDING, MOSAIC, AUX AND FOG
===============================================================================

11.1 Define two profiles and stop mixing their claims
----------------------------------------------------

COMPATIBILITY: reproduces the tested legacy island semantics, including fully
drained global-mode phases. This is the current paired-run baseline and the
right first price for the ownership restructuring.

FEATURE-LIVE: per-fragment/sample binding actually controls addresses and mode;
Mosaic selection affects the real selected material/address; required AUX uses
its declared geometry; fog factor reaches the real final-colour path in the
correct order. Every enabled feature changes an observable result and survives
synthesis. It has its own closure, budgets and evidence.

Do not call the first profile the finished production renderer. Do not make the
second profile reproduce a known missing feature solely to preserve the first
profile's CRC. [R03, R10]

11.2 Binding: a field that travels but controls nothing is not implemented
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

The expander stores a binding field, but its exposed sample-request record does
not carry a binding output to a resolver. The actual planner still consumes the
global bind_base_i and bind_mode_i pins. Those are read at planner acceptance,
many clocks after fragment admission. The directed fixture explicitly uses
fully drained mode phases because of this limitation. [R03, R05, R10]

Selected production contract:
  capture a binding key/version or a fully resolved descriptor with the accepted
  fragment/sample; resolve it at one named stage; retain the descriptor or an
  immutable version through the sample's last use. Base, format/filter, extent,
  LOD and palette identity must describe the same sample.

A minimal first extension can capture the current global base/mode with the
fragment at admission, proving late-ingress safety before adding a general
binding-table resolver. That still does NOT implement distinct per-sample
bindings by itself. Name that limit rather than pretending the stored binding
index already selects a texture.

Once the planner resolves format and effective filter, derive the routing class
from that result. The caller's original class may remain as an assertion/error
input during migration; it must not remain a second independent routing
authority indefinitely. Invalid descriptors with preserved valid owner identity
must produce the specified counted terminal error, not silently disappear.

Required detector: alternate base addresses, direct/palette formats and palette
versions on adjacent admitted fragments while downstream stages are stalled.
Changing the global pins after a beat is admitted must not alter that beat's
future sample. Use distinct textures and verify actual fetched addresses.

11.3 Mosaic must be on the address/material path
------------------------------------------------

The current Mosaic instance receives work and its counter moves, but the top
consumes the selection as evidence rather than planner address authority. This
is explicitly admitted in the RTL. [R03: Mosaic section]

For feature-live acceptance, place selection ahead of binding resolution and
capture its chosen material/tile as part of the request descriptor. Keep valid,
ready, source identity and chosen descriptor together. Perturb Mosaic choice in
a test: a known output/address must change. Perturb a counter-only connection:
the detector must fail even though 'Mosaic samples' still increments.

Do not manufacture material identifiers from convenient colour bits in a new
production contract. Use the declared input material identities and frozen
selection law. Keep this functionality change separate from metadata storage
replacement so its extra cost is visible.

11.4 Fog: pipeline the existing law; do not redesign the visual effect
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Both kernels exist and were fitted:
  raster Fog: 247 ALM / 166 registers / 3 DSP / 93.63 MHz reported;
  geometry fog factor: 341 ALM / 246 registers / 3 DSP / 68.63 MHz reported.
These are leaf measurements, not 100-MHz signoff or proof of composed placement.
They are not included merely because they are listed in the production manifest.
[R11, R19, R20]

The raster kernel currently computes factor conversion, channel difference,
multiply, rounded shift, add and clamp ahead of one elastic output register.
The geometry kernel computes far-distance, a wide product, rescale and clamp
ahead of its output register. Both have straightforward pipeline candidates.

Raster candidate:
  F0 capture/interpolate-factor result, enabled/exempt decision and config version;
  F1 compute clamped unit8 amount and signed channel differences;
  F2 register channel products;
  F3 round, shift, add original channels, clamp and present output.

Geometry candidate:
  G0 capture guarded distance, behind/disabled state, near/far/k and tag;
  G1 form the signed distance difference;
  G2 register the existing wide product;
  G3 apply the existing rounded rescale and clamp.

The exact stage count is adjustable against timing. Any further multiplier
pipelining must be supported by the actual target/Quartus inference, with its
latency and stalls verified. Keep the arithmetic widths and signedness until
an exact proof licenses a change. Never count one source multiplication as one
physical DSP block. [V04]

Every elastic stage carries alpha, refusal/status, owner or output identity,
and required original channels alongside its data. A downstream stall must not
shift sidebands relative to colour. Configuration is sampled/versioned with the
accepted work; taking the current fog colour several stages later is another
late-ingress bug.

Retain the order: material/lighting and the applicable toon/ramp operation,
then fog on the final source colour, then blend. Integrate at the real render
boundary that owns this order. A texture-only material subgraph is not evidence
that the full toon -> fog -> blend chain exists. [R19, R20]

Important arithmetic trap: the existing mix divides the weighted channel delta
by 256 with signed round-half-up semantics. Do not 'correct' it to /255 or use
C++ truncation toward zero for negative values. Even full fog amount 255 does
not license replacing the expression with literal fog colour. The independent
exhaustive channel check detects both proposed wrong substitutions.

Optional area refinement after correct integration: once the interpolated
Q16.16 factor has reached a fragment, its exact conversion to the 8-bit fog
amount is independent of RGB. It may be computed once at a captured stage and
stored as 8 bits instead of carrying a 32-bit factor through every owner queue.
That requires the same enabled/exempt/config snapshot and exact clamping law;
it must not quantize vertex factors before interpolation. Introduce an explicit
amount8 mix interface rather than silently changing the meaning of fogf_i.

Count raster Fog where it is instantiated. Count geometry fog-factor generation
in the geometry/vertex closure. Do not blindly add both leaf counts to the
texture island, or omit either from whole-console accounting. A shared source
file is not proof that there is only one physical instance.

11.5 Palette configuration timing
---------------------------------

The palette-load family appeared as a reported limiter in earlier composed
fits and is not permanently solved because one seed moved it below another
family. If the new composed report makes it relevant again, capture a complete
palette command beat before its decode/high-fan-out state updates. Preserve
command order, accepted rate and the point at which a committed palette becomes
visible to lookups. [R12]

Test adjacent BEGIN/WRITE/COMMIT operations, slot-generation changes, rejected
loads and a lookup around the publication edge. Do not register only the
payload while leaving its valid/opcode/generation from a different cycle. Do
not invent a multicycle exception for configuration merely because uploads are
usually infrequent; the protocol must actually guarantee the extra cycles.

===============================================================================
12. MEASUREMENT RULES CORRECTED BY THE EVIDENCE
===============================================================================

12.1 Same worst-path family is neither necessary nor sufficient for causality
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

The repository's M6 correction was valuable in exposing careless attribution,
but the later absolute rule is too strong. A successful register cut is EXPECTED
to remove the critical path and make another family become worst. That change
of identity does not make an improvement unknowable. Conversely, two paths
both called 'palette' but with different endpoints do not establish a causal
mechanism merely by sharing a broad label. [R12]

Replace the rule with four separate statements:

  STRUCTURE: what the RTL/netlist transformation definitely removes or adds.
  OBSERVATION: the measured area, slack and throughput for this exact specimen.
  REPEATABILITY: what matched runs/seeds establish, with sample count stated.
  MECHANISM: measured path/cell evidence versus a plausible interpretation.

The two post-P0-B seeds at 78.80 and 77.67 MHz are encouraging evidence that the
new specimen was not fast only in one run. Their 1.13-MHz difference is NOT an
upper bound on seed variation, and does not by itself establish the old design's
seed distribution. The earlier claims 'four times more repeatable' and 'exactly
+10.90 MHz attributable' went further than this small sample supports. The
interpretation that the fitter redirected its effort is a hypothesis, not an
observed scheduler trace. This brief does not withdraw the measured numbers.

Where magnitude matters, compare old/new on the same chosen seeds and identical
constraints/parameters, inspect the old and new path families, and report both
aggregate timing and per-family movement. A small paired set is better than
unpaired favorable anecdotes; more seeds improve confidence but cannot replace
source/constraint fidelity. Do not spend the entire implementation budget
seeding a plainly broken long combinational cone. [V05]

12.2 Fmax is a benchmark, not the signoff artifact
-------------------------------------------------

The vendor explicitly distinguishes Fmax from full timing signoff. Capture
setup and hold summaries at required corners, recovery/removal, pulse width,
clock assumptions, unconstrained paths and justified exceptions. Same-edge
hold problems are not generally fixed by lowering the clock frequency. [V02]

For each significant timing family retain:
  launch/capture names; endpoint class (core/core, input/core, core/output,
  input/output); clock relationship; data delay; skew; setup/hold corner;
  fan-out and relevant cells; number/severity of related failing endpoints.

Do not sum MHz gains from different stages. Do not use only the single worst
path when the next ten independent families are nearly as bad. Do not infer a
new V3 core clock from the legacy top's reported number.

12.3 Resource comparisons are physical experiments too
-------------------------------------------------------

ALMs depend on optimization, packing and placement as well as declared bits.
The old +159 leaf / +14 composed result shows scope dependence, not an exact
universal price for that register cut. A logical array's absence from a report
can mean RAM inference, elimination, renaming, flattening or an incorrect source
closure. Require a positive explanation.

For each proposed deletion retain before/after instance attribution and the
actual RAM inventory. Distinguish ALUTs from ALMs, MAP registers from FIT
registers, memory bits from block count, and DSP blocks from multiplier sites.
These distinctions prevent an impressive but fictitious saving.

12.4 Bounded artifact/provenance contract
----------------------------------------

A fit must read immutable source bytes: a pinned worktree or copied source
closure with hashes, not a live editing directory. Record exact top, parameters,
source hashes, device, Quartus version, QSF/SDC, seed and effort settings. This
allows continued work elsewhere without contaminating the fit. The closing
report explicitly confirms that run_block_fit.ps1 already copies this fit's
18 sources into its workspace. Preserve and verify that mechanism; do not
assign a duplicate snapshot-tool implementation. [R21, R25]

Retain raw MAP/FIT/STA evidence in an immutable artifact, compressed or outside
Git if size is a problem. Keep its content hash and retrieval reference beside
the summarized row. Discarding raw MAP reports as irrelevant node dumps removes
some of the evidence needed for the next RAM/port audit. A summary is a useful
index, not a substitute for all underlying proof.

Do not create a broad new tooling program. Apply this to the current V3 and its
next two experiments. The new paired comparator already deletes old dump files,
checks executable return codes, rejects near-empty streams, compares order and
fire-tests wrong colour/order/length. Preserve it. Add frozen binary/source and
stimulus provenance as the test evolves, not another competing harness. [R23]

Every row should separately expose:
  build success; functional-profile result; resource-rule result; setup result;
  hold/other-timing result; provenance completeness; production acceptance.

A labelled variant must not silently escape its base target's rules. A row
reading 'ok' because its only rule was max_dsp says nothing about 100 MHz or
whole-console admission. Keep the old rules visible alongside any approved
revised resource allocation.

14. ACCEPTANCE MATRIX AND STOP CONDITIONS
===============================================================================

The following matrix is the release criterion, not the number of commits or the
presence of a report saying 'closed'.

  Obligation                    Evidence required
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  One admission                 Same accepted beat reserves owner and front end;
                                deliberately ungated branch is detected.
  Identity preservation         All adapters preserve full slot/gen/sample;
                                upper slot bits and wrap are exercised.
  Front-end lifetime            Zero-work and stalled schedules prove last read
                                precedes COMBINE/retirement, or equivalent proof.
  Return authority              Snapshot/current checks, illegal sample, stale,
                                duplicate and unsolicited cases; real writes seen.
  Publication order             Commit cannot precede payload write; observer
                                reads actual state, not a loosely related counter.
  COMBINE authority             FINAL requires real combiner acceptance, not a
                                read reservation or intermediate FIFO capture.
  Sample metadata               Write/read identities match; held responses keep
                                complete metadata; read credits include in-flight.
  Output order                  Paired ordered streams and full-capacity stalls;
                                no silent skipped owner or duplicated retirement.
  Fault observability           Positive and negative test of each retained
                                evidence port, including transitive fan-in.
  Namespace/reset               One withheld adapter ACK prevents reopening;
                                old transport cannot acquire fresh authority.
  Feature liveness              Binding/Mosaic/fog changes affect real outputs;
                                bypassing each mechanism is detected.
  Resource acceptability        Exact profile, named instances, old-rule variance,
                                admitted whole-console allocation and reserve.
  Timing signoff                Required setup/hold/other checks at all corners;
                                honest endpoint classes and interface constraints.
  Workload viability            Useful throughput and drain latency under the
                                declared sample mix, memory and stall envelope.

Stop and publish a finding when a supposed representation change alters output
values, accepted-work counts or error semantics outside its declared scope;
when a RAM replacement adds unexpected readers/ports; when a 'safety' counter
becomes zero because its driver vanished; or when an extra ready condition has
only been added to one half of a handshake.

Do NOT stop merely because a diagnostic fit misses a historical subsystem gate.
Do stop production acceptance when the admitted product requirements remain
unproved. These are different decisions.
