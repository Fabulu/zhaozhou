0. EXECUTIVE DECISION AND AUTHORITY TO PROCEED
===============================================================================

Do not schedule a clean-sheet V4 rewrite. Do not run another pass whose main
activity is searching for unused declarations. Build a replacement completion
and arithmetic path inside V3, preserve the current machine as the behavioural
reference, and replace the implementations only after each complete boundary
has passed its tests.

The principal change is not "put every array into RAM." It is:

  Keep wide, persistent data resident in memory. Move small identities through
  the machine. Make the control that acts on those identities local and bounded.
  Read a result when an arithmetic phase needs it, rather than repeatedly
  copying it into another scheduler's private storage.

Four workstreams form one texture campaign:

  A. Owner/completion/COMBINE topology: exact membership factoring, compact
     monotone source state, and a combiner that reads resident owner planes.
  B. Arithmetic transport: adopt the existing paired PERSPUV candidate, qualify
     the actual reciprocal profile, and remove the live-ingress scheduling path.
  C. Timing locality: split cache miss management from the hit path and shorten
     the nearly co-critical palette loader and lookup controls.
  D. Selected memory substitutions: exact small-product ROMs and, only if the
     resource portfolio permits it, a response-payload pool.

A and B are the primary ALM venture. C addresses independent timing families.
D helps the scarce DSP budget but is not a substitute for A. The measured
bilerp entity is far too small to explain the island's area overrun. [R02]

0.1 What this proposal authorizes

The implementing agent may build and test replacement texture submodules,
change internal pipeline boundaries, retime coupled control/data expressions,
and change candidate-only internal interfaces. Every such change must preserve
external transaction semantics, prescribed arithmetic, required throughput and
frame deadlines. A bounded extra internal cycle is not automatically a frame
rate reduction. It is an implementation choice whose reservations, forwarding,
ordering and throughput must be designed and demonstrated.

This is not authority to change a publicly fixed latency without documenting
that contract, drop samples, shrink owner capacity, narrow numerical domains,
relax 60 fps, reduce the camera/world feature set, or raise resource ceilings.
Where a real throughput/resource contradiction exists, state it explicitly and
price alternatives. Do not silently choose the cheaper side.

In particular, do not stop after another report saying that the owner changes
"move a protocol." Designing those internal protocols is the task. Preserve
the contract at the island boundary; specify and verify the new internal one.

0.2 What stays outside this venture

Do not begin the projector, skinning, pose, culling or terrain-bake rescues while
texture is still open. Do not rerun all newly registered leaf targets. The
accounting repair already adopted from the memory-first brief is useful and
must be retained, but target registration is not a reason to launch a broad
measurement campaign against the texture work. [R20, R21]

Do not erase the current owner, old island, material oracle or their tests.
Keep the reference available in verification; exclude replacement predecessors
from the selected shipping hierarchy so history does not cost silicon.

0.3 Execution priority and the current pause

The run log at the primary reviewed pin says no Quartus job is alive and the
agent is waiting on a redline decision while repairing instruction-delivery
tooling. The final branch observation adds register analysis, not a new fit.
That is a recorded state, not live access to the machine. Check it once at
handoff, then start the authorized texture experiments. [N01]

Do not raise 7,500 ALM merely to unlock work. Do not require proof that a complete
new architecture will succeed before allowing a cheap component prototype.
The purpose of the prototype and gates is to obtain that evidence. T0 is a
short bounded reconciliation, not a repeat of the whole A0 census.

If candidate work already exists when this revision arrives, inspect and adopt
it where it satisfies these contracts. Do not restart from an earlier commit
because this document has a newer title. A failed candidate is retained as
reference evidence, not merged into the shipping path to justify its effort.

0.4 Expected outcome versus promised outcome

This document identifies specific hardware to replace, a mathematically exact
control simplification, an already measured paired-pipeline storage reduction,
and independent cache/palette timing interventions. It supplies a 7,500-ALM
allocation to earn. It does NOT establish that those allocations are achievable
simultaneously in the fitter. If a candidate misses, its named boundary and
receipt determine the next action. There is no numerical victory by decree.

2. CORRECT THE NEW DIAGNOSIS BEFORE ACTING ON IT
===============================================================================

Do not reopen a correction that the agent has already made. The historical
triage said clm_q, cbi_q and fcl_q were simulation-only. The latest report now
explicitly withdraws that claim and correctly names their functional writers
and readers. Retain that correction. They are necessary claims/actual-issue
facts, not free deletion opportunities. [R05, N02, N03]

The remaining serious overstatement is different:

  "The loop assigns all 64 rows, so this state needs 64 RAM write ports and
  cannot use memory."

That does not follow. A row assigned its old value is a HOLD, not a requirement
for an independent memory write. The functional recurrence is sparse: actual
events change named rows and admission initializes one row. The current coding
idiom, asynchronous consumers, reset shape and concurrent changes may prevent
RAM inference AS WRITTEN. None of that proves that an equivalent implementation
needs 64 independently changing writes. [N02, N03]

This correction does NOT make an all-RAM owner the recommended design. It
reopens an implementation question that was incorrectly declared impossible.
The default remains compact local fabric control, with wide resident payload.
Only selected fields with a complete, paid-for port schedule may move to RAM.

2.1 Actual field-update inventory, not assignment-line counting

The functional post-reset writer supports these addressed events. This table
counts an upper bound on DISTINCT row addresses receiving a write per field;
coincident events at one row must merge, and some combinations may be
unreachable. It does not count read ports or predict inferred hardware.

  Field   Logical updates in one clock                     Upper bound
  live    external release; admission                            2
  req     admission ONLY                                         1
  iss     TMU issue; AUX issue; admission                         3
  clm     TMU claim; AUX claim; admission                         3
  cmt     TMU publication; AUX publication; admission             3
  rdy     TMU ready claim; AUX ready claim; admission              3
  crs     COMBINE reservation; admission                          2
  cbi     actual COMBINE acceptance; admission                     2
  fcl     final claim; admission                                  2
  fdn     final publication; admission                            2
  ftc     historical fetch marker; admission                      2

The ready events use the publication owners, so they are not extra arbitrary
owner targets in an aggregate address inventory. Required masks are the clearest
counterexample to the 64-port claim: after admission, their values do not
change at all. [N03]

The source still maintains ftc for verification, but its ordinary fetch guard
was already removed in favour of the monotone fetch cursor. Do not charge its
source-level assignments as a new physical saving. Reconfirm its consumers in
the selected elaboration before claiming either cost or deletion.

The supplied sparse/dense recurrence check independently encodes the whole-array
and event-patch forms. They agree over 6,000 randomized 64-row cycles, including
same-row overlaps and admission precedence. On an idle cycle the sparse form
performs zero semantic writes. This is a finite update-algebra check, NOT RTL
equivalence, synthesis, or proof that every generated event is protocol-legal.

2.2 A small possible RAM experiment: the immutable required mask

req_q contains 64 four-bit required masks. It has one admission writer and
several consumers: the TMU/AUX issue validators, terminal snapshots and readiness
evaluations. A one-write implementation is possible in principle. A single
one-read RAM is NOT enough merely because the write side is simple.

First inventory simultaneous consumer addresses and their original snapshot
edges. Either carry the required bits with the existing accepted records, use
explicitly priced read replicas, or retime whole validation expressions. Do not
read the new row one cycle later and call it the old admission snapshot. A late
admission can otherwise turn a previously invalid future token into an
apparently valid return.

Default decision: do NOT spend several M10Ks and extra staging to save 256
logical bits unless a controlled mapping experiment demonstrates a worthwhile
ALM or timing benefit. This is a bounded optional experiment, not the backbone
of the rescue. It is included to replace a false impossibility claim with an
actual port-and-latency decision.

2.3 Read timing is not decided by _q versus _c spelling

A synchronous memory samples whatever valid address is present at its edge,
including a combinationally formed address. The real questions are which edge
is available, when the data returns, and which identity/required/progress facts
must describe the same logical instant. Renaming a signal cannot answer them.

READY binds cmt, req, rdy and crs to one owner and one instant. Retiming just
one term breaks the expression. Retiming the complete transaction is legitimate
architecture work, with publication, reservation and forwarding accounted for.
An internal latency change is not automatically a change to the frame deadline.

2.4 Why phase packing is not a RAM-port trick

Combining issued/claimed/committed into one phase field reduces its logical bit
count. It can INCREASE the number of independent writes to that combined field:
issue, claim and publication may name three different owners, plus admission.
Likewise actual COMBINE issue, final claim and final publication may coexist.

Do not turn the two-bit encoding into an unsupported dual-port RAM merely because
the encoded row is small. Keep the narrow multi-event state in fabric initially.
A later banked or event-journal candidate needs its own worst-case service-rate,
backpressure, forwarding and reset proof. Delaying unbackpressurable issue
notifications is not made safe by adding an unbounded queue in a drawing.

2.5 Correct the documents once, then implement

Update the false 64-write-port conclusion and the remaining derivative
"no memory candidate exists" statements. Preserve the already-corrected live
claim arrays. Label the frozen timing-exclusion result as a diagnostic, remove
the mixed map/fit delta attribution, and use the native 10,837-ALM headline.

Do not produce a new general RTL parser or another project-wide tooling sweep.
The writer/consumer evidence is sufficient to define this experiment. Reuse the
existing source/measurement tools, and move on to the T0-T6 sequence. The absence
of one 3,337-ALM deletion does not prove that a combined architectural replacement
cannot close the gap; nor does this plan prove in advance that it will.

3. ARCHITECTURE: ONE LIFETIME, RESIDENT PAYLOAD, LOCAL WORK
===============================================================================

The proposed selected path is:

  external fragment acceptance
    -> owner allocation + captured admission job + descriptor writes
    -> qualified reciprocal service
    -> paired perspective U/V pipeline
    -> existing atomic U/V + early-descriptor join
    -> sample expansion and captured binding/planner work
    -> cache hit pipeline / separate cold-miss sequencer
    -> whole-response metadata join
    -> class transport and palette / direct / filter work
    -> resident sample/AUX result planes
    -> compact ready ticket
    -> phase-scheduled COMBINE reads from those resident planes
    -> resident final-result plane
    -> credited ordered output
    -> external acceptance returns the owner credit.

There is ONE owner lifetime and ONE required-source definition. An intermediate
service may release its own execution context, but it cannot free the owner.
The owner remains authoritative until the external output handshake.

Wide payload is stored once per necessary lifetime. Small pipeline captures
are allowed and required: "resident data" does not mean feeding a multiplier
or a broad decision directly from an uncaptured memory output. The distinction
is between bounded local stage state and a second persistent, owner-indexed
copy of the same record.

The new completion complex comprises the owner, its result planes, ready
queues, the material record, and the combiner scheduler. Treating those as one
architecture boundary lets it delete the wide transfer queue and the combiner's
second immutable payload store. Optimizing each file in isolation cannot make
that topological change.

3.1 Keep the useful V3 work

Keep the identity encoding, 64-owner window, exact return classifications,
claim/commit distinction, synchronous result banks, D0 join discipline, carried
palette identity, cache multicast, single blocking cache miss, and ordered
output. Keep the three independently written ready queues until a replacement
has an honest multi-event schedule.

Keep the current arithmetic oracles. New storage and scheduling must not quietly
change rounding or saturation. The proposed PERSP rescale and ROM products have
explicit identities and their own tests; they are not permission to "clean up"
arithmetic elsewhere.

3.2 Candidate integration, not permanent dual hardware

Build the new owner/completion complex and any changed-interface combiner behind
a candidate wrapper. The wrapper exists for differential and subsystem timing
work. The production selection must instantiate either the existing complex or
the new one, never both. Test-only comparison state must be absent in the
production elaboration, with an explicit capability flag where tests depend on
its existence.

A new internal module name is not a V4 console rewrite. It is a replacement
organ with a sharply specified interface and an existing behavioural oracle.

5. OWNER MEMBERSHIP WITHOUT A WIDE SUBTRACTION PER EVENT
===============================================================================

The current owner already eliminated the literal per-slot generation table.
Do not budget that old 512-bit deletion again. Its current membership function
uses the modular distance between a full ticket and the retire ticket. [R05]

For the selected 64-owner profile, this function has two exact alternatives
that expose the small physical problem more directly.

Let:

  E = retire slot, 6 bits.
  G = retire generation, 8 bits.
  U = live owner count, in 0..64.
  S = queried slot, 6 bits.
  H = queried generation, 8 bits.
  internal ticket order = {generation, slot}.
  public owner order   = {slot, generation}.

The reference is:

  live = unsigned14(query_ticket - retire_ticket) < U.

Exact factored form:

  d = unsigned6(S - E);
  expected_generation = unsigned8(G + (S < E));
  live = (d < U) && (H == expected_generation).

The small slot distance must be compared to a count that CAN represent 64.
Narrowing U to six bits would turn a full ring into an empty ring. Generation
addition wraps modulo 256, exactly as the original 14-bit ticket arithmetic.

Why this is exact: a live interval contains at most 64 tickets. Starting at E,
it can cross the slot boundary at most once. The slot distance determines the
position within that interval; the comparison S < E determines whether the
query belongs to the current retire generation or its immediate successor.
There is no reason for each event lane to discover that with a general 14-bit
subtraction if the simpler form maps better.

5.1 Alternative with no per-query subtraction

Compute A = unsigned6(E + U), with U kept wide for the addition. Then:

  U == 0:
      false
  A > E:
      E <= S < A, and H == G
  otherwise:
      (S >= E and H == G)
      OR (S < A and H == unsigned8(G + 1)).

The otherwise arm includes the full U=64 case. It is not an empty interval.
This form allows shared or locally replicated endpoint registers and narrow
comparators. The first form may map smaller; the second may route better.
Both are exact. Compare their mapped logic and timing within the same event
wrapper, not by aesthetic preference.

5.2 The time point is part of the identity check

Preserve BOTH snapshot validity and current membership at claim. A token that
was not live at the snapshot cannot become authorized just because admission
happened before its later validation stage. A token that was live at snapshot
cannot remain authorized after external retirement. The present owner explicitly
checks both for this reason. [R05]

Therefore, a retimed lane captures the relevant pre-edge window facts with its
other snapshot data and checks current membership at the claim edge. Do not
replace those two checks with one shared delayed Boolean. Generation equality
alone is also insufficient: residual row contents outlive an owner's authority.

5.3 Implementation and evidence

Implement a profile-guarded helper with named ticket decoding. Use the generic
reference for unsupported capacities until a separate proof exists. Compare the
helper with the old expression every cycle in verification and run wrap-heavy
RTL tests. Do not subtract public {slot, generation} words directly.

The supplied Python model performed 2,229,920 comparisons across slot positions,
occupancies, adjacent/wrong generations and randomized full tickets. It also
rejects slot-only, full/empty and public-token-order mutants, and includes the
two time-point counterexamples. This proves the tested integer identity, not a
particular SystemVerilog cast or physical resource saving.

===============================================================================
6. COMPACT OWNER PHASES AND LOCAL EVENT UPDATES
===============================================================================

The first control replacement keeps the state in fabric where concurrent events
need it, but reduces redundant Boolean encoding and repeated indexed decisions.
It does not pretend that one dual-port memory can serve every event at once.

6.1 Four monotone states per required source

The current source masks separately represent issued, claimed and committed.
In reachable normal operation they advance monotonically until the owner's
next admission. Represent each source by two bits:

  00  not issued
  01  issued, not yet claimed
  10  terminal claimed, payload not yet published committed
  11  committed

Keep required as a separate bit. Required is an admission contract, not a
consequence of which returns happened to arrive.

Queries become:

  issued    = phase != 00
  claimed   = phase[1]
  committed = phase == 11

Per owner this replaces three 4-bit progress masks with four 2-bit phases:
12 progress bits become 8. Across 64 owners that is 256 logical bits. It also
removes some independent read/mux/update expressions, which may be worth more
than the raw bits. The physical effect is not known until mapping.

Keep the old masks reconstructed from phase in the verification adapter. Compare
all rejection decisions and event transitions, not merely final colour. The
encoding is justified over reachable legal old states; it does not claim an
arbitrary corrupted old bit pattern has a unique representation. Mutation tests
must target equivalent externally observable violations in the new design.

6.2 Three final-progress flags become one phase

The actual COMBINE issue, final claim and final commit facts are also monotone:

  00  no actual COMBINE job accepted
  01  actual job accepted, awaiting final return
  10  final return claimed, awaiting payload publication
  11  final payload committed

This replaces three bits by two, saving another 64 logical bits. Combined with
source phases the logical saving is 320 bits. That is NOT a fitted-register
prediction. Some original logic may already pack or optimize away, and the new
decoding logic has a cost.

Keep COMBINE reservation separate. Reserving a bank read or a candidate ticket
is NOT actual COMBINE acceptance. A final result before that acceptance remains
unsolicited and must be rejected. [R05]

6.3 Event-local, statically selected updates

Decode each event's target row once. Route its narrow event mask to a static
row implementation, rather than constructing several independent 64-way reads
and rediscovering equality for every field. Use explicit precedence per row:

  - Existing state is the default.
  - Valid issue events advance only previously unissued required sources.
  - Accepted terminal claims advance the selected source to claimed.
  - Payload publication advances only the matching claimed source to committed.
  - Ready reservation and actual COMBINE acceptance update their distinct facts.
  - Final publication and ordered fetch update their own facts.
  - External output ends the live lifetime.
  - A valid new admission initializes the entire new owner's state atomically.

Admission must never overwrite a still-owned row. The existing no-same-edge
full-ring bypass is retained, so the most dangerous release/reuse case is not
introduced merely to remove one cycle at saturation.

Do not replicate the complete membership, generation and terminal-validation
expression into all 64 rows. Shared event validation and shared address decode
should feed small row-local updates. Repeating the entire validator per row is
another way to build a larger crossbar while calling it local.

Choose structured local arrays or static generate rows based on actual mapping.
Source formatting alone does not guarantee a smaller decoder. Do not turn a
single shared decoder into dozens of replicated copies to satisfy a style rule.

A further controlled variant captures only the requested source's progress
facts in the terminal snapshot, rather than carrying whole masks into a later
sample-index selection. The request's source is already known at capture. Keep
the same snapshot edge and full identity; keep complete/coalesced masks where
READY genuinely needs them. This may reduce stage state and selection cones.
Do not credit a saving for fields synthesis already pruned, especially on the
AUX path whose source bit is constant. It is a mapped alternative, not a reason
to change the observed instant of an issued/claimed fact.

6.4 READY is computed after publication, not after claim

A source being claimed says the design has accepted responsibility for a terminal.
It does not say the corresponding memory word is already readable. A ready job
must observe all required source planes after payload publication.

When TMU and AUX publish for the same owner on the same edge, merge those source
updates before testing completeness. Create exactly one ready ticket. When they
publish for different owners, both can create tickets. Admission of a zero-work
owner can create a third ready event independently.

Retain the current three single-writer ready queues initially. A proposed
single queue needs a real three-event enqueue schedule and bounded buffering;
it cannot claim a single RAM write port while accepting three records.

6.5 Forwarding follows the new snapshot-to-claim latency

The present return pipeline has an explicitly derived one-cycle recent-claim
forwarding window. Adding an extra snapshot or validation stage can make it
longer. Derive the window from the actual read, claim and publication edges;
forward by FULL owner identity and source, not by slot alone. [R05]

Same-owner back-to-back duplicate returns must be caught even when their RAM or
fabric snapshots both predate the first claim. A delayed issued mask must not
retroactively legalize a return whose original snapshot was unsolicited.

A useful design artifact is a cycle table with, for every stage: owner handle,
snapshot instant, current-window check instant, claim edge, physical RAM-write
edge and publication edge. The table is a requirement for review, not another
project in itself.

6.6 What not to do first

Do not RAM-convert cmt and leave req/rdy/crs arriving a cycle earlier. Do not
serialize all events through one memory just because it removes many flops.
Do not collapse claimed into committed. Do not move quiet or aggregate queue
occupancy back into normal admission. Do not call the false simulation-only
triage a deletion opportunity.

The first owner candidate is latency-neutral where possible: exact membership
factoring, phase encoding and shared/static event decoding. Coupled read retiming
is a later candidate only when attribution shows a remaining cone worth moving.

6.7 Concrete row-local implementation experiment

Begin with a candidate beside the frozen owner, not by editing its golden state
in place. Retain the same public ports for the first membership/phase candidate.
Keep event validation shared. For each event, derive slot-select, full-identity
verdict and source mask once; feed statically selected narrow rows.

Two code shapes are worth a controlled comparison, not two permanent designs:

  A. One whole next-state recurrence, with factored validators and compact phases.
  B. Eight static groups of eight owner rows. Decode group and row separately;
     a row updates only for its local events. Read-port selection is a two-level
     captured or combinational tree as the original snapshot boundary permits.

The grouping is a candidate for routing locality and shared decode, not a promise
that Quartus will place eight groups together or that 8x8 is the optimal shape.
Do not add a stage only to make the diagram balanced. Preserve current edge
semantics in the first comparison. A grouped candidate that simply recreates the
same LUT network or adds replicated control is rejected without an island fit.

Keep the complete reset/priority truth table next to the update function. Fold
all events for a row before writing it once. Do not implement separate always_ff
writers for overlapping row state. A single assignment solves lost updates;
self-assignment in a loop does not by itself dictate 64 physical write ports.

6.8 Reset, bypass and simultaneous events are part of a RAM conversion

For any later RAM-backed control field, the design sheet must answer all four:

  RESET: which valid/generation facts make uninitialized rows unreadable?
  WRITE CONFLICT: what if admission and an old terminal name the same slot?
  READ CONFLICT: which value does a simultaneous read need, old or new?
  FORWARDING: which newly accepted event is newer than a captured snapshot?

The preferred required-mask experiment writes only on admission and keeps owner
identity control outside the RAM. A valid read must belong to an allocated
instance. Replicas receive the identical accepted admission record; a dropped
replica write is an identity/data defect even when masks often happen to match.
Do not use no_rw_check as a substitute for defining the collision law.

The compact source phase does not erase the one-cycle recent-claim forwarding
need. If a read is delayed, recompute the forwarding window from its actual
edges. Forwarding by slot alone is forbidden across generation reuse.

6.9 Preserve predicates at their actual event, including fault behaviour

The current C2 validator checks both its snapshot identity and current live
membership. Some C4 publication guards historically compare reconstructed
residual generation rather than re-running every C2 predicate. Do not silently
strengthen or weaken one when changing the representation. First establish
which cases are unreachable under the selected external contract, and which
are intentionally covered by fault injection and drain tests. [R05, N03]

A stage retaining authority granted at an earlier event is not automatically
wrong. Re-validating against a later state can also be wrong if it rejects a
previously accepted obligation. The replacement must preserve the specified
acceptance/claim/write/publication/release law, not a vague rule to "check live
everywhere". Any intentional correctness repair gets a separate failing test
and a declared contract interpretation before its area is compared.

===============================================================================
7. READ-LATE COMBINE: OPERATE ON RESIDENT OWNER DATA
===============================================================================

This is the main topology replacement. Optimize the owner/result/COMBINE boundary
as a unit rather than shrinking either module while preserving unnecessary data
transfers between them.

7.1 The current repeated carriage

The owner already stores sample0, sample1, sample2 and AUX in owner-indexed
result planes. It then reads those planes into a four-entry wide COMBINE queue.
The top separately aligns the material record. COMBINE V2 captures another
immutable payload for each of its eight execution contexts and later reads
that payload for its scheduled phases. [R05, R06, R09]

At the selected widths, the owner queue carries:

  4 entries x (14 owner bits + 4 x 40 result bits) = 696 logical bits.

COMBINE's immutable context payload is 110 bits x 8 contexts = 880 bits.
That latter payload is already memory-backed in the current implementation;
removing it is NOT a claim to remove 880 fabric registers. The avoidable cost
includes transport, staging, selection and duplication as well as storage.

The current material record comprises the base colour/alpha, recipe, weight,
sample count and AUX selection information. Freeze its actual typedef and
padding before changing the interface; do not infer the width from a comment
about an older 48-bit layout.

7.2 The new boundary

Keep result data resident in the owner's existing planes until that owner has
fully retired. After all required results are published, enqueue only a compact
ready identity. The combiner accepts that identity into an execution context,
then reads the owner planes and material record when a phase needs them.

Proposed internal job handshake:

  cmb_job_valid
  cmb_job_ready
  cmb_job_owner[13:0]

The handshake is actual COMBINE acceptance: it reserves a real execution context
and authorizes the later final return for this owner. A candidate selected for
this handshake but not yet accepted has only a reservation, not issue authority.

Preserve a separate reservation fact if the owner needs to prevent duplicate
candidate selection while the job is held. Do not reuse one Boolean for both
meanings.

7.3 Phase pipeline

A practical candidate phase path is:

  Q: Select NEW or CONTINUATION ticket. Reserve the phase's destinations.
  R: Issue one synchronous read to each needed resident result/material plane.
  D: Capture the bank outputs and the FULL phase identity together.
  O: Select operands and register the two arithmetic jobs.
  M: Execute the current two-lane arithmetic, or its qualified ROM equivalent.
  F: Perform the original recipe's rounding/clamp and write scratch or FINAL.

The precise number of physical registers depends on the existing V2 phase
implementation and arithmetic choice. The invariants do not: every read belongs
to one owner/phase; every accepted phase has its output capacity; and a phase
that waits for arithmetic never loses its identity.

Do not insert a giant combinational material/result mux directly at a bank
output. D is an intentional capture boundary. A memory saving that replaces
one register bank with a long memory-to-multiply route is not closure.

7.4 Port accounting

The three sample result planes and AUX plane retain their statically separated
writers. The combiner is their one scheduled reader. The material plane has
one admission writer and one phase reader. One phase can read the five banks
in parallel, with each bank serving only one read address that cycle.

A continuation repeats reads of immutable owner data. That uses bandwidth but
does not require a second read port if the scheduler launches at most one phase
per cycle. The operation is legal because the data remains resident until the
owner's external release. If another consumer is proposed later, it must be
added to this port budget rather than silently sharing a port that is busy.

Reads begin only after publication. Therefore a correct phase does not depend
on a particular read-during-write result for a just-arriving source. Assert the
separation. Do not rely on "old data" in simulation while asking the FPGA for a
different collision mode.

Inactive planes may contain old or uninitialized payload. The captured required
mask and recipe must gate their consumption, including status/refusal aggregation.
Do not clear every payload RAM on admission merely to make unused reads look
pleasant in a waveform. Do not let an unused stale status bit refuse a new
base-only job. Test this with deliberately poisoned inactive planes.

7.5 What remains in execution-context storage

Keep only state that genuinely changes while the job executes: phase, owner
identity, continuation scratch and any necessary final-handoff reservation.
Do not recopy all source colours, alpha, base and recipe into every context.
The immutable material and results are read from their owner planes.

The initial context count remains eight unless measured schedule/feedback
latency requires a different count. Reducing or increasing it is a profile
change, not a free bookkeeping edit. A longer ROM pipeline can increase the
number of contexts needed to sustain one scheduled phase per cycle.

7.6 Preserve the actual eight-recipe law

Keep the existing V2 arithmetic, phase counts, rounding points, saturation,
refusal behaviour and final alpha. Preserve the current AUX substitution law:
where the existing integration substitutes AUX into the sample2 position, that
is the reference behaviour. Do not silently invent a fourth material sample or
pretend that a nominal three-sample-plus-AUX interface proves four-way blending.

Zero-work/base-only cases still enter one defined completion path and produce
one final result. Early fast results may complete ahead of older jobs internally;
ordered external retirement still controls their visibility.

The final result may free its execution context on a proven credited handoff to
the final-return path. It may not free the owner. If the final receiver can
stall, reserve a bounded holding place before launching the terminal phase.

7.7 Migration test boundary

Build a candidate completion complex with the compact job interface. Use a
verification adapter or reference model to compare semantic events and final
streams against the frozen old owner-plus-COMBINE composition. Do not force a
cycle-by-cycle comparison of an intentionally changed internal handshake.

Compare separately: accepted external owners; rejected bad terminals and their
classes; exactly-once ready/job/final events; per-owner arithmetic outputs; and
ordered external output under long stalls. The test must expose actual issue
versus reservation so the new smaller interface cannot hide an early-final bug.

7.8 Smaller fallback if the complete pull design is not favourable

A lower-risk intermediate candidate joins the material read with the owner's
result read, so one captured record drives the old combiner. It can remove the
separate material-alignment bubble without eliminating COMBINE's payload copy.
It is a fallback with a smaller deletion certificate, not equivalent to the
resident-phase design. Do not keep both as permanent shipping paths.

7.9 Deletion certificate

The candidate must identify which wide CQ arrays, material alignment state,
immutable context payload planes, adapters and ready expressions disappear.
Then mapping must show which actually disappeared from hardware. Report new
phase captures, mutable scratch and memory-port costs beside those deletions.
The arithmetic 696 and 880 is a topology inventory, not an ALM forecast.

7.10 Define the new completion complex at a real boundary

Create a candidate completion-complex wrapper containing the owner lifetime,
ready queues, resident result/material banks and read-late combiner. Suggested
new module names are implementation proposals, not existing repository targets:

  zhao_texture_completion_resident
  zhao_texture_owner_local
  zhao_texture_combine_resident

The first wrapper should accept the same external admission, issue, TMU, AUX,
final-output and diagnostic obligations as the old composed completion path.
The internal COMBINE job port can change because both endpoints are replaced
inside that wrapper. Do not change the external island interface to make a leaf
comparison convenient.

Port schedule for one launched arithmetic phase:

  sample0 bank  terminal writer s0        one COMBINE read of phase owner
  sample1 bank  terminal writer s1        one COMBINE read of phase owner
  sample2 bank  terminal writer s2        one COMBINE read of phase owner
  AUX bank      AUX terminal writer       one COMBINE read of phase owner
  material      admission writer          one COMBINE read of phase owner
  scratch       continuation writeback    one selected continuation read
  final result  validated final writer    ordered-fetch reader
  context       admission writer          ordered-fetch reader

These are separate stores because their writers/readers can operate concurrently.
Do not combine them into one memory unless the new ports and arbitration are
fully accounted for. The three sample banks may share write data, but each has
its own registered one-hot enable; dynamic lane selection must not recreate the
old unfused flop-RAM problem.

7.11 Canonical edge schedule for the resident read

The existing v3bank registers mem[rd_addr] directly at its clock edge. It has no
payload reset. Use that concrete latency, not a comment calling all RAMs two
cycles or all _q addresses safe. One conservative pipeline is: [N09]

  E0: a Q ticket is selected; latch bank read addresses and full phase identity.
  E1: banks sample those addresses and update their output registers; delay tag.
  E2: capture the bank outputs and delayed tag as a complete D record.
  E3: select/register exactly the operands and recipe controls needed by phase.
  E4+: execute the selected DSP or ROM arithmetic pipeline.
  EW: write scratch or present a reserved final result; retire the phase once.

All E labels are rising edges; nonblocking updates become visible after the
edge. E2 therefore captures the E1 read, not a new E2 address. Another latency
is legal only when the edge table and all queue/forwarding reservations are
updated together. Throughput is determined by initiation interval and feedback
availability, not by the number of boxes in this illustration.

Avoid retaining all 160 source bits in every downstream stage. The D record
may be wide for a single capture boundary; O and later carry only selected
operands plus needed alpha/status/identity. If pruning makes an explicit packed
capture cheaper than bespoke enables, measure it rather than mandating source
style. Bounded captures remain real cost and belong in the 1,900-ALM complex.

7.12 Immutable lifetime and context-release certificate

For every owner/phase, prove:

  - required source data is published before the phase's first read;
  - accepted duplicates cannot rewrite those source planes;
  - material describes the same admitted owner and remains stable;
  - scratch is initialized by a phase that owns it, not by a second writer;
  - an execution context is not reused until its final packet is safely held
    by the final receiver, or by a separately reserved terminal slot;
  - the owner is not reused until its external ordered output is accepted.

A continuation can reread immutable data only while this lifetime remains open.
Counting active arithmetic contexts alone does not establish it: a completed
final may wait behind an older owner or a closed output sink for arbitrarily
long. That stall must pin the owner without pinning every arithmetic pipeline.

Error and refused cases use the same terminal ownership discipline. Poison
unused sample banks in the testbench. A base-only fragment must not inherit a
prior owner's colour/status because a read-late unit happened to fetch an
inactive plane in parallel with the active ones.

7.13 Replication that buys a real port is not gratuitous duplication

The rule is one justified persistent copy per port/lifetime need, not one
physical copy at all costs. The early descriptor and ordered output can both
need caller context at different independent addresses in the same cycle.
Sharing one RAM between them without another read port can add stalls or a
larger mux. Price that explicitly before consolidating it.

The read-late COMBINE proposal is attractive because its scheduler replaces an
existing result-bank reader and it no longer needs its own immutable payload.
It is not permission to make every consumer share one central transaction RAM.
Centralizing all reads can be worse for area, locality and throughput than a
small, justified replica.

8. PAIRED PERSPUV AND AN EXACT RESCALE ALTERNATIVE
===============================================================================

Adopt and qualify the existing paired candidate instead of writing another
paired scheduler. The matched MapOnly receipts now give 3,361 registers for the
old service and 961 for pairpipe, both using six DSPs. That is 2,400 fewer mapped
registers in the measured leaf profiles. The new fitted ALM and composed effect
are not measured by those receipts. [R07, R08]

Keep two U/V multiplication lanes. The lockstep proof licenses deleting two
independent schedulers and their rejoin, not serializing independent U and V
products. The paired candidate's capacity is sixteen terminal FIFO entries plus
one held output, counted across all in-flight and queued work. Output acceptance,
not pipeline writeback, returns its credit.

First qualify the candidate at the actual island tag width and selected capacity.
Keep every tag, saturation and depth-zero flag in the same held record. Retain
the declared treatment of depth-zero U/V values when comparing with the old
service, whose flagged output values may be unspecified/stale. Do not claim an
arithmetic mismatch in a field the old contract says not to consume.

8.1 Remove arithmetic transport cost without changing the result

The generic current service carries a signed 32-bit numerator multiplied by an
unsigned 24-bit mantissa into a signed 64-bit product. It then adds a variable
rounding bit, shifts and saturates. The historical 56-bit narrowing shortcut is
wrong, especially at the shift-wrap case. This proposal is a different, exact
algorithm, not that shortcut. [R07]

Let p be the exact product and:

  s = (32 - k) modulo 64.

The existing result before 32-bit saturation is:

  signed64(p + (1 << ((s - 1) modulo 64))) arithmetic_shift_right s,

with the addition interpreted in its existing 64-bit wrap domain. For this
product, -2^55 < p < 2^55.

For 1 <= s <= 55:

  rounded = (p arithmetic_shift_right s) + bit(p, s-1).

Then perform the original signed 32-bit saturation. This identity is exact for
negative values as well as positive ones because the arithmetic shift floors
and the selected discarded bit supplies round-half-up.

For 56 <= s <= 63, the rounded value is zero throughout the legal product domain.
For s == 0, preserve the existing unusual wrapped-rounding behaviour explicitly:

  p >= 0 -> signed minimum 32-bit result, saturation asserted.
  p <  0 -> signed maximum 32-bit result, saturation asserted.

That branch follows the original signed64 addition of the bit at position 63.
It is NOT the mathematically preferable interpretation of "round by zero bits."
It is the existing generic interface's behaviour, retained to avoid an unapproved
arithmetic change. Natural reciprocal outputs occupy a narrower exponent domain,
but the candidate need not rely on narrowing the public six-bit input.

Apply the existing depth-zero override separately. The identity above describes
the numeric arithmetic path, not permission to change its flagged zero-depth
semantics or make previously unspecified output payload a new requirement.

8.2 Hardware experiment

Keep the full product first. Replace the variable 64-bit rounding-add stage with
an extracted quotient window, a round bit and exact overflow/sign guards. The
output may need only the low result window plus sufficient high-bit information
to determine saturation. Do not discard high bits before proving those guards.
Rounding can itself carry across a saturation boundary, so test after rounding.

The hypothesis is reduced wide add/shift carriage and fewer live wide registers.
It might instead trade an adder for a comparable mux structure. Compare within
one wrapper. No resource number is promised.

The supplied model checks 204,032 cases across every six-bit k, including extreme
numerators/mantissas and the shift-zero branch. It rejects the naive narrowing
mutant. RTL tests must still validate signed casts, expression widths and
stalling through the actual implementation.

8.5 Late branch evidence: remove token tables, not useful pipeline boundaries

The agent's new 12:13 CEST analysis attributes 3,376 declared PERSPUV bits to
the sixteen-entry token tables and 594 to _q pipeline state. Those are declared
bit inventories, not fitted register attribution. It reports that Gate 2 inferred
only the e_tag memory and that the duplicated mantissa registers were merged
back together. The full underlying native map/fit detail was not independently
available in this revision; this is the agent's recorded diagnosis. [N17]

That supports the existing paired candidate: eliminate the unnecessary token
selection/rejoin architecture, not the registers that divide the arithmetic
into timing-safe stages. The matched native MapOnly summaries already show
3,361 versus 961 registers and six DSP each. Qualify that candidate, rather than
spending another long iteration trying to make the old per-axis table split
infer by cosmetic duplication. [N14]

Do not generalize the mantissa result into "identical memory replicas cannot
supply extra ports". Register merging undid this PARTICULAR attempted inference
shape. Explicit independently addressed physical RAM replicas remain a valid
candidate when inferred/instantiated and verified correctly. Equally, do not
suppress merging everywhere by default: preserving two unnecessary flop arrays
would pay twice for the same failed design. Remove the duplicate scheduler first.

The same late investigation identifies PERSPUV, owner and cache as the largest
fitted register consumers: 3,240, 3,018 and 2,945 respectively, as transcribed
from Gate 2. Their total is 9,203, 56.5% of the island. This is why the plan is
multi-block, not an assertion that owner-only work closes 9,000 registers. [N15]

9. CAPTURE ADMISSION ONCE; DECOUPLE IT FROM RCP SCHEDULING
===============================================================================

The current top couples external acceptance to owner credit and the reciprocal
service's ready signal. Its reciprocal input also consumes live fragment pins.
Those equations are functionally aligned today, but they place service scheduling
and input paths close to the owner-credit boundary. [R06]

Introduce a small admission-job queue, initially two entries, holding the entire
RCP launch identity and denominator: owner14 plus denominator24. Other required
attributes are written to the existing persistent owner/descriptor planes on
the SAME external acceptance. Do not make a second full-fragment queue.

External acceptance occurs only when BOTH the owner allocator and this local
job queue have capacity. That one event allocates the owner, stamps its handle,
writes numerator/descriptor/material data, and queues the arithmetic job. RCP
later accepts the captured queue head. It never rereads the current ingress
pins for that job.

This is a registered boundary, not a ready wire with a flop attached after the
fact. The queue contains the work that the delayed ready represents. No owner
may be allocated unless its launch record has a reserved place. No launch may
occur for a fragment the external interface did not accept.

A two-entry queue is a starting implementation, not a rate solution. It hides
short backpressure boundaries; it does not increase the reciprocal worker's
sustained capacity. The throughput gate in section 10 remains mandatory.

9.1 Event and fence obligations

The external admitted counter and RCP's accepted counter can differ while jobs
are queued. They must reconcile after a complete drain, apart from any explicitly
implemented and counted bypass. A test that asserts equality on every cycle
would be enforcing the old topology instead of the contract.

Include this queue in namespace-drain accounting. An empty owner output path
does not prove no accepted arithmetic job remains queued. On reset, clear its
valid state; on a generation-wrap fence, do not admit new namespace work until
all old queued and in-flight producers are accounted for.

Retain conservative no-same-edge full-ring credit bypass. A late output credit
is not available early merely because its numerical increment is predictable.

10. RECIPROCAL THROUGHPUT: THE MISSING RATE GATE
===============================================================================

A small and fast leaf can still be incapable of the frame workload. The current
queued reciprocal executes MW0, MX0, MW1 and MX1: four arithmetic launches per
nonzero reciprocal. One fully pipelined arithmetic worker accepting one launch
per cycle therefore has an absolute lower bound of four cycles per independent
nonzero result in sustained operation. More contexts hide feedback latency;
they do not defeat that arithmetic bound. [R10]

The repository's measured context sweep is consistent with it: approximately
5.78 clocks per reciprocal at eight contexts, 4.38 at twelve, and 4.07 at
sixteen. The twelve-context leaf uses three DSPs and eight M10Ks and reaches
100.95 MHz. Those facts describe that leaf profile, not a completed island.
Do not subtract the twelve-context old service's 1,802 ALMs from a current
island entity instantiated with eight contexts and claim an 816-ALM saving.

10.1 Count the correct units

Record, for every workload trace and camera configuration:

  F = fragments actually accepted after the relevant upstream rejection.
  D = nonzero reciprocal jobs after exact reuse, not sample count.
  Z = zero-denominator jobs and their explicitly scheduled treatment.
  P = U/V product-pair jobs, including every independently required coordinate.
  S = material sample footprints after sample expansion.
  A = cache accesses and requested lanes; keep these two units separate.
  C = filtered channel jobs, not filtered footprints.
  M = unique blocking cache line fills, not missed lanes.
  K = COMBINE phase launches, not merely completed fragments.

The old 541,640 footprint count and later roughly 1,094,600 multi-sample demand
cannot simply be substituted into every one of these variables. The current top
already shares one reciprocal among the sample work derived from a fragment.
Do not credit that same sharing a second time. [R06, R15]

At 100 MHz and 60 fps there are approximately 1,666,667 gross clocks per frame.
The workload budgeting convention reserves 20%, leaving approximately 1,333,333
design clocks. If a trace really requires 541,640 independent uncached nonzero
reciprocals, four launches each require 2,166,560 cycles. That exceeds even the
gross frame, before stalls. The example is conditional on that job count; the
arithmetic is not conditional.

Build this rate worksheet before spending a full fit on an architecture that
cannot satisfy it. The supplied checks evaluate the lower bound, not a real
scene trace. A real trace that supplies F, D, P, S and C is still required.

These are per-resource capacity bounds. Do not add all stage-work counts as
though the pipeline ran every stage serially: independent stages overlap.
Conversely, cache blocking, finite contexts, response credits and dependency
feedback can prevent ideal overlap. The actual cycle trace, not a sum of leaf
latencies or an ideal maximum-rate diagram, resolves that interaction.

10.2 Default three-DSP route: existing V3 service plus proven exact reuse

Use the already developed queued reciprocal at the actual chosen profile. The
owner's standing six-to-three-DSP direction remains the default. Qualify twelve
or sixteen contexts for latency hiding, with the true token width and output
backpressure. Do not keep eight solely because the old service used eight.

An optional small exact memo can reduce arithmetic jobs where repeated depth
really exists. Begin with four entries, fully compared by the complete 24-bit
denominator, carrying result mantissa, exponent and zero status. The memo does
not store owner identity. A hit is retagged with the current owner and must
reserve response capacity before acceptance.

A four-entry memo is bounded control, not a broad fully associative table. Its
measured area and hit rate determine whether it remains. It must not compare
only leading denominator bits, reuse approximate results, or confuse an old
owner with a repeated numerical input. The reciprocal is a pure function of the
complete denominator under the fixed arithmetic profile, which licenses exact
reuse across owners; it does not license approximate interpolation.

Initially allow duplicate simultaneous cold requests to execute independently.
Coalescing them requires waiter identities, cancellation and response fanout,
which can cost more than a tiny memo saves. Do not add that machinery without
measuring a trace that needs it.

Hit replies and worker replies need a bounded merge and reserved destination
slots. Never let a run of memo hits starve completed misses. Owner output remains
ordered even if the arithmetic service completes in a different order.

For the illustrative 541,640 jobs and a 4.38-cycle miss cost, an otherwise ideal
memo route needs a miss fraction no greater than about 0.562 to fit the usable
frame, before other stalls. That means at least about 43.8% effective reuse.
This is a necessary-rate illustration, NOT an expectation that real scenes have
that locality. Measure it; a pretty hit rate on a constant-depth test is not a
production performance result.

10.3 Zero work and coordinate reuse are semantic opportunities, not freebies

Skipping reciprocal work for a base-only fragment might be safe, but the current
path sends such fragments through RCP/PERSPUV as well. A bypass needs its own
atomic route to the descriptor/expander and a defined treatment of AUX context,
depth-zero flags and evidence counters. Do not take the saving before that route
has been built and tested.

Likewise, independently required U/V sets need their own products even if they
share a reciprocal. Reusing a denominator is not evidence that coordinates,
binding, LOD or palette identity can be reused too.

10.4 Escalation condition

If the measured required D and the stall model exceed the three-DSP service's
capacity even with justified reuse, stop calling it a closure candidate. Do not
raise Fmax on paper, reduce scene complexity secretly, or assume downstream
parallelism compensates for insufficient reciprocal throughput. Section 11 is a
priced architecture alternative for precisely this condition.

11. CONDITIONAL DUAL-WORKER RECIPROCAL ARCHITECTURE
===============================================================================

This is a conditional alternative, not the default adoption instruction. It
uses six DSPs instead of the approved three-DSP objective. It must therefore be
explicitly selected and reflected in the texture AND whole-console allocation.
It must not be smuggled in as an implementation detail.

The reason to consider it is throughput, not a nicer leaf benchmark. A single
three-DSP worker is an arithmetic bottleneck if the required trace demands too
many independent jobs. The alternative preserves full arithmetic and increases
product-issue capacity while avoiding two complete copies of every queue,
context store and input/output shell.

11.1 Split by phase, not by owner

Use one context pool, initially 32 contexts, and two pipelined arithmetic workers:

  MW worker: MW0 and MW1 products.
  MX worker: MX0 and MX1 products.

Each worker accepts at most one phase per cycle. Each nonzero reciprocal consumes
two MW and two MX launches. The arithmetic lower bound becomes two cycles per
reciprocal. The context count must cover the real loop latency and queue policy;
32 is a proposed profile, not proof that all implementations saturate both lanes.

The existing exact arithmetic tile can be reused for each worker first. Do not
simultaneously invent a new multiply law. Keep the normalize, seed, truncation,
wrap, correction, rescale and clamp rules identical to the current reciprocal.

11.2 Queue and phase flow

  NEW -> MW0 -> MX0 -> MW1 -> MX1 -> DONE -> output acceptance -> FREE.

Maintain separate NEW and continuation queues where distinct writers can enqueue
on the same cycle. MW1 continuations should not be indefinitely starved by new
jobs. MX work likewise needs bounded arbitration against zero phases. Every
phase ticket carries context and phase; every result is written under the
context identity that launched it.

Zero input must retain one scheduled terminal writer. One option is to route
zero jobs into the MX worker's terminal schedule using a ZERO opcode. They do
not masquerade as four real products, and they do not create an independent
final-result write that can collide with MX1 on the same edge.

11.3 Make the scratch stores genuinely single-writer

A naive two-worker conversion gives MW0 and MX0 concurrent writes to the same
scratch X plane, violating the intended memory ports. Avoid that structurally:

  - Keep the seed X0 in immutable admission payload.
  - MW0 reads X0 directly and writes only W.
  - MX0 reads X0 from its immutable seed view and writes the updated X.
  - MW1 reads updated X and writes updated W.
  - MX1 reads updated X and W and writes the final result.

Only the MX worker writes scratch X. Only the MW worker writes scratch W. If
scratch X needs two simultaneous reads, one for each worker, mirror its storage
with the SAME single MX writer, rather than inventing a three-port primitive.

11.4 Example payload-port inventory

For 32 contexts, a candidate layout can use:

  P_MW: m24 + seed32, admission writer / MW reader             2 SDP slices
  P_SEED_MX: seed32, admission writer / MX reader              1 SDP slice
  P_OUT: owner14 + k6 + zero1, admission writer / output reader 1 SDP slice
  S_W: 32 bits, MW writer / MX reader                         1 SDP slice
  S_X_MW: 32 bits, MX writer / MW reader                      1 SDP slice
  S_X_MX: 32 bits, same MX writer / MX reader                  1 SDP slice
  RESULT: final mantissa, MX terminal writer / output reader   1 SDP slice

That is eight PAYLOAD M10K slices in a straightforward implementation. The seed
ROM, queue memories, preload/reset behaviour, output heads and any extra port
replicas are additional. Eight is not the total tile count. Price and map them.

The duplication of X is deliberate port replication. Report its physical cost.
Do not hide it behind one logical table name. Delayed continuation publication
must make the corresponding scratch write visible before the consumer read.
Provide bypass only where the selected read-during-write mode and cycle table
actually require it.

11.5 Illustrative schedule evidence and limits

The independent supplied queue model schedules 12,000 jobs through two workers,
assuming nine-cycle worker latency and 32 contexts, in 24,024 cycles including
fill/drain, or 2.002 cycles/job. It counts 24,000 MW and 24,000 MX launches.
This demonstrates that the abstract dependency graph can approach its two-cycle
bound under that model. It does not measure RTL queue bubbles, initialization,
RAM response latency, timing, or service stalls.

Do not quote 2.002 as an implemented performance result. The real dual-worker
prototype must reproduce its own schedule, under both saturated traffic and
arbitrary downstream stalls, before this route is eligible.

11.6 Allocation consequences

With six DSPs in paired PERSPUV and zero-DSP ROM versions of COMBINE and bilerp,
a six-DSP reciprocal alternative gives a twelve-DSP texture arithmetic subtotal.
That can remain below the local fourteen-DSP ceiling. It is three more than the
nine-DSP texture route contemplated by the global rescue portfolio.

The additional three must be explicitly allocated in that global <=94 plan;
unknown remaining functions do not become free because a contingency line
exists. The RCP ALM and RAM budget must also be revised. Two workers may exceed
the default 1,000-ALM RCP allocation substantially. A plausible architecture is
not a paid-for architecture.

No automatic selection is authorized by this document. Present the measured
three-DSP rate failure, the dual-worker result, and the complete resource delta
as one bounded decision. There is no need to reopen the console architecture
or weaken precision to make that decision.

12. CACHE: KEEP HITS SHORT, SEQUENCE MISS MANAGEMENT
===============================================================================

The existing cache's payload/tag memories are already synchronous and statically
banked per lane. Replacing them with another cache is not the first task. The
current combinational C3 path does too much at once: compare tags, identify
needed lanes, choose the first missing line, compare other lanes to that chosen
identity, form the multicast mask, count it and update miss/fill/valid controls.
That is a concrete route from a tag bit into broad control. [R11]

12.1 Separate the fast decision from cold work

Keep the all-hit path's response reservation and retirement cadence unchanged.
On C3, determine hit/need and whether any enabled lane misses. A miss captures
or locks the complete request identity, enters a busy state, squashes the
in-flight probes and rewinds the request issue pointer. It need not compute
and apply the final multicast update on that same edge.

Proposed cold states:

  M_SELECT: select the first needed lane from a captured need mask.
            Register its complete line identity.
  M_MASK:   compare captured requested line identities against that registered
            selected line; form the exact multicast mask and invalidate the
            affected entries.
  M_REQUEST: hold the aligned fill address and valid until the fill handshake.
  M_FILL:   receive the eight halfwords, write the selected lane banks, and
            publish the tag/valid only at the correct completed-fill edge.
  REPLAY:   resume the still-resident missing request and younger requests.

The expected price is a few additional cycles per blocking miss, not per hit.
The potential gain is a shorter tag-compare/priority/multicast/update cone.
Whether that is acceptable depends on measured fill count and the memory-service
budget. Do not assume misses are rare in an adversarial or cache-conflict trace.

12.2 Capture enough identity, but do not duplicate the world

The missed request remains in the request queue because it was never retired.
One implementation locks the queue head and reads its captured line identities
in the cold states. Another holds the small per-lane line IDs and need mask
locally. Choose by mapping and port availability. Neither may consult a younger
request's current addresses after the issue pointer has advanced or rewound.

Disabled lanes that happen to reference the same line must not enter the fill
mask. Enabled misses to the same line must still multicast one fetch. Keep the
existing one-blocking-miss policy; introducing MSHRs or speculative parallel
fills is a different venture.

12.3 The reservation invariant remains load-bearing

For the selected request/response capacity:

  response entries queued + noncancelled probe results in flight <= REQN.

A hit retirement transfers a reservation into the response FIFO; it does not
free it. Consumer acceptance frees it. A miss refunds exactly the probes being
squashed, leaving already queued responses untouched. The current cache has
C1 and C2 probe valids in that refund. [R11]

The cold-only split deliberately avoids adding another all-hit stage. If an
implementation later does add one, update the refund set, pipeline capacity
and issue rule together. Never keep an old two-valid refund expression after
adding a third in-flight result.

The supplied finite queue model exercised 80,000 cycles of stalls and misses,
then reconciled 39,117 accepted and delivered requests in its tested run. It
checks the reservation invariant during operation and rejects a missing-refund
mutant. It is not a cache data/tag RTL simulation.

12.4 RAM and load-order obligations

Read valid, tag and data with the same logical probe snapshot. Do not read an
old tag and a newly asserted valid bit on different edges. Keep statically
selected per-lane memory processes with clock-only payload writes; no reset
loop over data/tag RAM. The small validity plane can remain fabric state.

Do not accept fill data before the request/mask protocol makes its destination
unambiguous. If the fill interface permits a response on the request-acceptance
edge, that case needs an explicit bypass or stage arrangement. Assert the exact
provider contract; do not guess a minimum memory latency.

Keep counters off the hottest live classification path where their event can
be registered without changing meaning. Register a complete event delta, not
one flag while its mask still advances independently.

13. PALETTE: LOCAL SLOT CONTROL AND HIERARCHICAL COVERAGE
===============================================================================

The palette loader is nearly co-critical with RCP in Gate 2. Calling it a cold
path does not exempt it from a shared 100-MHz clock. The resident data is already
in RAM; the target is control topology, not moving 16,384 data bits that have
already moved. [R04, R12]

13.1 Preserve the precise load law

BEGIN accepts only a new generation. An accepted BEGIN immediately invalidates
the selected slot and must make a simultaneous same-slot lookup stale/nonresident.
A refused same-generation BEGIN must not perform that invalidation. WRITE is
legal only during an open load. END publishes resident only for the matching
slot/generation with valid CRC and complete coverage.

The load port currently never refuses. Do not insert a multi-cycle END protocol
or deassert ready without treating that as a real interface change. This candidate
keeps the existing load event cadence.

13.2 Eight coverage groups instead of a full reduction at END

Retain the 256 presence bits so duplicate writes cannot impersonate complete
coverage. Organize them into eight static groups of 32, with one full flag per
group. A WRITE updates its selected group and computes that group's full flag
from the post-write word. BEGIN clears the presence and group-full guards.
END tests the eight group-full flags instead of reducing all 256 bits through
the residency update cone.

The last WRITE followed immediately by END is correct: the last WRITE's edge
updates its group's full flag, so END sees the complete flags on the next edge.
There is no hidden extra completion cycle. CRC and invalid-END precedence remain
as before. A duplicate leaves its group no fuller than the presence bitmap says.

This uses a few additional small guard bits to shorten a broad control decision.
It is not a memory-capacity optimization. A one-bit M10K coverage table with
read-modify-write and duplicate-write bypass would be a disproportionate first
solution to 256 bits of rare-path state.

13.3 Local slot selection

Capture a one-hot loader slot selection on accepted BEGIN. Each static slot's
residency register can consume its local select at END rather than repeatedly
decoding a binary loader index through broad conditions. Keep binary slot
identity where the external matching check requires it; the two encodings must
be derived from the same accepted BEGIN.

For lookups, sample generation and residency at lookup acceptance. Carry the
accepted-BEGIN forwarding verdict with the request and RAM data. Do not recheck
against the slot's later generation at response time, when a new load may have
started. The status and data are one response record even on a stale answer.

The palette model checks 200 sequences containing 71,200 writes, including
repeats and the last-write/END boundary. The actual RTL tests must also exercise
lookup overlap with accepted and rejected BEGIN, incomplete END, bad CRC,
stray WRITE, back-to-back loads and reset during a load.

13.4 Placement only after the cones are explicit

If short local logic still routes badly, inspect the actual next specimen's
clock skew and placement. A small palette region or local register duplication
may help. Do not use a floorplan as a substitute for removing a 256-bit coverage
reduction from a slot-update cone, and do not prescribe coordinates from an old
fit whose placement is no longer the candidate's placement.

14. RESPONSE TRANSPORT: OPTIONAL RESIDENT PAYLOAD POOL
===============================================================================

The dispatcher currently captures complete response data, token and metadata
through a raw queue and class queues. Its own fitted attribution is about
471 ALMs. A replacement can save wide fabric carriage, but it is not plausibly
the source of several thousand ALMs on its own. [R02, R13]

This packet is OPTIONAL and subordinate to the RAM portfolio. The default
closure route may keep the current dispatcher if the proposed pool costs more
ports, staging or RAM than its area saving justifies.

14.1 One write, one scheduled read

A candidate stores each accepted complete response in a resident pool and queues
only its pool index and class. For the current widths, a complete record is
approximately 64 data + 18 token + 40 metadata + 2 class = 124 bits. Use the
actual selected typedef; do not hide padding or per-record status.

Twenty slots can cover the current four raw positions and four positions in
four class domains. A pool writer accepts at most one cache response per cycle.
One read arbiter supplies class-head buffers. Because ingress supplies at most
one response per cycle, one scheduled payload read per cycle can sustain the
aggregate steady-state rate, provided head buffering hides read latency.

This does NOT provide four simultaneous RAM reads. A design claiming four class
outputs per cycle from this one memory is missing replication or banking. The
existing class consumers may pop held heads simultaneously; those heads are
local captures, not four reads from the pool on that edge.

14.2 Read-port geometry must match the claimed RAM cost

The proposed 124-bit simple-dual-port layout uses four 40-bit slices, with depth
and padding chosen from the installed Cyclone-V templates. Verify the supported
mode and physical count in the exact-tool experiment. A true-dual-port alternative
priced as seven 20-bit slices is a different candidate and must be verified
separately. Do not mix the SDP budget with a TDP port schedule. The source bank
and newer vendor guidance are not a replacement for that mode check. [N09, N13]

All slices share the same write record, read record, enable, valid and identity.
The output capture joins them atomically. Use explicit registered reads and
validate the selected read-during-write mode, even if lifetime assertions make
same-record collision unreachable.

14.3 There are two distinct ownership domains

Pool ownership protects the RAM record until its complete payload is captured
in a class-head buffer. Class occupancy protects the pending consumer result
until that consumer accepts it. These can end on different edges.

Return a pool slot only when its complete read result has been captured safely,
NOT when the read was merely requested. That gives at most one pool-free event
per scheduled read, avoiding a four-consumer/four-free-write queue problem.
The class's occupancy credit remains charged until its consumer handshake.

Count read-in-flight plus held heads within the class reservation capacity. A
one-cycle RAM response and another capture boundary may require several local
credits to sustain one read per cycle. Size the exact implementation from its
cycle table. Do not retain four queued IDs and add four uncounted heads beside
them while continuing to call the capacity four.

14.4 Arbitration and adverse traffic

Select a class with both a queued identity and reserved head space. A stalled
class must not block all other ready classes merely because it owns the oldest
pool record. Use bounded fair arbitration; test a permanently stalled class
with continuous traffic in each other class. No ready class may starve because
another class repeatedly returns credit at the most favourable edge.

Preserve the explicit error-class terminal. An invalid class must not disappear
from a queue or leak an owner. Test metadata/token alignment on all classes,
not just the most convenient direct-colour path.

14.5 Go/no-go

Reject the pool if its SRAM slices, head registers, free list and arbitration
erase the mapped register/ALM advantage or violate the 64-M10K texture portfolio.
The current dispatcher is a valid fallback. Do not spend a full island fit merely
to discover that an optional four-M10K pool cannot fit the already allocated
memory budget.

15. MEMORY ARITHMETIC FOR MATERIAL COMBINATION AND BILINEAR FILTERING
===============================================================================

Small exact products are useful DSP-to-memory exchanges. They are not an excuse
to move arbitrary full-width arithmetic into enormous tables, and they are not
expected to close the owner-control ALM gap by themselves.

15.1 Exact unsigned byte product

Define Q(x) = floor(x*x/4). For unsigned bytes a and b:

  a*b = Q(a+b) - Q(abs(a-b)).

The two arguments have the same parity, so their quarter-square truncations
cancel exactly. Arguments fit a 512-entry table; a 16-bit table output covers
the required values. The proposed implementation is a 512x16 ROM with two independent read addresses,
intended to supply one such product per clock after its registered-read latency.
Its one-M10K packing is a required exact-tool inference check, not arithmetic
proof and not guaranteed by the source declaration. [N09, N13]

The supplied model exhaustively checks all 65,536 byte pairs. The RTL experiment
must prove that the two reads infer the intended single true-dual-port ROM and
that the following subtract/round stage meets the selected clock. A constant
array that becomes fabric LUTs is a failed memory substitution, even when its
functional outputs are correct.

15.2 Material COMBINE

Replace the two actual product lanes with two independently addressed
quarter-square ROM lanes, retaining two products per scheduled phase. That is
a proposed two-M10K exchange for the two current DSPs, not an instruction to
serialize them or to multiply the ROM count by every recipe call site.

Place recipe scheduling before the shared lanes. Extract magnitude and sign
for signed differences, compute unsigned products in the ROM path, then apply each original recipe's magnitude rounding, sign restoration,
add, doubling and clamp in its specified order. Section 15.2A fixes that order.
Recipe-level lerp/add/modulate identities must be checked against the current
C++ oracle and V2, including endpoint weights and saturation. [R09]

Integrate this with the resident-data combiner only after its functional phase
schedule is stable. The additional ROM latency must be represented in phase
reservations and continuation release. It cannot be hidden by asserting a
completion valid early.

15.2A Exact material rounding contract, independently checked again

The current C++ material oracle is explicit, and its distinctions survive the
ROM substitution. Do not unify these into one signed rounding helper. [N12]

  UNIT_MUL(a,b) = min(255, (a*b + 128) >> 8).
  MODULATE2X   = min(255, 2*UNIT_MUL(a,b)).
  LERP(a,b,w): d=b-a; m=(abs(d)*w + 128)>>8;
               clamp(a + (d<0 ? -m : m), 0, 255).

MODULATE2X doubles AFTER the original unit multiply rounds. With a=1,b=64 it
returns zero; doubling the raw product before rounding returns one and is wrong.
For LERP, a=128,b=127,w=128 returns 127. Replacing its magnitude rounding with
((d*w+128)>>8) returns 128. Both errors can pass broad random tests if ties and
low products are not deliberately present.

DETAIL_LIGHT has two rounded unit-multiply layers per RGB channel and retains
sample0 alpha. DETAIL_MASK uses the s0/s1 RGB product and s0/s2 alpha product.
MASK is a binary alpha predicate, not a hidden alpha multiplication. The value
255 still represents 255/256, not exactly one. [N12]

The new independent checks cover all 65,536 MODULATE2X byte pairs and 327,680
LERPs at five selected weights. They expose 27,594 wrong results from the
"double raw product first" mutant and 16,768 wrong negative-rounding cases from
the signed-half-up mutant in that selected set. These are model comparisons,
not a new RTL execution. The implementing candidate must repeat the comparison
against the repository oracle and its actual response/phase pipeline.

15.3 Bilinear interpolation: preserve the single final rounding

The current one-channel lane evaluates:

  A = (t00 << 8) + (t10 - t00)*fu
  B = (t01 << 8) + (t11 - t01)*fu
  result = ((A << 8) + (B - A)*fv + 32768) >> 16.

Do not round A or B to a byte. That changes the interpolation law. The current
implementation deliberately registers exact wide intermediates between the
horizontal and vertical operations. [R14]

For byte inputs/fractions, A and B are in 0..65,280, and B-A is in
-65,280..65,280. Two byte-product ROM lanes can compute the horizontal signed
differences. For the vertical product, split abs(B-A) into high and low bytes:

  abs(B-A)*fv = (high_byte*fv << 8) + low_byte*fv.

Use two more byte-product ROM lanes, restore the sign, add the shifted A and
round ONCE. This gives a proposed four-M10K, zero-DSP one-channel filter while
preserving the exact result. The supplied model compares 100,000 random legal
bilinear cases in addition to the exhaustive byte-product check.

Pipeline Q ROM read, product reconstruction and final rounding with complete
token/channel identity. Do not return channel results from different pipeline
stages under a shared current token. Preserve arbitrary downstream stalls using
either correctly chained elastic stages or explicit terminal reservations.

15.4 Do not confuse channel rate and footprint rate

This is one CHANNEL job per clock, not one four-channel footprint per clock.
The current design intentionally has one channel lane because the earlier
workload counted roughly 180,000 filtered channel jobs. That old figure is not
a licence to make every one of a later million sample footprints four-channel
filtered work. Measure the selected scene/format/mip mix. [R14, R15]

If filtered-channel demand exceeds the available lane cycles, an additional
filter lane is a separate RAM/ALM allocation. A second zero-DSP lane still costs
memory ports, ROM replicas, queueing and output assembly. Never advertise
zero-DSP arithmetic as zero-cost throughput.

15.5 Texture DSP portfolios

Using the current functional arithmetic split, the straightforward qualified
three-DSP RCP route gives:

  RCP 3 + paired PERSPUV 6 + material 2 + bilerp 3 = 14 DSP.
  Replace material products with ROMs:                      12 DSP.
  Replace bilerp products with ROMs as well:                 9 DSP.

These are planned component counts, to be verified in the actual composition.
The current composed baseline is seventeen, not fourteen: the RCP swap has not
been priced into that baseline merely because a leaf exists.

For the conditional six-DSP dual-worker RCP route, both ROM substitutions give
12 DSP. Keeping the three-DSP bilerp with that route would give 15 and breach
the current local ceiling. The trade cannot be chosen one block at a time while
ignoring its neighbours.

16. BINDING, SAMPLING, CONTEXT AND CAPABILITY INTEGRITY
===============================================================================

Resource closure must not be purchased by measuring a narrower machine than
the project intends. The current top and tests contain several distinctions
that must be made explicit before calling the next specimen production-complete.
This section is a capability audit, not authority to implement unrelated features.

16.1 One binding input is not a complete binding service

The current island exposes one fragment binding selector and global bind_base_i /
bind_mode_i inputs. The sample path and planner consume these through the existing
composition. A global live configuration input is safe only under a documented
stability/epoch rule or an atomic capture at the proper request boundary. [R06]

Trace exactly which accepted fragment/sample owns a binding base/mode. Hold a
sample behind planner/cache backpressure while changing the next fragment's
binding. If the older sample observes the newer configuration, repair the
binding transport rather than blessing the current fixture as the contract.

A preferred production extension is a compact binding handle plus generation,
resolved through a synchronous binding table at a named planner stage. Capturing
an immutable epoch reference can be smaller than replicating full base/mode data
through every stage. The binding table's read/write ports and invalidation rules
must be specified. Do not merely add another late owner sidecar lookup.

Existing tests that deliberately drive a constant binding are still useful for
that profile. They do not prove arbitrary binding changes while requests are
in flight. Keep that distinction in the acceptance record.

16.2 Three samples do not automatically prove independent coordinate sets

The current top has one U/V numerator pair and derives sample work from it.
Some arrays in the integration replicate that pair into multiple sample positions.
That can be correct for a declared material/coordinate profile. It does not by
itself implement every independently transformed U/V set implied by a broader
material specification. [R06]

Record which profiles share a denominator, which share coordinates, and which
need additional products. The rate worksheet must include the actual P and S
counts. Preserve full parallel U/V arithmetic for every independent pair that
the selected contract requires. Do not reinterpret a sampling requirement as
optional because it is inconvenient for the reciprocal/coordinate budget.

16.3 Mip and palette fields must stay typed

The LOD field is Q4.4 in an eight-bit carrier in the current top. A four-bit
connection in the wrong nibble previously made nonzero mip levels unreachable.
Test nonzero integer levels AND fractions. Keep the complete palette slot and
generation alongside the sample from descriptor to planner to response join.
Do not reconstruct either from an owner slot that may now mean something else.

Test CLUT/direct/bilinear format classification using combinations that actually
agree between binding and route class, plus deliberate disagreement/error cases.
A positive error counter in a deliberately inconsistent fixture is not proof of
normal production operation; zero on a path never exercised is not proof either.

16.4 The context remains opaque

The owner stores and carries the full caller context, but the public island
output exposes a narrower tag. That output alone cannot prove the high context
bits survived. Verify the full context at the owner boundary or through a
verification wrapper. Do not pack recipe or weight into caller-owned bits.
Do not increase the shipping output width merely to make a test convenient;
a simulation-visible probe can establish the internal contract without adding
unnecessary package-boundary timing paths. [R05, R06]

16.5 Epoch wrap is a system obligation

The local owner fence and generation window are retained, not declared magically
complete by the smaller encoding. The current source explicitly distinguishes
local quiet from acknowledgements by retained external producers. Old traffic
must not survive a namespace reuse and later match a new generation. [R05]

Before full integration acceptance, identify every producer capable of retaining
an old owner/sample token: arithmetic queues, sample planners, cache replay,
fill transport, palette/response queues, AUX provider and final return path.
Define stop/ack/drain semantics for each. An idle diagnostic is not a promise
that an external source can never emit a previously accepted old response.

Do not widen generation and call the wrap problem solved; that postpones reuse.
Do not add an expensive global quiet reduction back to normal admission either.
Normal credits remain local. The exceptional fence owns the global drain.

17. AN HONEST ALM, REGISTER, DSP AND MEMORY PORTFOLIO
===============================================================================

A successful collection of leaves can still exceed the device. Allocate the
selected whole texture composition before adopting attractive local trades.
Use mutually exclusive accounting groups so moving a block across an interface
does not make its cost disappear.

17.1 Proposed ALM allocation -- a design budget, NOT a forecast

  Group                                                     ALM allocation
  Completion complex: owner, result/ready transport, COMBINE          1,900
  Paired U/V arithmetic and its local result storage                   800
  Default qualified reciprocal route                                1,000
  Planner and captured binding work                                   700
  Cache hit/miss machinery and payload banks                          950
  Response transport, palette and one-channel filtering               800
  AUX machinery, including div6                                      500
  Remaining glue, admission, descriptors, expansion and Mosaic        850
                                                               ----------
  TOTAL                                                             7,500

The grouping is a target partition for the selected implementation. It does not
claim that the owner alone can hit 1,900 while another 537-ALM combiner remains
outside that number: COMBINE is INCLUDED in the completion-complex allocation.
Likewise AUX includes its divider; reciprocal includes its own ROM and queues.
Top glue must not be charged inclusive of children already in other groups.

The completion target is deliberately demanding. Replacing its topology, not
just removing 320 logical phase bits, is what could make it plausible. Use mapped register/memory/logic structure to screen out candidates that have
not removed the intended machinery. Mapping is not a measurement of this fitted
ALM allocation; do not claim a candidate passes or fails 1,900 fitted ALMs from
an estimated or absent map ALM column. Characterize the completion boundary when
its physical result can select the architecture. The budget is not a post-fit rule chosen to flatter the result.

The six-DSP reciprocal alternative may require a larger ALM allocation. It has
no automatic claim on unused room. Rebalance against measured savings or reject
that particular implementation. Never change 7,500 to accommodate it quietly.

17.2 Register accounting

Retain the 9,000-register redline. Record mapped and fitted counts separately.
Wide queues, pipeline replication, output captures and inferred small memories
must be attributed. Logical calculations such as 320 phase bits, 696 CQ bits or
880 immutable context bits identify structure; they are not additive fitted
register savings.

The 2,400-register PERSPUV MapOnly reduction is actual measured leaf evidence,
but the composed saving still depends on the chosen profile and fitter. Do not
apply a universal map-to-fit multiplier. The old and new counts are precisely
why a controlled same-stage comparison is valuable.

17.2A Fitted-register allocation to earn, not a prediction

  Completion complex including resident COMBINE                  2,200
  Paired U/V plus its terminal holding                           1,150
  Qualified default RCP inclusive                               1,450
  Planner and its captured binding work                            700
  Cache request/hit/miss/response machinery                       1,200
  Response routing, palette and one filter                          900
  AUX including div6                                               650
  Remaining glue, descriptors, admission, expansion and Mosaic      750
                                                               ------
  TOTAL                                                          9,000

These groups match the ALM partition and are mutually exclusive. They are
aggressive design allowances. No line above has been fitted as a new candidate.
An actual implementation can redistribute registers between groups, but cannot
silently exceed the whole-island ceiling or omit newly introduced staging.

The late register attribution makes cache and response payload queues worth a
specific experiment rather than blanket rejection because they are shallow.
In the selected four-lane, SRCW=18 cache, the raw request record is 4 enable bits
+ 128 address bits + 18 identity bits = 150 bits. Four request records are 600
logical bits; four 64+18-bit response records add 328. Reset-free synchronous
queue bodies with reserved/captured heads can move some of these 928 payload
bits into RAM. Address/valid/credit controls stay explicit. [N07]

This is not a promise of 928 fewer fitted registers. The new heads/prefetch
records cost registers, and a four-deep RAM is expensive in whole physical
blocks. Straight 40-bit SDP slicing would provision four plus three M10Ks before
any shared packing; exact supported mode/inference must be checked. Add that
seven-block candidate to the RAM bill before adopting it. Do not infer the net
benefit from a logical bit count or from the old queue's source width alone.

Use at most one such cache/response staging experiment if the post-T1/T3 mapped
register attribution still makes it necessary. Prefer a single-record, properly
reserved head over a new multi-record staging queue that recreates the payload
just moved. Do not make the cache and class dispatcher share one payload RAM
unless the two write/metadata timing domains have actually been unified and
the resulting port/lifetime protocol has its own tests.

17.3 Memory is not an unlimited residual pool

Gate 2 already uses 49 M10Ks. A conservative illustrative portfolio that assumes
a +7 M10K reciprocal replacement, +2 material ROMs and +4 bilerp ROMs reaches:

  49 + 7 + 2 + 4 = 62 M10Ks.

That is conditional arithmetic, not a prediction of the exact current eight-
context replacement delta. It shows how little room remains. Add the optional
four-M10K response pool and the same portfolio becomes 66, beyond the local
64 ceiling. The pool is then rejected unless measured deletions reclaim at least
the needed capacity without breaking ports or timing.

The resident-data combiner removes a private payload plane and some transport
state, which might reclaim memory. No reclaimed block is credited until mapping
shows the selected old instance disappeared and fitting confirms the physical
packing. A few thousand fewer logical memory bits may release no whole M10K.

The full console also needs video buffers, geometry/terrain stores, FIELD vector
register files, residency, detail-normal data and other consumers. Subtracting
texture's RAM count from the FPGA capacity does not give texture ownership of
all the remainder. The global memory-first portfolio governs that allocation.
[R16, R20]

17.3A Select a portfolio; do not add every attractive option

The fastest path to the local fourteen-DSP limit does not require both colour
ROM rewrites: a qualified six-to-three RCP replacement alone would change
17 to 14, if its composed mapping preserves the measured DSP change. Paired
PERSPUV changes no DSPs. This makes a useful INTERMEDIATE texture portfolio:

  baseline 49 M10K + illustrative RCP delta 7 = 56 M10K;
  retain existing material/filter DSPs; resulting DSP target 14.

That leaves eight local RAM blocks for a justified response pool or cache queue
experiment before any reclaimed blocks are credited. The more ambitious
nine-DSP colour-ROM portfolio reaches an illustrative 62 M10K BEFORE either
of those choices. Both portfolios use the same 64-M10K ceiling. They are
alternatives to price, not additive guaranteed savings.

The global <=94-DSP rescue may eventually require the lower texture DSP choice.
Record a fourteen-DSP intermediate honestly and reserve the later change in the
global bill. Do not declare the global nine-DSP allocation achieved because the
local texture hard limit is met. Conversely, do not throw away useful local
register reductions to pre-empt a global memory allocation never approved.

One late report says the RCP replacement "also removes roughly 870 ALM".
The old reciprocal's own attributed logic is about 870 ALM, but the replacement
is not free: it adds its own arithmetic, queues, control and memory. The matched
NCTX=12 leaf delta is evidence at that leaf profile, not a license to subtract
the entire old composed entity. Keep only the composed measured NET delta.
[N15, R10]

17.4 Every memory row gets a port contract

For each proposed memory list:

  - payload width, logical depth and physical mode;
  - number of physical replicas and width slices;
  - named writers, maximum writes per cycle and collision policy;
  - named readers, maximum reads per cycle and return latency;
  - reset/initialization strategy and validity guard;
  - required read-during-write behaviour or proof of noncollision;
  - output-stage capacity and the event that releases a reservation;
  - mapped inferred bits and fitted physical block count, separately.

M10K inputs are synchronous, and supported modes have different widths and
port capabilities. Clock/read enables are supported; an old comment claiming
conditional reads categorically cannot infer RAM is not a device rule. The
exact Quartus version and RTL shape still decide inference. Map-only RAM slices
are not yet a guarantee of final fitted block assignment. Consult the installed
Quartus 17.0.2 templates and actual native summaries; newer documentation is
background guidance, not exact-version packing evidence. [N09, N13]

17.5 Diagnostics do not get a blanket exemption or blanket deletion

Keep the required error classes, conservation counters and high-water marks.
A diagnostic used to prove a property must actually observe that property and
have an exercised denominator/positive control. Counters that must update by two
or three on simultaneous events use one combined delta and one assignment.

Move counters off long live combinational cones by capturing event deltas where
semantically permitted. Mark the counter latency. Do not delete counters to make
a reported area target pass while removing the only evidence that a protocol
still works. Conversely, avoid shipping redundant shadow payload solely because
a verification-only comparison still needs it.

21. FAILURE DECISIONS AND EXIT CRITERIA
===============================================================================

21.1 What earns retention

Retain a replacement when its semantic gates pass, its throughput supports the
required trace, and its total resource/timing contribution is preferable in the
selected portfolio. A DSP reduction that explodes ALMs or consumes unavailable
read ports is not preferable. A register reduction with no ALM gain may still
help routing, but record the real benefit rather than inventing a cell saving.

21.2 What to do when the owner reduction is smaller than hoped

First compare the actual decoded-event/membership/queue costs. Check whether
the compiler merged old arrays already, whether the new phase encoding merely
added decode logic, whether control fanout duplicated local terms, and whether
the resident combiner truly removed the wide and immutable payload copies.

If phase encoding alone does not pay, it can be rolled back independently while
keeping exact membership factoring and the resident completion topology. The
proposal is a set of testable replacements, not a doctrine that every new module
must ship because it was written.

If the whole completion complex remains too large, examine its actual read-port
and event schedule. A carefully pipelined banked control candidate may then be
worth designing. Its read expressions must move atomically, and its multiple
event writers must be funded. Do not jump from "fabric still large" to "one
M10K should hold it all."

21.3 What to do when area succeeds but clock does not

Read the new worst paths. Do not reapply the old credit-fanout diagnosis from
Packet C. Distinguish arithmetic depth, high fanout, memory-to-control capture,
clock skew and physical distance. Remove or partition the actual cone. Avoid
false paths or multicycle exceptions unless the real protocol permits them and
the corresponding hold/capture obligations are proved.

A pipeline stage must carry its complete record and its credit. A delayed ready
without storage is not a pipeline. A delayed write enable without data/identity
alignment is the D0 bug in another form.

21.4 What to do when rate fails

The reciprocal and filtered-channel worksheet is binding evidence. A 100-MHz
clock does not rescue a worker with insufficient launches. Select a priced
higher-throughput architecture, establish exact reuse in the real workload, or
surface the unresolved requirement conflict. Do not lower the workload secretly.

A memo with poor hit rate can be removed. A dual-worker prototype with excessive
ALMs can be rejected. Those results improve the design decision; they do not
justify pretending either candidate meets a deadline it misses.

21.5 Texture completion means all of the following

  - Correct selected capabilities and arithmetic, including all required sample,
    format, mip, palette, AUX and error behaviour.
  - Owner lifetime, rejection, generation/fence and ordered output obligations
    established at the intended integration scope.
  - Selected resource limits met without omitted children, shadow duplicates,
    hidden provider hardware or unpriced port replication.
  - Operational timing and the applicable qualification headroom met under
    their actual boundary constraints. Carry the 115-MHz texture qualification,
    required seeds and later 105-MHz renderer boundary unless a later adopted
    same-boundary ruling explicitly supersedes them.
  - Required production-shaped frame workload demonstrated with adequate reserve.
  - Source/configuration identity and native reports retained.

Only then freeze the accepted texture reference and proceed to the global
memory-first rescue's post-texture packets. A0 accounting completion is not
texture completion. Gate-2 area improvement is not texture completion either.
