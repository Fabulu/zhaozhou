0. OWNER RULING AND IMMEDIATE PRIORITIES
========================================

[Evidence: R3-R19, R23-R26, N1-N6. Rulings below are proposals.]

Do not treat the texture island as production-correct or timing-closed. Equally,
do not treat 69.05 MHz, or the newly reported 29.74 MHz leaf, as evidence that
Zhaozhou's intended capabilities are impossible on this device. The evidence
names specific implementation failures, several with direct repair paths.

There are FOUR separate acceptance questions:

  CORRECTNESS: Does every accepted fragment keep its own data, material, identity,
  status and required output order through variable latency and backpressure?

  CAPACITY: Does the correct workload fit the frame budget, including all colour
  channels, primary samples, AUX, cache misses and real material product jobs?

  PHYSICAL CLOSURE: Does the selected, source-matched hierarchy meet setup, hold,
  reset and interface requirements within its resource budget?

  EVIDENCE QUALITY: Do tests detect wrong work, and do measurement scripts classify
  endpoints and preserve provenance correctly? Green activity counters and a
  completed fitter process do not answer the first three questions.

The new information changes the first interventions, not the product goals:

  1. KEEP the b7b62542 context-wire and response-arbitration fixes. They repair
     real faults. Do not spend another pass rediscovering or reverting them.

  2. KEEP the 2c858dbf increment narrowing. Its two-bit range follows from the
     two-issued-jobs-per-cycle invariant. Verify that invariant and retain the
     full-width accumulated totals. Its speedup is still unmeasured.

  3. WRITE the exact ingress-to-combiner identity test immediately. Use a unique
     tag and immutable accepted packet, not a histogram alone. A single-fragment
     invalid-input-poison test should precede elaborate RAM or bit-OR theories.

  4. MAKE correct ingress capture the first functional repair. The source still
     reads live U/V numerators, material fields, base colour and class at later
     pipeline stages. A context transported faithfully after a late, incorrect
     capture is still the wrong context.

  5. FIT the corrected combiner in isolation as a bounded attribution experiment;
     read the new worst path. Do not launch an expensive complete-island refit
     after each speculative glue edit. Independent leaf fits may proceed while
     the functional top is repaired; they are not production acceptance.

  6. BUILD the production topology already intended: allocate a central fragment
     record before reciprocal work and retire only after material combination.
     Replace the private perspective ROB with a paired stream; overlap descriptor
     reads; use queued RCP jobs; cut measured cache and combiner feedback paths.

  7. CLOSE real bindings, formats, RGB/alpha filtering, palette residency, Mosaic
     consumption and AUX flow control before claiming a production island fit.

  8. FIT the selected functional island, then its real renderer composition,
     using the same semantics, parameters and dependency bytes as the tests.

The first small repair is NOT necessarily the final architecture. A diagnostic
context store can make the existing chain testable without immediately rewriting
all services. Preserve that useful checkpoint. The production lifetime design
must still eliminate the misplaced ordering boundary and unnecessary ownership
copies; a temporary adapter must not become an undocumented permanent architecture.

The old brief's broad structural direction remains justified. The new brief is
more selective about sequencing: do not rebuild a multiplier before testing a
landed narrow-increment fix, and do not debug context RAM timing before checking
whether the wrong context entered the RAM in the first place.

At no point may a weakened assertion, a zero-work bypass, an unobserved datapath,
an unapproved clock reduction, or a lucky seed stand in for the recovery gate.

================================================

[Evidence: R3-R7, R20-R26, N2-N6; V1-V2, V5-V7.]

1.1 Do not collapse different experiments into one timeline of speed
------------------------------------------------------------------

  Experiment                      Source       ALMs  fitted regs  M10K DSP MHz
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  historical texture island       afb7070f     7,720    11,790      18  17  69.05
  historical COMBINE.V1 leaf       4215c1c7     1,994     1,383       0   2  29.74

The island row has 25,872 block-memory bits, 889 virtual pins and 9,238 seconds
of recorded run time. The combiner row has 596 virtual pins and 9,417 seconds.
These are historical run durations, not estimates for future work. Both used
the provisional Cyclone V device and Quartus Lite 17.0.2. Each row must retain
its own sourceCommit; the ledger file's newest commit is not the source of every
row it contains.

The separate corrected renderer-shell result of 99.34 MHz is another hierarchy,
not an earlier version of this same island measurement. Neither 99 -> 69 nor
69 -> 29 is an established regression of one identically constrained machine.

The report compares the earlier II=1 combiner at 494 ALMs / 524 registers / eight
DSPs / 100.12 MHz with COMBINE.V1. Preserve that historical comparison, but do
not attribute the entire area/frequency difference to multiplier style alone:
control organization, visible counters, topology and physical boundaries differ.

1.2 What remains useful in the 69.05 MHz island evidence
------------------------------------------------------

The archived path table exposes an external depth-to-RCP seed path at -4.482 ns
and an internal RCP scheduler/address path at -3.630 ns under a 10 ns target.
The latter corresponds to about 73.37 MHz by the simple period/slack calculation.
Removing external starts from THAT placed netlist therefore does not turn it
into a 105 MHz design. This is a diagnostic calculation, not a new fit.

Representative archived path families are:

  depth input -> RCP context/seed                               -4.482 ns
  RCP valid/ready selection -> indexed context address           -3.630 ns
  combiner lane operand -> result record                        -1.590 ns
  cache tag comparison -> earlier c1 state                       -1.586 ns
  perspective queue pointer -> multiply result                   -1.493 ns
  RCP completion control -> perspective storage                  -1.360 ns
  perspective retire head -> FRAGROB AUX queue                   -1.166 ns

These are examples from a selected export, not a complete census and not a
promise that these remain the next worst paths after any source change. Their
architectural meaning is nevertheless useful: selection plus lookup, arithmetic
plus writeback, and backward control cones are spending too much of a cycle.

1.3 What functional failure does and does not do to an old fit
-------------------------------------------------------------

The old composed test took the zero-sample bypass for all 64 fragments. That
invalidates its claim to exercise material combination. It does NOT establish
that all material arithmetic was removed from the fitted design. Synthesis sees
RTL connectivity and constants, not the particular stimulus used by a separate
C++ simulation. A variable top-level context input can retain arithmetic even
when the test always happens to drive its material bits to zero.

Conversely, a truly hardwired class or an unconsumed Mosaic result can permit
constant propagation or pruning. Connecting a previously dead functional output
can change the fitted netlist. The changes must be assessed in the actual source
and resource-by-entity reports, not inferred from whether a test counter moved.

Therefore the 7,720 / 69.05 row remains genuine evidence for its pinned experiment.
It is not a present-source production certificate, a reliable final resource
budget, or a measurement of the complete advertised material workload.

1.4 The new COMBINE.V1 result and the landed patch
-------------------------------------------------

The repository identifies this exported critical path:

  Decoder5~9_OTERM21DUPLICATE
    -> Add46~41_OTERM2248_OTERM2772
  setup slack -23.624 ns at a 10 ns target
  equivalent period 33.624 ns; about 29.74 MHz

The old code accumulated jobs_inc[rec[i].recipe] inside a nested record/job issue
loop, using a 32-bit temporary per recipe. The new code makes that increment two
bits and leaves the eight accumulated totals 32 bits. The issue architecture
allows at most two jobs each cycle, so 0, 1 and 2 are the whole legal increment
domain. This is a sound target for a small repair, subject to proving that bound.

The report's stronger rhetoric is not accepted as proof. Small arithmetic width
does not rule out a long mux/control/routing cone. Decoder/Add names alone do
not prove every contributing cell or that synthesis literally retained fourteen
full-width adders. The RTL also contains wider temporaries, and fitted register
counts include boundary effects. Inspect the full path, mapped cells and before/
after fit before assigning an exclusive cause or a quantitative saving.

Also, the variant called LOGIC2 reports TWO DSPs, not its stated zero-DSP goal.
It meets the separate <=2 DSP cap but has not demonstrated the intended zero-DSP
implementation. Resource usage is whatever the fitter reports, not the name or
an RTL attribute. Section 11 distinguishes correction, remeasurement and redesign.

1.5 Preserve the classifier repair; do not overstate its result
--------------------------------------------------------------

The earlier ad-hoc script classified all 1,820 exported combiner paths as
virtual-pin starts because it looked for ':' or '|' in the node name. The now-
committed tools/quartus/analyse_paths.py repairs that specific fault: it parses
the top module's RTL port list and checks exact base-name membership. It also
refuses empty port/path parses. Keep it; do not recreate a fix already present.

Its own title correctly says it classifies WHERE PATHS START. It does not check
that both endpoints are internal sequential elements. A register-to-output path
therefore appears in its 'internal' bucket. The newest perspective example is
literally head_q[2] -> v_o[28], an internal start and a top-level output.

Consequently the reported -0.463 ns / 95.57 MHz value is not a demonstrated
register-to-register intrinsic core limit. Likewise, the 1.732 ns difference
between categories is not guaranteed recoverable pipeline margin. It is a
comparison of archived start classes, not a re-fit without physical boundaries.

For signoff-oriented diagnosis, extend the tool to classify BOTH endpoints using
the timing netlist's object types and exact port membership. Preserve UNKNOWN
for unresolved aliases. Its present RTL regex is useful triage, not a general
SystemVerilog parser or a substitute for an elaborated, source-matched netlist.
Fail explicitly on a missing named top, incomplete port parsing, or mismatched
report/source provenance. Test unpacked/multidimensional port bit names and
multiple declarations rather than assuming every top matches today's formatting.
[N9-N11]

1.6 Hold timing and the boundary model
--------------------------------------

The old hold report's worst path is:
  pal_ld_gen_i[0] -> u_palette|ld_gen_r[0]
  arrival 1.003 ns; required 6.008 ns; slack -5.005 ns
  zero data logic levels; launch clock modeled with zero network delay

This is strong evidence of a boundary-model problem, not a five-nanosecond
palette comparator. It is not permission to ignore internal hold or external
interface requirements. Report internal and boundary paths separately and use
justified clock/input/output models. A lower clock does not generally repair
same-edge hold. Fmax is a benchmarking summary; signoff also needs setup, hold,
recovery, removal, pulse width and constraint coverage. [V1-V2,V5-V7]

1.7 The area envelope remains a gate, not a waiver
-------------------------------------------------

7,720 ALMs exceeds the earlier 7,500 island redline by 220 (2.93%). The provisional
device has 41,910 ALMs; reserving 10% leaves 37,719 usable, not 37,719 reserved.
The island's share of the FPGA does not establish the full console's remaining
ALM, DSP, RAM or routing margin. Budget only the selected final hierarchy, without
double-counting superseded alternatives or pretending other subsystems are free.


1.8 The newest cross-block triage: useful, not one common source baseline
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The 15 archived reports span September 3-5. The newer correction establishes a
common 10 ns clk template in the block-fit flow. Do not preserve the withdrawn
claim that perspective's 82 MHz contradicts -0.463 ns: its overall worst path
starts at a top-level port and is -2.195 ns, giving 1000/12.195 = about 82 MHz.
The -0.463 figure came from a different START category. Both numbers can be true.

Common clock intent improves cross-row interpretation. It does not make source,

The triage reports positive internal-START slack for several older leaf fits,

The report also flags terrain_residency_v2 at -6.292 ns in an older report. That

2. CORRECTNESS DIAGNOSIS AND THE RECIPE ANOMALY
==============================================

[Evidence: current top/test R23-R24 as retained through N1; R8-R18; N2-N6.]

2.1 Established corrections -- retain them
------------------------------------------

b7b62542 connects .f_ctx_i(fr_f_ctx), selects the palette response identity from
a latched palette token, and prevents the bilinear producer from being consumed
when palette wins the shared response port. The class can now vary at the top.
Do not describe those exact old wires as still unfixed at this baseline.

Those changes do not establish correct early capture, legal palette residency,
complete format support or final ordering. Distinguish a repaired local wire
from the architectural guarantee it was intended to help enforce.

2.2 The first decisive source defect: delayed reciprocal, live numerator
----------------------------------------------------------------------

At island ingress, frag_ready_o is RCP24 readiness. The later perspective unit
accepts when rcp_r_valid && rcp_r_ready, but its numerators are directly wired to
frag_u_over_w_i and frag_v_over_w_i. The reciprocal has a token; the numerators
have no corresponding accepted-packet storage at this seam.

Timeline:
  accept A at ingress;
  producer legally advances to B or removes valid and changes idle data;
  RCP eventually produces reciprocal(A);
  perspective consumes reciprocal(A) with the currently driven U/V numerators.

This defect is visible without a timing fit or a theory of bit corruption. The
original input must remain valid only until its own handshake, not until a much
later service completes. Holding it for the whole island would serialize the
machine and hide the error rather than repair it.

2.3 The recipe was connected, but it is still captured too late
--------------------------------------------------------------

fr_f_ctx now reaches FRAGROB, but it is still constructed from live ingress
recipe, weight, count and context. FRAGROB accepts it when pu_valid && pu_ready,
after reciprocal and perspective latency. The source also reads live binding,
LOD and AUX request fields there. At later TMU issue, plan_src_id takes the live
frag_class_i. At material combination, base RGB/alpha still come from live ports.

A perfectly correct ctx_m RAM would preserve this incorrectly captured packet
perfectly. A RAM-read delay patch cannot recover an input identity that was never
stored at the right event. Trace capture first; then inspect RAM read alignment
if its input is shown correct.

The same opaque context is also interpreted as world coordinates. Packing recipe
bits into that word is not a valid way to retain an independently opaque context
and world X/Z. Keep typed, separate fields or a generated explicit format whose
width accounts for everything. Do not silently overwrite caller-owned bits.

2.4 What the mixed and fixed recipe counts establish
----------------------------------------------------

For 64 accepted fragments, recipes 0..7 repeated eight times, each with three
primary samples and no declared faults, the actual combiner job masks give:

  recipe                per fragment   expected jobs    observed jobs
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  0 PASSTHRU                 0              0                0
  1 MODULATE                 4             32                0
  2 MODULATE2X               4             32                0
  3 LERP                     4             32                4
  4 ADD_SAT                  0              0                0
  5 MASK                     0              0                0
  6 DETAIL_LIGHT             6             48               24
  7 DETAIL_MASK              4             32              112
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  total                                   176              140

The distribution is wrong. Its shape does not uniquely identify a bitwise OR,
metastability, a field-layout fault, a RAM-cycle fault or a sampling-phase fault.
A periodic input sequence sampled much later under structured stalls need not
produce a uniform or adjacent-neighbour smear. After the final input transfer,
the test leaves its last material values on idle ports; late capture can over-
represent that last recipe. Exact contributions require an instrumented trace.

The agent's fixed-recipe runs report 64*4 jobs for recipes 1 and 2, and 64*6 for
recipe 6. That is useful evidence against a permanently broken single-bit recipe
decode. It does NOT prove every combiner behavior or exclude every transition-
dependent corruption mechanism: even a hypothetical a OR b gives a when a=b.
The defensible conclusion is that varying-fragment association is the immediate
investigation, with a concrete late-capture bug already present in the source.

Do not replace one overconfident story with another. The proposed root-cause
claim becomes exact when a trace identifies the FIRST handshake where A's
stored/consumed fields differ from A's accepted packet.

2.5 The '57 accounted for, seven missing' deduction is invalid
-------------------------------------------------------------

The nonzero job counters imply 1 LERP + 4 DETAIL_LIGHT + 28 DETAIL_MASK = 33
product-bearing executions, assuming complete jobs and the current recipe laws.
Adding 24 bypass fragments from the INTENDED input mix assumes the very recipe
preservation already known to fail. Zero-product counters cannot distinguish
zero bypass fragments from eight, thirty-one or another number.

Thus the histogram does not prove seven fragments disappeared. The repository
reports 64 retirements, but neither that number nor any product histogram proves
unique original identities. It could be wrong recipe assignment, duplicates,
missing identities, or multiple mechanisms. Count accepted, combined and retired
FRAGMENTS by identity and recipe separately from PRODUCT JOBS.

Required accounting at a drained checkpoint:
  accepted identities = retired identities + explicitly cancelled identities;
  each accepted identity has one terminal disposition;
  input recipe histogram equals consumed recipe histogram when no legal rewrite
  exists; product totals follow the consumed fragments and frozen job law.

2.6 The highest-value diagnostic experiment
-------------------------------------------

Use simulation-only monitors at five seams:
  E0 ingress fire: copy the entire accepted input with a monotonically increasing
     software sequence ID and the real hardware request token.
  E1 reciprocal-to-perspective fire: compare denominator identity and numerators.
  E2 perspective-to-FRAGROB fire: compare context, recipe, count, binding and AUX.
  E3 FRAGROB-to-combiner fire: compare the stored record with E2 and with E0.
  E4 final output fire: check exact expected colour/alpha/status, unique identity
     and required output order.

First use one accepted fragment, then deassert valid and poison ALL idle input
fields. Repeat with two distinguishable fragments and alternating recipes 1/2.
Then run the 64-fragment case with changing weights, base colours and resources.
Hold a valid stalled input stable as required; poison only after its transfer
or while valid is low. Do not introduce an illegal driver to test a legal DUT.

Decision tree:
  E1 wrong -> ingress numerators are unowned; repair capture before changing RAM.
  E2 wrong -> metadata association failed before FRAGROB storage.
  E2 right, E3 wrong -> inspect context address/data/valid and overwrite hazards.
  E3 right, E4 wrong -> inspect combiner jobs, result selection and final ordering.

Log the first divergence, not only the final histogram. Exact per-fragment
comparisons can fail even when aggregate counts accidentally match. Monitors
need not add a single production output pin.

2.7 A diagnostic bridge, without creating a permanent second owner
------------------------------------------------------------------

For a bounded debugging patch, reserve a complete input record BEFORE offering
work to RCP. Gate upstream acceptance on local capture capacity; issue the stored
request from a credited ingress queue. The existing eight-bit token must not
wrap onto a still-live record. Use a bounded allocator/generation scheme or a
proved non-reuse rule; do not blindly index a small RAM by low token bits.

Retrieve the numerators associated with the returned RCP token, and preserve
metadata through perspective into FRAGROB. Capture any binding descriptor or
version required to prevent a later draw-state change from reinterpreting it.
Retain base colour, recipe/class, opaque context and actual AUX coordinates.

The bridge's slot may be released only when a later owner has atomically copied
ALL needed fields, not when one subfield reaches one service. The production
solution in sections 3-7 removes this temporary duplication: central FRAGROB owns
the record from ingress through final retirement. Do not globally permit only
one live fragment and call the concurrency problem solved.

2.8 The composed test still proves too little
---------------------------------------------

The test's loop may stop when 64 are submitted and retired, but its final checks
still accept retired > 0 rather than asserting an exact full drain. The added
recipe checks are plurality and inequality, not exact distribution or identity.
DETAIL_LIGHT > MODULATE does not prove a 6:4 ratio; 24 > 0 passes it.

A nonzero first colour is not a meaningful end-to-end oracle. Make base colours
different from the expected textured outputs and compare every fragment with
independent scalar reference evaluation. Assert all expected IDs exactly once,
all expected work, and no unexpected refusal or stale/cold data use.

The palette preload performs WRITE operations without a matching BEGIN/END
transaction and without proving the required entry coverage. The palette rejects
writes outside an open load. A lookup counter moving therefore does not prove
that the test has a resident palette or has returned usable palette colours.
Load the instantiated number of entries legally; check completion/residency and
errors; then compare actual colours. Do not bless undefined RAM data.

2.9 Bindings, filter metadata and missing colour work
-----------------------------------------------------

FRAGROB supplies binding+s, but the planner reads live bind_base_i/bind_mode_i
rather than resolving the sample's binding. Offsetting an unused binding cannot
create independent material samples. Three samples also receive a replicated
U/V pair instead of independently stored raw descriptors.

The bilerp lane takes live plan_acc_fu/fv when an older cache response arrives.
Store fractions, format, byte/nibble selection, class, palette handle and status
with the sample identity through all cache latency, replay and class queueing.

The current top submits low bytes of four texels as one channel and replicates
the result as RGB. That is not full RGB565 filtering. Decode the actual format,
issue all required RGB/alpha jobs, collect them under the same SampleTag, and
commit ONE complete sample. Correcting this changes capacity demand.

2.10 Palette/nearest convergence after the local fix
----------------------------------------------------

Palette token selection and bilinear collision gating are now repaired, but:
  - palette slot/generation still come from transaction-token slices rather than
    a stored material palette binding;
  - stale/nonresident responses must not be used as valid colours;
  - direct-nearest data is consumed without a complete return path;
  - the added class is still live at later issue rather than transaction-owned;
  - a fixed-latency palette response needs capacity reserved before lookup if
    the downstream result port ever backpressures.

At present FRAGROB's always-ready response input makes priority-to-palette a
usable local collision repair. Do not replace it solely for cosmetic fairness.
The production complete-class merger needs independently buffered, tagged
packets and accepted-only winner readiness with a bounded arbitration policy.
Unknown classes must become declared faults, not silently dropped obligations.

2.11 Remaining ownership and flow-control defects
-------------------------------------------------

Mosaic's chosen tile is not used by the real address path. Feeding its activity
counter is not evidence that its hash/pick logic affects pixels or survives
synthesis. Capture real candidates and consume the selected tile in binding.

FRAGROB retires BEFORE the out-of-order combiner, whose output is wired directly
to the island boundary. It cannot guarantee final order on the wrong side of a
reordering service. Keep the parent live through tagged final-result writeback;
only the central final retire stage releases the transaction.

The TMU descriptor issue FSM is I_IDLE -> I_READ -> I_HOLD. Even continuously
ready downstream, it transfers only one sample every three clocks. Overlap RAM
reads; do not confuse a three-cycle latency with an II=1 service.

The AUX reader changes ax_slot_q and asserts ax_pend_q while a separate clocked
read still sees the old address. An always-ready consumer can take old context.
Add explicit read/capture-valid alignment. This is separate from the mixed-recipe
root cause; validate rather than assuming every RAM read has the same fault.

AUX_PIPE advertises ready=1, overwrites its sheet-request stage without waiting
for sheet_ready, and can push two producers into an uncredited eight-entry return
queue. Preserve its arithmetic, but add credits and real holding/return storage.

FRAGROB return validation must check sample-index legality, required bits and
duplicate arrival BEFORE payload writes, in addition to valid/generation. Merge
simultaneous error-counter increments once. The combiner and reciprocal output
selectors must hold an offered packet stable when a different slot completes.

These are named integration defects. They do not imply rewriting all arithmetic,
and they cannot be repaired by increasing fitter effort.

4. TRANSACTION, CREDIT AND MEMORY LAWS
=====================================

[Evidence: R1, R8-R19, V3-V4; proposed invariants.]

4.1 Define the records once
---------------------------

Generate or share a conservative packed-struct definition for:

  FragmentRecord:
    downstream raster context (pixel/tile/depth/blend/provenance as required)
    raw reciprocal denominator
    sample_count and required_mask
    recipe, weight, base RGB, base alpha
    sample[3]: raw U-over-W, V-over-W, binding slot/generation, LOD, flags
    auxiliary world X/Z and auxiliary binding/envelope reference
    Mosaic candidate IDs/weight/enable, where required
    error/status aggregation

  FragmentTag:
    slot, generation, plus a flush epoch if chosen by the agreed protocol

  SampleTag:
    FragmentTag + sample index

  SampleMetadata:
    format, filter class, fu/fv, palette handle, subword selection,
    binding epoch/generation, error flags

  SampleResult:
    SampleTag + complete colour/alpha + status

  CombineResult:
    FragmentTag + final colour/alpha + status

The exact widths follow the real ABI and existing fixed-point laws. This list
is not permission to replace source IDs with transaction IDs: provenance and
return routing are different fields. Keep opaque raster context opaque.

A slot is released only after its final output handshake. No live input field
may be consulted to interpret a delayed result. Same-cycle input advance is a
normal test case, not an adversarial misuse.

4.2 Preserve ordering once, at the final boundary
------------------------------------------------

Allocate the ordering position BEFORE RCP24. Return ordering from RCP24, memory,
class decode and combination may differ. The final ROB is the only authoritative
ordering point. There must not be a second expensive complete fragment ROB inside
PERSPUV merely to restore order already owned by the parent.

Maintain an allocation-order ring of slot IDs or an equivalent proven circular
allocation scheme. Do not conflate physical slot index with allocation age.

4.3 Stable ready/valid and local credits
---------------------------------------

A held output must preserve valid and EVERY payload field until accepted.
A priority encoder selecting a different completed slot while ready is low
violates this law even if no arithmetic result is lost internally.

For a nonstallable pipeline, reserve an output-storage credit on input acceptance:

  outstanding = accepted - consumed_or_cancelled
  credit       = output_capacity - outstanding

Here outstanding includes both in-flight and queued results. Do not count only
already-produced results and discover overflow when delayed results arrive.
Credit returns on final dequeue, or on a documented cancellation that cannot
produce another response. One net counter update handles simultaneous events.

A fixed pipeline CAN tolerate unbounded downstream stalls with finite storage
if admission stops and every already-admitted result has reserved space. No
unbounded stall-duration assumption is needed in that design. Adequate capacity
relative to latency is required for throughput, not for that safety argument.

Do not quote average rates as burst bounds. Four multiplications per reciprocal
limits long-run reciprocal throughput; it does NOT prove reciprocal completions
cannot arrive in adjacent clocks. Credit or buffer those bursts explicitly.

Use small registered/skid boundaries to cut cross-component ready cones. A chain
of combinational ready assignments through five elastic stages is still a
combinational path; describing each stage as 'local' does not remove it.

4.4 Memory ownership must be a physical port schedule
----------------------------------------------------

For every proposed RAM, write down:

  word width/depth; physical banking; read address producer;
  write address producer; maximum distinct addresses each clock;
  read latency; read-during-write policy; initialization/validity policy.

Two independent writes plus an independent read require three address accesses,
not an ordinary simple-dual-port RAM. Calling an array a bank does not change
that. Conversely, two reads and writes at only two shared addresses may fit a
true dual-port structure; count addresses and collision semantics, not verbs.
Intel's published RAM guidance is explicit about this distinction. [V3,V4]

Keep small validity/mask/queue-control state in flops where indexed updates are
useful. Put wide persistent payload in suitable memories. Use static bank
instances, clocked reads and writes, and explicit fabric capture stages where
needed. Do not attach an asynchronous reset to payload RAM ports or reset every
payload entry. Reset validity, then initialize control in a bounded sweep.

Clock-enable and read-during-write behavior must match the selected device and
inference pattern. Use a small synthesis probe rather than trusting a comment
that claims an array 'must infer RAM'. Acceptance evidence includes the inferred
memory names, not only total M10K usage.

4.5 Finite tags need a finite-lifetime proof
------------------------------------------

Keep the ratified eight-bit generation unless an explicit interface revision is
approved. Reject sidx=3. Reject unrequested, duplicate, stale and post-abort
returns BEFORE writing payload or completion masks.

Generation is not an infinite-age defense. Stop admission on abort, invalidate
live parents, cancel/drain all child queues, advance generations as specified,
and acknowledge the flush before allowing old identities to be reused. Prove
that no old response can survive the permitted wrap interval.

All token widths are derived. A future change from 16 to 32 slots changes
SLOT_W. Do not silently squeeze wider identities into the cache's fixed 16-bit
field. Keep class metadata separate where possible rather than stealing high
bits of a provenance field.

5. RCP24: REMOVE SCANS AND PIPELINE THE EXACT ARITHMETIC
======================================================

[Evidence: R8, R19; proposed implementation.]

5.1 Why registering pick_i alone is not the final answer
-------------------------------------------------------

RCP24 currently performs free-context selection, ready-context selection and
done-context selection with combinational searches. The ready selection feeds
context lookup and ultimately multiplication. Normalization and seed lookup
also begin at raw input ports. The result is selected combinationally from live
contexts. The timing report identifies several parts of this organization.

A registered-pick experimental variant is useful for attribution, but it must
reserve the chosen job when picked, not when it eventually reaches the multiply.
Otherwise successive clocks can select the same pending job. Similarly, the
completion test must know about every new in-flight stage; inserting a register
without extending busy/phase tracking can declare a context finished early.

Use at most one small registered-pick experiment to attribute the measured
scan/address path, then compare against the queue-based production candidate.
Do not conflate that RCP experiment with the already-landed COMBINE.V1 counter
fix. They affect different path families and different hierarchies. Preserve
the exact older RCP as the arithmetic reference while developing the service.

5.2 Queue organization
-----------------------

Use eight contexts initially and the existing exact reciprocal semantics:

  FREE_CTX:  context IDs ready for allocation
  NEW_JOB:   newly initialized contexts awaiting MW0
  CONT_JOB:  contexts made ready by an arithmetic writeback
  RESULT:    stable completed reciprocal packets

NEW_JOB and CONT_JOB are separate because allocation and writeback may both
produce work on the same clock. They each have one producer and do not imply a
two-write-port queue. The issue arbiter selects one ready job with a bounded
fair policy. A small round-robin arbiter between two heads is not an eight-entry
search through wide context state.

There is exactly one outstanding job per context. State transitions are:

  FREE -> INITIALIZING -> READY -> RESERVED/IN_FLIGHT -> READY or COMPLETE

Reservation happens when a queue entry is removed into the issue pipeline.
A context is never enqueued twice for the same phase. A final result receives
storage credit before it can complete. Free the internal context either when
its result enters independently owned result storage, or when that result is
accepted downstream: choose ONE rule and include it in occupancy accounting.
The parent fragment remains live in FRAGROB in either case.

5.3 Front end and arithmetic pipeline
-------------------------------------

Proposed explicit register boundaries:

  N0: accept {raw denominator, FragmentTag} into credited ingress storage.
  N1: balanced leading-zero count and zero classification.
  N2: normalization shift and exponent capture.
  N3: seed-ROM output capture / immutable context initialization.

  J0: select NEW_JOB or CONT_JOB; latch context ID and phase; reserve it.
  J1: synchronous context read / capture the actual operands and status.
  J2: phase selection and, for MX, t = 2^31 - w; register 32-bit operands.
  J3: explicit multiply; pipeline inside the arithmetic block as required.
  J4: phase-specific exact rescale and writeback.
  J5: final reciprocal rounding/clamp into a stable result packet when needed.

These are semantic boundaries, not a claim that every implementation needs
exactly this many physical stages. Merge only after timing evidence shows it
safe; split the multiplier if its mapped implementation needs it. The critical
rule is that arbitration, variable addressing, subtraction and multiplication
must not all share one register-to-register interval.

Latency is not initiation interval. With multiple independent contexts, a deeper
pipeline can continue launching one microjob each clock. Conversely, a stage
added to a globally busy FSM can reduce throughput. Test both explicitly.

5.4 Preserve the arithmetic and prove any narrowing
---------------------------------------------------

The existing four-job algorithm is:

  MW0: w = (m * x) >> 24
  MX0: x = ((x * (2^31 - w) + 2^29) >> 30), stored as 32 bits
  MW1: repeat MW
  MX1: repeat MX
  result = (x + 64) >> 7, then the existing 24-bit clamp/zero law

Keep the seed table, rounding locations, truncation and modular semantics.
The current 32x64 expression is a promising physical-width reduction target.
The earlier architecture reports an exhaustive maximum w of 0x401FEF88, below
2^31, making the MX second operand nonnegative and 31-bit within that audited
domain. That is a REPOSITORY-REPORTED proof result, not reproduced in this review.

Do not infer a 31-bit bound for EVERY possible t from an upper bound on w
alone: w=0 gives t=2^31, which needs 32 unsigned bits. A zero-input bypass or
an independently proved positive lower bound on w may exclude that case from
the nonzero iteration, but explicit unsigned 32-bit operands safely cover it.

Before narrowing, rerun an exhaustive audit against the exact current ROM and
all reachable denominator/phase cases, including zero. Check m, x, w and t at
each iteration. Then use explicit unsigned 32x32 operands with a 64-bit product.
Store bounded fields at their proved widths. Invalid external values must follow
the agreed error contract rather than entering a narrowed unproved domain.

Do not replace exact reciprocal with a new approximation. Do not assume that
writing a narrower expression automatically saves three DSP blocks: inspect the
mapped DSP implementation and measure. The approved RCP target is three DSPs,
with four as its stated hard ceiling; those are gates, not our achieved result.

5.5 Context memory without accidental extra ports
-------------------------------------------------

Separate immutable and mutable data. Immutable m/exponent/seed/tag is written
on allocation and read on issue. Mutable iterate/intermediate values are written
by the single arithmetic writeback stream and read by the issue stream.

Avoid initializing mutable result RAM and writing arithmetic results to another
address of that RAM on the same clock. The seed can remain in immutable storage;
MW0/MX0 select the seed while later phases select the updated iterate. Completion
uses the final writeback value or a specifically staged read, not a live scan.

For eight small contexts, an explicitly budgeted flop implementation may beat
several poorly utilized M10Ks. Compare that variant with a legal-port RAM layout.
Do not force RAM for aesthetics, but do not call 1,000 unbudgeted registers free.
Keep operand-width recovery separate from storage-topology recovery in evidence.

5.6 Throughput and output safety tests
--------------------------------------

Long-run arithmetic ceiling: one reciprocal per four multiply launches on one
lane. Four jobs per reciprocal is NOT a four-cycle response-spacing guarantee.
Require exact four-job counts for non-bypassed inputs and a declared zero-input
policy. Preserve deterministic counters when introducing a zero bypass.

Measure launch utilization, reciprocal completions/clock, occupancy, each queue's
stall reason, and maximum starvation. Context count must cover the new feedback
latency. If eight is insufficient, measure the cause before increasing it.

A blocked RESULT head is a latched packet. Later completions cannot change its
tag, reciprocal, exponent or flags. Test with a higher-index result held while
a lower-index context completes; this targets the current combinational-selector
failure class.

IMPORTANT: the 1.64-products/clock requirement cited in recent discussion belongs
to the two-lane perspective throughput context, not to a one-multiplier RCP24.
It is not a meaningful direct acceptance condition for RCP24. Give each service
its own units: RCP microjobs/clock and reciprocals/clock; perspective products/
clock and UV pairs/clock; final island fragments/frame.

6. PERSPECTIVE: REPLACE THE TOKEN-TABLE SCHEDULER WITH A PAIR STREAM
=================================================================

[Evidence: R9, R19; proposed implementation.]

6.1 The latest array split is not a complete port or timing repair
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The reviewed source splits e_num and e_q into U/V arrays. That removes one kind
of cross-axis array inference ambiguity. It does not remove the path:

  work-queue pointer -> work-queue read -> token-table read -> multiplication

Nor does it make every result array one-write-port. e_q_u/e_q_v are cleared on
acceptance at tail_q and written at arithmetic completion at p3_i_q. Those can
be two independent writes, in addition to the output read. A claim that splitting
names alone gives a simple-dual-port result store is incomplete.

6.2 The simpler production form
-------------------------------

The parent already owns all lifetime and ordering. Each perspective request is
one complete sample descriptor:

  {SampleTag, U-over-W, V-over-W, reciprocal mantissa/exponent, metadata ref}

Launch U and V together through the same fixed-latency stage structure. Neither
axis requires an independent work-discovery queue or a private sixteen-entry
fragment ROB. The two products belong to the same sample and have the same
latency. Keep them together through saturation and output capture.

  P0: credited capture of both operands, reciprocal, tag and metadata ref.
  P1: explicit signed product stage for U and V, two physical lanes.
  P2: exact rounding-bias formation/addition.
  P3: exact arithmetic rescale.
  P4: saturation of both coordinates; OR their status; produce one pair.
  P5: stable pair output FIFO / local consumer capture if needed.

Retain the current numerical law until a separately proved width reduction is
available. Signed numerator times unsigned mantissa must be expressed with
explicit sign extension. Avoid accidental unsigned multiplication or truncating
the product merely because final coordinates are 32 bits. Validate legal exponent
bounds and existing malformed-input behavior before narrowing shifts or bias.

A zero-denominator/error request takes a tagged deterministic bypass that merges
back without losing identity. It must not silently disappear or overtake output
where ordered service semantics are required. The final parent ROB handles
external ordering, so local out-of-order bypass is legal if correctly tagged.

6.3 Capacity and acceptance
---------------------------

Target initiation interval: one COMPLETE UV pair per clock when credited.
That is two arithmetic products per clock, not two fragments. Preserve the
existing measured-service throughput test or replace it with a stronger paired
one that names its units and legal workload explicitly.

Reserve output capacity for every accepted pair. Depth should sustain the
pipeline's latency and declared burst target, with admission stopping safely
under arbitrary downstream stall. Record utilization, input gaps and blocked
output separately; do not report input starvation as an arithmetic limitation.

The expected advantage is removal of the table storage and pointer-to-multiply
cone, not merely shifting the same arrays into different variable names. Measure
ALMs, registers, DSPs, RAM inference and pair throughput together.

7. FRAGROB, DESCRIPTOR EXPANSION AND FINAL RETIREMENT
====================================================

[Evidence: R10, R19; proposed implementation.]

7.1 Allocate BEFORE reciprocal and retain ownership THROUGH combination
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Move its architectural role, rather than wrapping the existing incorrectly
ordered chain with another full ROB. Keep a single central free/ordering manager,
per-slot masks, immutable descriptors, sample result banks and final result bank.
Existing useful free-list, generation and registered-read mechanisms may be
reused after their boundary assumptions are updated.

On ingress acceptance:

  allocate slot and ordering position;
  increment its generation;
  capture all descriptors/meta/base/context;
  issue one reciprocal only when the material needs perspective work;
  issue one AUX descriptor event if required;
  create an immediate completion-check event for genuine no-work bypasses.

On final output acceptance, and only then:

  invalidate the transaction;
  advance the ordering ring;
  return its slot.

7.2 Replace the serialized sample FSM with an overlapping memory pipeline
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The current issue sequence spends one clock choosing a descriptor, one reading,
and another offering it, then returns to idle. With three samples per fragment,
that is nine issue clocks per fragment before any cache cost. No amount of calling
those states 'pipeline stages' changes their serialized control.

Production expansion should overlap different samples:

  cycle t:     issue descriptor read for sample 0
  cycle t+1:   capture sample 0; issue descriptor read for sample 1
  cycle t+2:   present sample 0; capture sample 1; issue sample 2 read
  cycle t+3:   present sample 1; capture sample 2; optionally read next fragment
  cycle t+4:   present sample 2; advance the next fragment's pipeline

Every register carries a valid bit and SampleTag. A stall may pause admission
at a registered credited boundary; it must not mix a new address with an old
data word. At steady state, the descriptor engine can issue one sample each
clock, including across parent boundaries when downstream capacity exists.

Use a reciprocal-result FIFO plus a per-parent sample cursor, as the earlier
architecture proposes. That eliminates the three-write sample-work queue.
But size it for actual completion bursts or use result credits: average RCP
rate is insufficient justification for a two-entry buffer.

7.3 Bank storage and expose the actual samples
---------------------------------------------

Use static banks by sample index for descriptors and results. Parallel writes
of an accepted three-sample descriptor go to three physical banks, not three
addresses in one ordinary RAM. Capture synchronous outputs before combining.

The newer FRAGROB already has registered o_s_rgb/o_s_a outputs for all three
samples. Reuse that correctness improvement. Do not reintroduce live head-index
reads to avoid a clock. If the final architecture reads result banks by a combine
request token instead of the retire head, the token must be latched with the RAM
address and matched to the arriving words.

7.4 Completion events, not a table-wide scan
-------------------------------------------

A sample or AUX return validates identity and updates only its indexed state.
Then it schedules a completion-check token. A short stage sees the updated masks
and enqueues a combine request once all required data is present. A per-slot
combine_enqueued bit prevents double scheduling when two returns arrive together.

Two sources may create events in one clock. Give them separate small skids or a
banked two-producer structure. Explicitly distinguish duplicate events (harmless
if deduplicated) from duplicate sample results (a fault, never an overwrite).

The combiner writes FINAL_RESULT[slot] and final_ready. It does not free the
fragment. This naturally permits fast material recipes to complete ahead of slow
ones without corrupting external order.

7.5 Pipelined final retirement
------------------------------

Replace the head lookup -> mask lookup -> selected payload interval with a
registered head descriptor and a separate completeness/read step. For throughput,
prefetch the next eligible final result while the current one is in a stable
output register or skid. Preserve the no-overwrite and no-reuse rules.

Initial target: one final fragment per clock on the all-bypass/hot path, subject
to the declared downstream acceptance and the true workload. A three-state FSM
that retires once every three clocks should be counted as such, not described as
II=1 merely because its RAM is synchronous.

7.6 AUX read and accounting repairs
-----------------------------------

Fix the ax_slot/ax_ctx validity mismatch with an explicit read-valid pipeline.
The valid bit becomes visible only with the context for the same token.

Validate sample index <3, required bit set, arrived bit clear, slot-valid and
generation before committing TMU data. Combine simultaneous error increments
once. Prove all counters and free/live counts against accepted handshakes.

Keep sixteen slots initially to retain the approved baseline, but measure the
whole transaction residency time. Little's-law accounting matters: required live
slots is approximately arrival rate times average lifetime, with additional burst
margin. Sixteen cannot be assumed adequate for an arbitrarily deep service chain.
A 16/32-slot comparison is a later measured capacity decision, not a default
substitute for fixing serialization or missing credits.

8. CACHE_PIPE: CUT THE MISS-CLASSIFICATION FEEDBACK PATH
======================================================

[Evidence: R3, R11; proposed implementation.]

8.1 The observed issue is a control loop across pipeline stages
--------------------------------------------------------------

The current source compares C2 tags, computes miss information, and includes
!c3_miss in c1_go. The measured path from c2_tag into c1_tag and fill/miss state
is consistent with that backward dependency. The RAM banking work was useful,
but a synchronous RAM does not make the entire surrounding pipeline fast.

Keep the working static lane data banks, explicit validity and exact address/tag
semantics. Do not build a new unrelated cache policy or add multiple outstanding
misses before fixing this concrete feedback path.

8.2 Register classification and use a bounded probe window
----------------------------------------------------------

Proposed decomposition:

  C0: capture/queue the request token and addresses.
  C1: issue registered addresses to the lane RAMs with probe identity/epoch.
  C2: capture RAM tag, valid and data outputs with the matching request.
  C3: register per-lane hit/need decisions.
  C4: commit a hit result or dispatch a registered miss descriptor.

C1 admission depends on local probe credits and previously registered miss state,
not combinational tag equality being evaluated several stages downstream.

Because the first miss is now discovered later, younger probes may already be
in flight. Preserve the current in-order/replay model explicitly:

  - identify each probe's request position and replay epoch;
  - on the oldest miss, record the oldest uncommitted request;
  - discard younger speculative decisions from the old probe epoch;
  - release their PROBE credits, not their external transaction identities;
  - service the fill completely;
  - restart issue from the oldest uncommitted request;
  - never emit two sample responses for one external request.

An extra cycle of miss-detection latency is acceptable. Lost or duplicated
responses are not. This design avoids propagating same-cycle tag comparison
through the entire issue enable and counter network.

8.3 Reserve capacity before an unstallable read
------------------------------------------------

A synchronous RAM result that cannot be stopped needs storage reserved before
its address is launched. Track in-flight probe/decision reservations as well as
already queued responses. The response FIFO simply being non-full TODAY does
not prove it can hold all results already launched.

A practical first candidate has a probe/decision window at least as deep as its
unstallable stages, plus registered output elasticity. Derive the exact depth
from the implementation. Demonstrate continuous all-hit acceptance after fill
and safe suspension under output stall. Retain a single miss engine initially.

8.4 Fill protocol, generation and counters
------------------------------------------

The line has eight halfword beats at the current 16-byte line size. A line becomes
valid only after every required beat is captured. A miss may multicast a line
to multiple static lane banks using a registered mask. Snapshot that mask and
line identity; later requests must not alter an outstanding fill.

Preserve request identity on every replay. Keep external accepts, lane hits,
line fills, multicast lanes and speculative replays as different counters. A
replay is not a second sample retirement. Move noncritical event accumulation
behind registered event pulses if counter fanout contributes to timing; preserve
exact counts, including multi-lane events in one clock.

Test alternating addresses colliding in the same cache index, partial fills,
misses on different taps, fill backpressure, output backpressure, replays during
new arrivals, and binding-generation changes at the permitted boundary. Do not
claim a clock fix from an all-hot one-line benchmark alone.

9. BINDINGS, MOSAIC AND THE TMU PLANNER
=====================================

[Evidence: R1, R12, R18-R19, R23.]

9.1 Capture static work once, not from live global ports
-------------------------------------------------------

The binding table owns resolved base address, format/filter policy, dimensions,
mip descriptors, palette handle and generation. Program it outside the hot
per-sample loop using the declared resource-upload/residency boundary.

A frame references immutable/pinned binding generations. A lookup either returns
that binding or reports stale/not-resident according to the contract. Do not
silently substitute whichever binding currently occupies the slot.

At the sample boundary, use SampleTag to resolve its stored binding. Carry a
resolved binding record or a short-lived resident reference through the planner.
No later stage may sample bind_base_i or bind_mode_i without a corresponding
transaction handshake and lifetime guarantee.

9.2 Put Mosaic on the real data path
------------------------------------

Mosaic is a material/tile selector, not a texel sampler. Its exact hash, modulo,
negative-coordinate fold and weight extrema are frozen semantic laws.

Keep the existing exact shift/add implementation where verified. Connect:

  stored candidates + projected/sample coordinates + enable
       -> Mosaic pick
       -> selected tile/binding/mip base
       -> actual planner addresses

Do not derive candidates from RGB bytes. Carry its token through every added
stage. Either include it in the ordinary credited stream or provide proper
fork/join buffering if other work proceeds in parallel. Ignoring req_ready is
not a fork implementation.

The selected tile and folded coordinates must be reconciled with the reference's
coordinate units and wrap behavior. Do not accidentally apply the mirror fold
twice or feed a folded coordinate to the world-space hash. Preserve fold-only
walls/undersides when Mosaic selection is disabled.

Only add more register cuts in the hash/modulo path if the complete connected
implementation actually needs them. The old isolated ~79 MHz result is historical
evidence, not a diagnosis of the now-connected production shape. A counter-only
Mosaic in a fit is insufficient evidence in either direction.

9.3 Keep the planner's useful arithmetic staging
------------------------------------------------

The current TMU_PLAN already separates request capture, mode selection,
coordinate/fraction formation, wrapping/row calculation, and final addresses.
Its recent bounded-coordinate work should not be thrown away blindly.

Replace repeated static binding/mip calculations with resolved descriptors when
that matches the approved architecture. Preserve the existing format encodings,
wrap encodings, signed/floored behavior, REP4 edge cases and error handling. The
source records several errors caused by guessing those encodings; do not repeat
them during a 'cleanup'.

Attach SampleMetadata before the cache request is accepted. A convenient form:

  SAMPLE_META[slot][sidx] := {fu,fv,format,class,palette,subword,status}
  cache_request          := {SampleTag, enabled taps, byte addresses}

This metadata slot lives until that sample has finished decoding. If any stage
permits multiple outstanding lookups for the same sample, add an explicit lookup
sub-identity rather than overwriting its metadata.

Current class is forced to bilinear in the top; production class must come from
the resolved format/filter semantics. An unsupported combination is refused
explicitly and completed/faulted coherently; it is never an unconsumed response.

10. RESPONSE DISPATCH, DIRECT FORMATS, PALETTE AND FILTERING
=========================================================

[Evidence: R1, R13-R15, R23-R25.]

10.1 Keep decoupling, stop copying wide payload everywhere
---------------------------------------------------------

RSP_DISPATCH's raw FIFO and separate class queues are a useful separation of
cache work from slow filtering. Its admitted head-of-line limitation is real:
a full class at the raw FIFO head can delay other classes behind it. That is
acceptable initially if measured against the actual mix, not hidden.

The current implementation copies raw data into a raw FIFO and then class FIFOs.
A production option is one RAM-backed raw-result store with token/reference
queues into each class. Use registered front entries, a clear owner for each
payload slot, and a release on decode consumption. Do not create a multi-read RAM
that all three classes must read simultaneously without providing banking or
arbitration. The shared cache produces at most one new raw sample per clock;
capacity must be calculated for that supply and actual class read demands.

Prefer the smallest change that meets timing/area after the functional paths are
connected. The dispatcher is not the dominant measured setup limiter, so it is
not the first block to rewrite for aesthetic reasons.

The current unknown-class drop behavior must be replaced or constrained by an
enforced impossible-input contract. Otherwise it strands the parent sample.
A defined error completion is preferable to silently losing a transaction.

10.2 Direct-nearest path
-----------------------

Decode the precise requested texel/subword using SampleMetadata. Preserve the
reference expansion, alpha and format semantics. 'RGB565 expansion' is not a
license to choose between bit replication and zero fill by taste; call the
existing oracle in the differential and implement its exact rule.

Return a complete SampleResult. The current near_ready=1 with unused outputs is
not an implemented nearest path. Add a nearest-only scene whose expected colours
cannot be reproduced by the bilinear or base-colour bypass.

10.3 Palette lookup and residency
---------------------------------

Use the palette slot and generation from the binding. They are not bits of the
fragment/sample generation. Preserve separate identities through lookup:

  lookup request = {palette slot, palette generation, index, SampleTag}
  lookup result  = {colour, resident/stale/error verdict, SampleTag}

The existing palette has an explicit BEGIN/WRITE/END protocol, completeness
tracking and CRC verdict. Keep those checks. The island bench must actually
perform a legal complete load; issuing WRITE operations alone is not evidence
that a resident palette exists.

The current implementation invalidates a slot while loading. The earlier
architecture proposes inactive-bank loading followed by atomic commit. Do not
mix their guarantees. For this recovery, either pin and forbid reload of active
frame palettes, retaining the tested loader, or implement the approved banked
commit as a separately tested step. In both cases no accepted sample may read
new bytes under an old binding without a defined stale result.

The lookup output is not backpressurable. Reserve completion storage before
issuing the read. Carry SampleTag through the same latency as colour/status.
Do not let a valid pulse vanish while another decode class owns the sample
commit port. Test concurrent load/lookup boundaries, missing entries, duplicate
writes, CRC failure, stale generation and generation wrap/flush constraints.

10.4 Full-colour bilinear completion
------------------------------------

Retain zhao_texture_bilerp_lane's exact arithmetic as the starting point:

  exact horizontal intermediates;
  vertical accumulation;
  ONE final rounding, not rounded horizontal colours fed to a second filter.

The existing lane can accept one channel job per clock and contains the two
horizontal products plus the vertical product. Its top-level integration is what
fails to supply complete colour work.

A footprint record holds four decoded texels, fu/fv, format/alpha policy and
SampleTag. A small channel sequencer emits the required channels. Each job has
{SampleTag, channel}; each result updates exactly that channel. A required/arrived
channel mask schedules ONE completed SampleResult. No partial colour is exposed.

Do not assume alpha always requires filtering, and do not assume it never does.
Use the supported format's rule. CLUT filtering, when legal, needs palette-resolved
colours for the taps, not interpolation of palette index numbers. Keep illegal
format/filter combinations explicitly rejected rather than inventing semantics.

Initially retain the current one-channel-per-clock lane to avoid changing both
functionality and the service rate at once. The older architecture's one-DSP,
II=3 channel engine remains a possible resource variant only after full-colour
channel demand is measured. Do not charge an II=1 rate with an II=3 resource fit.

A pipelined multiply/rounding split may help a future limiting filter path, but
keep exact intermediate widths. Use output credits to remove long combinational
ready chains when connecting it to the rest of the island.

10.5 One completion merger, no ambiguous OR
--------------------------------------------

Nearest, palette and bilinear have separate stable output skids/FIFOs. A small
fair arbiter chooses one complete SampleResult each clock. Only that producer
receives ready. Identity and colour/status are selected together.

Two-entry skids are a starting configuration, not a proof for all burst patterns.
Reserve output capacity for nonstallable producers. Under sustained convergence,
backpressure propagates to class admission, not to already-launched palette
reads that have nowhere to put results. The single result-bank write port is
safe only because this merger enforces one commit per clock.

11. COMBINER: FIX INSTRUMENTATION FIRST, THEN MEASURED STRUCTURE
===============================================================

[Evidence: R16,R19; N3-N6. The proposed lane/event implementation is not fitted.]

11.1 What the new evidence changes
----------------------------------

The old 'two DSPs' result was not an acceptance of COMBINE.V1. The completed
older-source leaf fit now reports 1,994 ALMs, 1,383 fitted registers, two DSPs,
zero RAM and 29.74 MHz. Its stated preference condition was <=800 ALMs and
>=125 MHz; it fails those conditions. The two-DSP ceiling alone is insufficient.

Do not conclude that an exact material combiner intrinsically needs a 33.6 ns
cycle. The implementation contains a dynamic per-recipe accumulation inside a
nested scheduler loop, and that increment was wider than its reachable range.
The landed two-bit correction directly targets this structure.

Do not conclude that the correction alone achieves 125 MHz either. The old
composed island contains separate arithmetic/writeback and scheduling violations,
which can become limiting after the counter cone is shortened. Those paths and
the functional ownership defects remain independent obligations.

Keep the old II=1 combiner as a reference/comparison target. Do not delete an
alternative before a replacement wins the relevant correctness, capacity and
physical comparison. Do not adopt the eight-DSP alternative silently either.

11.2 Retain and validate the landed two-bit increment correction
---------------------------------------------------------------

For every recipe r, the per-cycle increment is in {0,1,2} because the block
issues at most two lane jobs per cycle. Two unsigned bits suffice; one does not.
The accumulated job total remains 32 bits and retains the existing wrap/saturate
contract. The source uses ordinary accumulated totals; do not change counter
semantics as part of a timing patch without updating the explicit contract.

The proof obligation belongs at the grants, not at the narrowed value:
  total distinct product launches in a clock <= 2;
  each launched job maps to exactly one recipe;
  each launch is counted once;
  a not-yet-launched or retried descriptor is not counted as new arithmetic;
  sum(per-recipe increments) equals actual launches.

A two-bit temporary would wrap if a future broken scheduler issued four jobs.
Therefore assert the original grant bound independently. Do not let narrower
instrumentation conceal the violation it is supposed to observe.

The repository reports its 16-check material differential still passing after
this patch. That supports functional equivalence on those tests. It is not a
post-fix frequency measurement, a complete formal proof, or an island proof.

11.3 Preferred final counter topology: count the final two events
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Do not accumulate a dynamic array of 32-bit values while discovering work.
First decide which jobs actually launch. Their registered metadata includes
recipe. Then count those two events independently of the search topology:

  event0 = {fire0, recipe0}
  event1 = {fire1, recipe1}

  for each statically generated recipe k:
    delta_c[k] = zero_extend(fire0 && recipe0 == k)
               + zero_extend(fire1 && recipe1 == k);  // unsigned two-bit sum

    delta_q[k] <= delta_c[k];                        // optional timing cut
    total[k]   <= total[k] + zero_extend(delta_q[k]);

This is a structural sketch, not drop-in compiled RTL. Use widths/casts supported
by Quartus 17.0.2. The recipe decoder is parallel; each total has ONE full-width
increment. Counter feedback does not participate in job readiness or record
selection. No per-job variable-index increment remains in the scheduler cone.

Use the actual multiply-launch fire if the counter's contract is 'product jobs
issued'. A reservation into an operand stage is not necessarily a product launch
when cancellation can occur between them. Keep an additional descriptor-issued
counter only if useful and name it differently.

Registering the small event/delta separates scheduling from the wide total add.
It delays public visibility by one cycle, so define a drain/snapshot barrier that
includes pending counter events. A frame snapshot cannot miss the final launches.
Reset, abort and accounting epochs need the same explicit drain/cancel rule.
The count remains exact; telemetry latency is not permission to drop events.

Keep the important counters. They found real duplicate/missing-work problems.
The correct response to expensive instrumentation is bounded event aggregation
and a sane readout interface, not deleting the observability that exposed bugs.

11.4 Measure the small fix before selecting the larger rewrite
--------------------------------------------------------------

Experiment C0: reproduce the historical leaf's source, boundary and settings.
Experiment C1: same structure with only the landed two-bit correction.
Experiment C2: final-two-event counting with a registered small delta if C1 still
shows a counter/control feedback problem.

Use equivalent ports or an explicitly accounted wrapper. Record raw fitted and
synthesis counts separately. The report mentions 744 synthesis registers versus
1,383 fitted registers with 596 virtual pins: neither number can be substituted
for the other, and their difference is not automatically all scheduler state.
Use resource-by-entity and endpoint evidence before attributing the area.

For each experiment, run exact numerical/job tests, inspect the new top path,
and report ALMs, registers, mapped DSPs, memory, II and held-output stability.
If C1 removes the critical family and meets its gates, do not implement C2 merely
because a diagram looks cleaner. Conversely, a faster counter is not a reason
to leave a failing scheduler or material-writeback path untouched.

The comparable timing run needs a trustworthy both-endpoint classifier. Keep
the committed RTL-port membership repair, which now handles flat Decoder/Add
starts, and extend its scope rather than treating an internal START as a fully
internal register-to-register path.
Retain the full critical paths or a hash-addressed artifact bundle, not only a
summary with an untested label.

11.5 Keep every recipe's exact arithmetic and job law
----------------------------------------------------

  PASSTHRU          0 products
  MODULATE          4 products: RGB and alpha
  MODULATE2X        4 products
  LERP              4 products: RGB and alpha
  ADD_SAT           0 products
  MASK              0 products under the current select semantics
  DETAIL_LIGHT      6 products: three first-layer and three dependent second-layer
  DETAIL_MASK       4 products

The older three-product RGB-only budget omitted real alpha work for several
recipes. Use the implemented/reference law. A zero-sample base-colour return is
an explicit untextured case, not evidence that a textured recipe worked.

Preserve unit multiplication ((a*b)+128)>>8 and its existing clamp behavior;
do not substitute division by 255. MODULATE2X doubles after the existing rounding.
LERP rounds a magnitude and reapplies direction; it is not automatically a
signed-difference multiply rounded toward positive infinity. Preserve alpha,
refusal rules and per-fragment saturation-count semantics as well as RGB.

The scalar reference is necessary but not the transaction controller. Compute
expected colours from accepted records, not from the DUT's late metadata.

11.6 Structural repair when the measured next path requires it
--------------------------------------------------------------

Separate these timing responsibilities:
  grant/reserve a job;
  read/capture its operands and immutable recipe metadata;
  multiply;
  round/normalize the unit product;
  apply recipe-specific sign/add/double/select;
  write the channel result and release dependent work.

A function call inside a clocked writeback block does not create those registers.
Place explicit boundaries where timing requires them, carry identity through
all stages, and update busy/reserved state at the earliest irrevocable grant.
Completion is set only at result writeback; a deeper multiplier must not let a
job appear ready again while its earlier launch is still in flight.

Keep the initial record count small until measured feedback latency and throughput
justify more. Do not grow the current nested RECS x 7 scan from two to eight
records and hope memory inference makes it cheap. A bounded two-head arbiter or
queue selects a ready record; a local seven-bit dependency mask chooses its next
one or two jobs. Register the selection before wide operand access. Completion
events return a record to the ready queue only once, using an enqueued/reserved
bit to prevent duplicate ready entries.

A practical initial organization issues up to two eligible jobs from one captured
record. It reads one wide immutable snapshot, not two arbitrary record addresses.
The tradeoff is possible idle lane slots; measure useful launches per clock on
the declared recipe mix. Do not claim ideal two-lane utilization from the port
count. Add a second capture bank or another ready context only when the trace
shows a real bottleneck and the resource budget permits it.

If two records must issue in parallel, explicitly provide two operand read
addresses, for example replicated immutable RAM or true dual-port read scheduling.
Mutable intermediates belong in static channel banks or small working registers,
not an accidental multiport array. Intake initialization, arithmetic writeback
and retirement are distinct possible accesses and need a legal port schedule.

Initial job expansion and up to two simultaneous continuation events must have
real storage capacity and write bandwidth. Separate per-lane event skids can
serialize queue insertion. 'Continuations are enqueued' is not an implementation
if two producers overwrite the same pointer or one RAM port is asked for three
unrelated writes.

11.7 Multiplier-style selection is a controlled comparison
---------------------------------------------------------

The present 'LOGIC2' name is not proof of zero DSP use. First inspect which
operators mapped to its reported two DSP blocks. Compare logic and explicit
vendor-DSP implementations with the SAME scheduler, arithmetic, pipeline and
observable outputs; otherwise a style comparison is confounded by control logic.

Maintain the two-DSP combiner ceiling during the recovery candidate comparison.
An explicit primitive/IP must be supported by the installed tool/device and have
its mapped count demonstrated. A multstyle attribute is a request, not physical
evidence. Do not speculate that a packed mode fits until its synthesis probe does.

The current island using 17 of 112 DSPs does not mean the other 95 are available
to this block: the complete selected console has other demands. A proposed DSP
budget change requires that complete budget and a measured Pareto comparison.
It is an explicit owner decision, not a repair of a broken report threshold.

11.8 Completion, output stability and parent lifetime
-----------------------------------------------------

The combiner may finish fast bypasses before older product recipes. Return a
complete tagged final-result packet to central FRAGROB, not directly to the
external raster consumer. Only central final retirement enforces external order.

Select an output once and hold the entire packet until accepted. A newly
completed lower-numbered record must not replace a higher-numbered record
already offered under valid while ready is low. This needs a locked selection
or output FIFO, independent of arithmetic correctness.

The parent slot is not freed when a combiner record is accepted or completed.
It is freed only by final output acceptance after its result and context have
been committed. No tag may be recycled while an old arithmetic/event pipeline
can still write it. Same-cycle completion, output stall, abort and counter drain
belong in the acceptance tests.


12. AUX: KEEP THE MATH, REPAIR ADMISSION AND RETURN STORAGE
=========================================================

[Evidence: R1, R10, R17, R23.]

12.1 The divider is not the first thing to replace
-------------------------------------------------

The existing A0/A0b cut separates subtract/shift from clamp classification before
the six-bit restoring divider. That is a sensible prior improvement. Its exact
negative/saturated/degenerate coordinate law must remain unchanged.

The immediate defects are flow control and context association, not a need to
invent a new divider. A degenerate envelope still produces exactly one tagged
completion without a sheet read.

12.2 Credits before the fixed pipeline
--------------------------------------

Introduce an AUX descriptor queue and outstanding-credit count. A descriptor is
admitted only when storage is reserved for its eventual request/result. Track
all accepted work, including work still inside normalization/division.

A0 carries the actual world coordinate and envelope from the parent snapshot.
Metadata indexing must refer to the same token when the divider is issued and
when it returns. Retain the previous side-channel alignment fix.

At divider output, capture a complete sheet-request packet in a FIFO. If the
sheet interface is not ready, the packet stays valid and stable. Never overwrite
it with the next divider result. Credit availability ensures every already-issued
job can be captured even after admission stops.

12.3 Return convergence
-----------------------

Sheet responses and degenerate bypasses may arrive together. Either provide two
physically banked enqueue ports with proven queue capacity, or give each source
its own skid and merge one complete tagged AUX result per clock. The latter is
usually easier to reason about and avoids a two-write payload RAM.

The current eight-entry queue with no full check is not sufficient evidence.
A sixteen-entry candidate matches the initial maximum parent population, but
independent service capacities and flush policy still need to be proved. Safe
smaller admission limits are possible; measure their capacity impact.

Do not interpret {sheet tag, strength, zero} as an arbitrary RGB third texture
sample without proving that mapping against the material/reference contract.
AUX and primary sample 2 are distinct concepts. Capture AUX sideband separately
and apply it where the reference law applies it. The present unconditional
substitution must either be justified for the selected recipe or removed.

Acceptance includes long sheet stalls, simultaneous bypass/response, out-of-order
sheet returns if the interface permits them, output stalls, and exact matching
of world coordinates, envelope and parent identity.

13. THROUGHPUT, CAPACITY AND RESOURCE ACCOUNTING
===============================================

[Evidence: R8-R19; arithmetic under stated assumptions, not measurements.]

13.1 Count clocks and work separately
-------------------------------------

At 60 frames/second:

  frequency       raw clocks/frame       80% example usable clocks/frame
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
   69.05 MHz        1,150,833.33                   920,666.67
  100.00 MHz        1,666,666.67                 1,333,333.33
  105.00 MHz        1,750,000.00                 1,400,000.00

The 80% column is an illustrative service-time reserve matching the earlier
budget reasoning. It is not the same thing as 10% FPGA fabric reserve and must
not be subtracted a second time from an already-reserved workload budget.

RCP24's ideal one-lane four-job arithmetic bound at 100 MHz is 416,666.67
reciprocals/frame before reserve and 333,333.33 with that 80% allocation. At
69.05 MHz the corresponding figures are 287,708.33 and 230,166.67. Real throughput
is lower when scheduling, context limits or downstream stalls waste launches.

These numbers explain why clock matters, but they are NOT game-FPS predictions.
The actual post-Early-Z survivor count and all other stages determine capacity.

13.2 The serialized FRAGROB problem in comparable units
-------------------------------------------------------

For three samples per fragment:

  current maximum sample issue rate = 1/3 sample/clock
  sample issue work per fragment   = 9 clocks

For an illustrative 276,480-fragment three-sample workload, issue alone consumes:

  276,480 * 3 * 3 = 2,488,320 clocks

That is 149.3% of the raw 100 MHz frame, before misses or other stalls. This is
an analytical stress case, not a claim that every actual frame has that mix.

An overlapping one-sample/clock descriptor pipeline reduces that particular
issue term to 829,440 clocks, without changing any material or reducing fidelity.
The potential gain is in that service term, not a promised 3x whole-game speedup.

13.3 Full-colour filtering must enter the budget
------------------------------------------------

For an II=1 channel lane:

  J_filter = 3 * N_RGB_filtered_samples
             + N_additional_alpha_channel_jobs

At one channel per clock, J_filter is a lower bound on filter clocks. If the
one-DSP II=3 variant is chosen, the service term is approximately 3*J_filter,
plus pipeline fill/drain and stalls. Do not compare that variant's DSP count with
the II=1 variant's rate.

For the illustrative all-filtered three-sample workload above, RGB alone is
2,488,320 channel jobs. A single one-channel/clock lane cannot meet a raw 100 MHz
frame for that particular stress case. That does NOT prove the intended mixed
nearest/filter workload requires extra lanes. It proves that the mix must be
measured rather than inferred from the incomplete one-byte top.

The older architecture's ~180,000 channel-job workload is a specific model, not
an immutable universal property of terrain or future bump mapping. Keep the
selected v1 workload, but measure its actual classes, alpha work and recipes.

13.4 A useful first performance report
--------------------------------------

For every recorded workload, report:

  N_survivors; N_untextured; N_samples by count and format;
  N_nearest; N_palette; N_bilinear; N_channel_jobs;
  N_AUX and N_degenerate; recipe histogram and exact product histogram;
  RCP accepted/jobs/completed; UV pairs; cache accesses/line fills/replays;
  final fragments retired; total cycles; each queue's full/starved cycles;
  peak occupancy and maximum observed transaction lifetime.

Approximate stage-service lower bounds are:

  RCP             4*N_RCP / measured job-launch utilization
  perspective     N_perspective_pairs / pair issue rate
  descriptor      N_samples / descriptor issue rate
  cache           accesses plus the actual fill/replay model
  filtering       channel jobs / channel issue rate
  material        material products / (2 * product-lane utilization)
  final output    N_fragments / final-retire rate

Their maximum is a necessary lower bound, not the full execution time. Dependencies,
finite queues, memory arbitration, bursts and head-of-line stalls can make the
composition slower. Validate with the actual cycle trace, not only this maximum.

13.5 Resource plan
------------------

Retain the earlier 6,600-ALM objective / 7,500 hard island budget as the initial
comparison envelope, with the existing stated register and DSP limits recorded
separately. Do not waive a rule merely because the measured partial island is
small relative to the whole FPGA.

Likely sources of recovery, to be measured:

  - explicit RCP operand-width narrowing after exhaustive proof;
  - replacing PERSPUV's token tables/queues with a paired stream;
  - eliminating repeated wide fragment/footprint queues;
  - RAM-backed descriptors/results with legal physical ports;
  - small registered control rather than wide feedback and priority cones.

Likely new costs that must be INCLUDED:

  - complete ingress metadata and lifetime storage;
  - real binding/Mosaic consumption and full decode paths;
  - correct colour/channel collection and per-class completion skids;
  - final-result storage and ordered retirement after combination;
  - pipeline registers, credits and trustworthy status/counters.

The approved expectation of more M10Ks is reasonable: using 32-56 rather than
18 can be a good trade if it removes thousands of general registers and closes
timing. It is not a license to allocate a memory per bit or ignore port count.
Only a correctly connected production hierarchy provides the actual total.

There is a candidate DSP route without cutting filter rate: IF a new measured
RCP implementation reduces six DSPs to three, that would recover three, the difference between the old
17-DSP island and the earlier 14-DSP ceiling, all else equal. This is a candidate
budget reconciliation, NOT a prediction that synthesis will necessarily do it.

13.6 Counter capacity and instrumentation cost are part of the machine
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Count fragments and arithmetic jobs separately. A zero-product recipe still
consumes fragment storage, output bandwidth and possibly samples/AUX. Product
counters cannot replace fragment-identity accounting.

Telemetry must count at its declared event, absorb simultaneous launches without
lost updates, and expose a drain/snapshot barrier. Include its registers, adders,
readout and pipeline cuts in the resource report. Conversely, do not label all

A narrower public read port and simulation-only identity probes are appropriate,

port counts.

E. Validate palette setup with BEGIN, every required WRITE, END and successful
   residency/version checks. Require zero writes-outside-load and no use of a
   cold/stale colour. Exercise nearest, palette and bilinear as distinct paths.

F. Test both product lanes issuing the same recipe on the same cycle: delta=2,
   not 1. Then different recipes, one active lane, zero lanes, pipeline drain,
   abort, reset and the frame snapshot boundary. Prove launch count <=2 before
   trusting a two-bit delta. A valid counter patch must preserve exact totals.

G. Hold a completed output while a lower-index or bypass result becomes ready.
   Assert stable valid/tag/RGB/alpha/status until the original transfer completes.
   Separately assert final original-fragment order at the external boundary.

These are local regression obligations, not a new feature roadmap. Their failure
must make the relevant task red even while the old smoke test remains green.


15.1 First test: mixed per-fragment identity
-------------------------------------------

Write an island differential whose expected output is computed at INPUT
HANDSHAKE from an immutable software copy of that fragment and its resources.
Use deliberately different depths, signed numerators, base colours, recipes,
weights, bindings, LODs, palette generations and AUX coordinates.

After each input handshake, immediately change the input ports to a different
legal request or poison values while valid is low. This directly detects late
reads of live ports. Delay reciprocal completion with real service behavior;
introduce cache misses and output stalls so fixed-latency accidents cannot mask it.

Expected output is indexed by original transaction/retirement identity, not by
whatever recipe is live at output time. Assert:

  all N accepted; exactly N retired;
  every expected tag once, in the specified final order;
  exact RGB/alpha/status equality for every fragment;
  no unexpected refusal, stale, duplicate or dropped result;
  complete drain to zero live entries and empty queues.

The final timeout path is a failure even if some fragments retired. For the
existing 64-fragment case, assert submitted==64 AND retired==64. Printing those
numbers or using them only as a loop-break condition is not equivalent.

15.2 Material tests that defeat the current false pass
-------------------------------------------------------

Use base colours deliberately different from every expected texture result.
Include PASSTHRU, all two-sample recipes, DETAIL_LIGHT and DETAIL_MASK with
three distinct sample colours and variable alpha. Use weights 0, 1, 127, 128,
254 and 255, and source pairs that exercise increasing and decreasing LERP.

Assert exact product counts by recipe and saturation events as well as output.
Also swap two fragment recipes while preserving the aggregate histogram: the
identity differential must fail even though the per-recipe counters agree.

Include sample_count zero as an explicit separate expected base path. It must
not accidentally account for the success of a supposedly textured test.

15.3 Flow-control and memory tests
-----------------------------------

Exercise long and randomized stalls at every ready/valid boundary, including
sheet_ready and final out_ready. Change other unrelated inputs during stalls.
Assert offered packets remain stable. With one producer blocked, continue legal
work in another class to test convergence and fairness.

For each RAM reader, use distinguishable values at adjacent addresses and
alternate them every cycle. This catches the AUX one-cycle address/data mismatch
and the more general registered-address/capture-valid error.

For fixed pipelines, fill every reserved output slot, stop the consumer, allow
all in-flight results to arrive, and prove no overwrite. Then resume and require
exact drain. Test simultaneous arrivals from multiple producers and push/pop
coincidence. Match queue arithmetic to its physical write ports.

15.4 Token, reset and malformed-input tests
-------------------------------------------

Cover more than 256 allocations/reuses, slot wrap, generation transitions,
abort with work outstanding, stale responses after cancellation, and reset
release. Verify old work cannot match a recycled transaction.

Inject illegal sidx=3, valid but unrequested samples, duplicates, wrong generation,
unknown decode class, incomplete palettes and malformed binding modes. Every
case has a declared bounded outcome and exact fault count. No invalid response
is allowed to corrupt a live record or leave the machine waiting forever.

15.5 Arithmetic tests
----------------------

RCP24: exhaust all 24-bit denominators if feasible in the existing audit flow,
or exhaust the mathematically sufficient normalized domain plus prove the
normalization reduction. Check intermediate bounds and exact results, not only
final images. Include zero and every ROM-bin boundary.

Perspective: rails of signed numerators; all legal exponents; rounding half-cases;
positive/negative overflow; zero/error bypass; paired U/V identity. Test any
width-reduced multiplier against the existing full-width reference.

Filtering: four different texels, all extreme channel values, fu/fv boundary
values and random fractions; full RGB and applicable alpha. Preserve the exact
single-rounding bilerp result. Direct/CLUT format tests include subword selection.

Mosaic: negative world coordinates, period boundaries, weight 0/255, fold-only
mode, and source-level equality to the frozen hash and mirror laws.

AUX: envelope boundaries, degenerate envelopes, negative/saturated coordinates,
variable sheet latency and no-read degenerate completion.

15.6 Capacity tests and acceptance sensitivity
----------------------------------------------

Add warm-cache nearest, warm-cache filtered, three-sample terrain, mixed material,
AUX-heavy, palette-heavy, controlled miss, cache-index conflict and adversarial
stall workloads. Report each separately rather than hiding a bad case in a mean.

At least one test must demonstrate each optimization's intended sensitivity:

  disabling the descriptor pipeline returns the sample issue term toward II=3;
  breaking a token/context connection fails the identity differential;
  returning base colour fails textured cases;
  suppressing one RGB channel fails colour comparison;
  changing Mosaic's selected tile changes the expected address/result;
  dropping a completion stalls/fails exact drain;
  double-issuing a job fails counts even if the colour is unchanged.

Do not let the same implementation compute both the DUT result and the expected
result. Reuse the canonical scalar laws, but keep the transaction orchestration
of the scoreboard independent of the RTL's queues and scheduling.

Tests can inspect internal simulation-only signals or bind assertions without
adding hundreds of physical debug pins. The production counter interface should
be narrow and registered where possible. Changes to real counters still need
resource/timing measurement; simulation-only observability does not justify
rewriting historical fit claims.

STOP CONDITIONS
---------------
Do not resume unrelated hardware roadmap expansion while any acceptance-critical
condition below is unresolved:

  an accepted field changes ownership or is read from live ingress later;
  an offered output changes under stall;
  identity/colour/status/order or exact required work diverges;
  a claimed throughput omits channels, samples, misses or AUX obligations;
  a queue/RAM lacks the physical ports or credits its proof assumes;
  a slot is reused while stale child work can still reach it;
  a counter or endpoint classifier cannot be trusted;
  an allegedly passing fit uses different bytes or unjustified constraints;
  a required datapath is unobserved, constant-folded or replaced by a bypass;
  the selected legal workload exceeds its frame/resource budget.

Recording a failure honestly is necessary but does not make its gate green.
An implementation task is complete when its falsifiable acceptance conditions
pass, not when the remaining defect has a new paragraph explaining it.


17. WHAT NOT TO DO; WHAT IS GENUINELY PROMISING
==============================================

[Evidence: Assessment from the preceding source and timing evidence; not a timing guarantee.]

17.1 Do not convert uncertainty into either doom or reassurance
--------------------------------------------------------------
This review does not establish that 105 MHz is unattainable. It identifies why
the measured implementation fails. Conversely, a named critical path is not
proof that one added register will close every relevant path. At least four
families of expensive structures and several inter-block connections appear in
the archived failure list. The functional omissions also mean the corrected
machine must be remeasured with more real work, not less.

The encouraging fact is the nature of those failures: priority selection into
indexed storage, several operations combined in one cycle, backward cache-miss
feedback, and repeated storage/ownership machinery. Those structures offer
specific cuts and simplifications. This is more actionable than discovering
that the required arithmetic's irreducible operations exceed the device's total
capacity. The review has not found evidence of that latter impossibility.

17.2 The recommended intervention is structural but bounded
-----------------------------------------------------------
Retain the exact reciprocal law, format decoding, interpolation, material recipes,
Mosaic hash and AUX coordinate law. Keep useful existing implementations as
oracles or leaves. Replace the organization that combines them badly.

The intended intervention is not 'rewrite eleven arithmetic units'. First retain
and measure the small combiner counter correction and prove ingress ownership.
The larger production intervention is:

  - one correct transaction owner and final ordered-retirement mechanism;
  - a queue-driven reciprocal engine with honest pipeline boundaries;
  - a paired perspective stream instead of private reordering and axis queues;
  - overlapping descriptor and result reads rather than stop-and-wait FSMs;
  - a registered cache miss/classification boundary with accounted replay;
  - a short-stage material-job machine and tagged completion paths.

The other components mostly need correct metadata, buffering, credit and format
integration. They must still be measured: a source inspection cannot promise
that they will never become the next timing limit.

17.3 Rejected shortcuts
------------------------
Do not lower the product clock or cut terrain quality as the first response to
this implementation. Such a product change would require an explicit separate
decision backed by complete workload evidence.

Do not put each slow leaf on its own private clock. Clock-domain crossings add
protocol, buffering and verification costs and do not repair insufficient work
per second. A multi-clock architecture is an alternative architecture, not a
free timing waiver.

Do not replace exact arithmetic with approximate reciprocals or change rounding
because a smaller expression fits. The shared executable law is one of this
project's strongest assets.

Do not fix a queue bottleneck by unboundedly increasing its depth. Depth absorbs
bursts and latency; it does not raise a permanently insufficient service rate.
Nor should every register array be forced into RAM: small control bits with
independent updates belong in registers when the port analysis says so.

Do not repeat an unchanged high-effort fit indefinitely, substitute a favourable
seed for structural margin, or report a weaker constraint as a design speedup.
Preserve the original reference measurement and show the new tested configuration
beside it, with resource and throughput columns.

Do not freeze broken code to preserve a measurement. Preserve its immutable
revision and evidence, then fix it. Adding testbench-only scoreboards does not
require changing a production port list. A correct functional path matters more
than keeping a stale number 'current' by refusing to improve the design.

17.4 The first checkpoint that would materially reduce the risk
--------------------------------------------------------------
The first useful checkpoint is not a new full-console feature. It is an exact
mixed-recipe stream with verified accepted-to-consumed field identity, plus a
source-matched physical remeasurement of the narrowed-increment combiner. The
next major timing checkpoint is an isolated, queued RCP candidate that removes the measured scan/address cone
without losing required product throughput. That establishes both that latency
can safely change and that the leading timing problem responds to a targeted
architectural intervention.

Next, pass the paired perspective path and demonstrate overlapping descriptor
issue at the intended rate. Then refit the corrected composition and read the
NEXT worst paths. This sequence replaces a frightening headline with a series
of falsifiable claims. It does not promise the final result before it is measured.

Bottom line: there are enough concrete, repairable-looking structural mistakes
here to justify a serious recovery effort without sacrificing the console's
central capabilities. There is not enough evidence to call the recovery already
solved. Execute the gates, preserve the failures, and make the selected machine
rather than the roadmap checklist the unit of progress.

APPENDIX A. SMALL ARITHMETIC AND PROTOCOL CHECKS
===============================================

A1. Clock and area arithmetic
-----------------------------
  69.05 / 105                         = 0.657619
  shortfall relative to 105 MHz       = 34.2381%
  105 / 69.05 - 1                     = 52.0637% improvement required
  1000 / 69.05                        = 14.4823 ns equivalent period
  1000 / 105                          = 9.52381 ns target period
  7,720 - 7,500                       = 220 ALMs
  220 / 7,500                         = 2.93333%
  41,910 * 0.9                        = 37,719 usable ALMs
  41,910 * 0.1                        = 4,191 reserved ALMs

These are arithmetic transformations of the reported numbers. They are not
predictions of a new fit, nor a proof that the target period can be achieved.

A2. Material-job law and the mixed 64-fragment diagnostic
----------------------------------------------------------
The required jobs for one legal fragment of each recipe, from req_mask(), are:

  recipe          jobs     for eight fragments
  ------------------------------------------------
  PASSTHRU          0               0
  MODULATE          4              32
  MODULATE2X        4              32
  LERP              4              32
  ADD_SAT           0               0
  MASK              0               0
  DETAIL_LIGHT      6              48
  DETAIL_MASK       4              32
  ------------------------------------------------
  total                           176

The reported mixed histogram totals 140, with DETAIL_MASK alone accounting for
112 rather than 32. More than one counter moving proves only more than one job
class was exercised. Even an EXACT aggregate histogram is necessary but not
sufficient: swapping two fragments' recipes could leave it unchanged.

For the test's accepted inputs, assuming all 64 are accepted with no abort:
  retired fragments                 64, exactly once each
  source tags                       0x1000..0x103F, exactly in required order
  required primary sample results   192, unless an explicitly specified refusal
  fragments requesting AUX          22 (indices 0,3,...,63)
  nominal CLUT/bilinear split        32 fragments each, before channel expansion

Do not call 96 bilinear sample completions '96 RGB channel jobs'. Completing
full RGB filtering would require three colour-channel jobs per filtered RGB565
sample. Optional alpha work depends on the format's actual rule.

The production test must derive every expectation from the CAPTURED accepted
input packet, not reconstruct a packet from what input ports happen to show
when a later output appears.

A3. Minimal late-capture counterexample
---------------------------------------
  edge 0: accept A, tag A, numerator 0x00010000, recipe MODULATE.
  edge 1: accept or offer B, with a different numerator and recipe.
  later:  reciprocal(A) becomes valid while ingress ports show B or C.

The reviewed top wiring sends reciprocal(A) and LIVE numerator(B/C) together
into PERSPUV. Later, FRAGROB packs LIVE recipe(B/C/last) rather than recipe(A).
The retained fr_f_ctx connection repairs neither late read.

An even simpler test accepts A, then deasserts frag_valid_i and aggressively
changes all unaccepted ingress data. The implementation must ignore those values.
Changing the final output by changing an invalid input is a direct witness of
unowned live data. Preserve normal ready/valid rules while a VALID input is
stalled; poison only after transfer or while valid is low.

A4. Latency is not initiation interval
---------------------------------------
Current descriptor reader, continuously ready downstream:
  cycle n:   IDLE chooses descriptor A.
  cycle n+1: READ waits for/captures A.
  cycle n+2: HOLD transfers A.
  cycle n+3: IDLE chooses descriptor B.
  cycle n+5: HOLD transfers B.

The transfer spacing is three cycles. A correct overlapping pipeline could read
A at n, B at n+1 and C at n+2, and later deliver them on consecutive cycles.
Each item can still have several cycles of latency. The improvement comes from
overlap, not from pretending synchronous memory has zero latency.

With an 80% design-cycle allowance at 100 MHz/60 Hz:
  floor(0.8 * 100,000,000 / 60) = 1,333,333 cycles
  II=3 descriptor issue ceiling = 444,444 samples, before all other losses
  II=1 descriptor issue ceiling = 1,333,333 samples, before other bottlenecks

Those are ceilings, not delivered frame capacity. Cache misses, filter work,
combination, service bubbles and memory traffic still impose their own limits.

A5. Held-output counterexample
-------------------------------
An out-of-order service offers completed slot 1 with valid=1 while ready=0.
Before the consumer accepts it, slot 0 completes. A combinational lowest-slot
selector now offers slot 0 under the same held valid. The consumer did not
request that change. A stable output register or locked selected slot prevents
it. Final frame ordering is a separate obligation enforced by central FRAGROB.

A6. Credit invariant for an unstalled arithmetic pipeline
--------------------------------------------------------
Let Q be the reserved response capacity. Let outstanding include EVERY accepted
job whose reserved capacity has not been released, whether still computing,
waiting on memory or already in the result FIFO. Admission requires:

  outstanding < Q
  next_outstanding = outstanding + accepted - final_output_consumed

Each admitted job reserves one eventual result slot. The response queue cannot
overflow even if the consumer stalls indefinitely, provided accepted work cannot
produce more results than were reserved and no other producer steals that space.
The pipeline eventually stops ACCEPTING jobs, not necessarily mid-computation.
Latency and burst timing determine utilization and sensible Q, not the validity
of this reservation invariant. Multiple result producers need enough physical
write bandwidth or separately credited holding slots and arbitration.

A7. RAM-port accounting
-----------------------
A single true dual-port block can access two independent addresses per clock.
It may read and write at either address, subject to the device's supported
read-during-write behavior. It does not automatically supply independent addresses
for acceptance initialization, arithmetic writeback, and retirement read.

For PERSPUV result storage, remove the acceptance-time zero write for nonzero
jobs by carrying a validity/zero case, or redesign as a stream. Splitting U/V
arrays alone does not remove that extra write per axis or the unregistered
retirement read. A real inference report is the acceptance criterion.


A8. Why the seven-missing-fragment deduction does not follow
------------------------------------------------------------
Observed nonzero jobs imply 4/4 + 24/6 + 112/4 = 33 product-bearing executions
if those executions completed normally and the counters/law are correct. It is
not valid to add the intended 24 bypasses once recipe association is broken.
For example, 33 product-bearing plus 31 bypass executions would total 64 and
produce no contradiction in the zero-product counters. This is an illustrative
compatible count, not a claim that 31 bypasses actually occurred.

Identity logging must distinguish missing fragments, duplicate retirements,
wrong recipes and errors. Only it can close the transaction ledger.

A9. Fixed inputs do not exclude every transition-dependent mechanism
--------------------------------------------------------------------
For a hypothetical bug output_recipe = current_recipe OR previous_recipe:
  constant 1: 1 OR 1 = 1
  constant 2: 2 OR 2 = 2
  constant 6: 6 OR 6 = 6
  changing 1 -> 2: 1 OR 2 = 3

Thus the repository's fixed-recipe controls are useful but not a proof against
ALL OR-like transition bugs. This brief does not claim such a bug exists.
The source-demonstrable unowned late reads are the priority because they are a
specific mechanism and have a minimal legal-input counterexample.

A10. Two final launch events need only two increment bits
---------------------------------------------------------
Each of two lanes is either inactive or launches one of eight recipes: nine
possibilities per lane, 81 pairs total. For each recipe k:
  increment[k] = (lane0_active AND recipe0=k)
               + (lane1_active AND recipe1=k).
It is 0, 1 or 2. Summing all eight increments gives 0, 1 or 2 actual launches.

An exhaustive Python enumeration of these 81 abstract event pairs was performed
while preparing this revision; it checked that bound and count conservation.
The 64-fragment expected recipe mask total, 176 jobs, was also recalculated.
These are arithmetic/model checks, NOT execution of the RTL or a timing test.

A11. New leaf-frequency arithmetic is not a system prediction
--------------------------------------------------------------
  10 ns - (-23.624 ns) = 33.624 ns
  1000 / 33.624       = approximately 29.7407 MHz
  10 ns - (-3.630 ns) = 13.630 ns
  1000 / 13.630       = approximately 73.3676 MHz

The first is a historical flat combiner's reported path relationship; the second
is an internal path in the old island. Their different sources, topologies and
boundaries prevent treating them as successive whole-console clock readings.
Neither is a prediction of the increment-fixed or ownership-corrected design.
