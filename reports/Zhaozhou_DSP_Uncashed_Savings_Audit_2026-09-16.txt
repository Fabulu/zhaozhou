MAIN FINDING
============
173 DSPs is not an estimate of the completed rescue architecture. It mixes old
receipts for still-selected roots with zero contribution from unpriced roots.
It retains both old projectors, old pose/cull prices and the original bake. It
does not incorporate G8A/G8B as the production composition.

The four largest substitutions identified here cover 72–75 DSPs of reductions
against their historical scopes. Only the combined G8B endpoint is currently
physically priced; pose/cull/bake endpoints remain structural and require their
applicable gates and adoption. Adding the currently omitted raster/texture
scope back produces approximately 119–122 DSP for an incomplete, mixed-evidence
planning scope, NOT a completed-console estimate.

There is also a substantial further packing portfolio. Its declared preferred
endpoints amount to roughly another 30 DSP under the stated baselines, before
optional ATTR1 and the unverified current TESS-site saving. These are proposed
implementations, not savings that can be banked now. Complete FIELD and other
missing functions must still enter as positive costs.

2. LARGE IMPLEMENTED OR PARTLY MEASURED REPLACEMENTS
==================================================
A. Old geometry projector + terrain projector + tessellator: 33+33+6=72.
   New G8B @g8b-t1b: 34 DSP for its complete wrapper/subsystem, including the
   shared projector, terrain tessellation and arena machinery.
   Historical-scope bridge: 72 -> 34, difference 38.
   This avoids inventing a separately measured 24-DSP projector subtree.
   It is not a matched composed A/B saving. G8B timing remains red at 73.59 MHz.
   Original raw T1b fitter summary also reports 18 VIRTUAL pins and 0 physical
   pins; this must not be described as a physical-board/zero-virtual-pin result.
   Earlier default/old projector prices are not additional hardware to retain
   after one shared service is actually adopted.

B. Pose: 18 -> 4 structural, difference 14.
   Current source defaults MUL_LANES_QUAT=1, MUL_LANES_MAT=1: one 16x16 and one
   32x32 lane. A 32-bone palette takes 3,694 measured RTL cycles; 128 palettes
   imply 472,832 cycles, about 28.4% of a 100 MHz/60 Hz raw frame.
   The current DSP endpoint is NOT independently fitted. The old 18-DSP map
   remains in the score. Do not reuse the withdrawn '17-DSP pose saving'.

C. Culling: 15 -> 6 structural, difference 9.
   Current default is two 33x33 lanes, not the old four evaluation lanes plus
   private extraction multiplier. Functional tests cover the alternatives.
   Two-lane II is 22. Whole-workload qualification and current fit remain owed;
   the roadmap explicitly notes a disputed workload row.

D. Bake: 17 -> 3–6 structural, difference 11–14.
   zhao_terrain_bake_v2 exists, using one state-muxed signed34x34 multiplier
   and a RAM-oriented meets plane. The manifest retains v1 until the named
   terrain gate passes. It is NOT in the current G8B projection wrapper.
   Do not count both this collapse and a later packed 17-to-2 as independent
   savings: the latter is only the residual 3–6-to-2 improvement.

Conservative subtotal: 38 + 14 + 9 + 11 = 72.
Aggressive bake endpoint: 38 + 14 + 9 + 14 = 75.
The portfolio is not banked; its evidence stages differ.

5. MEMORY-FIRST WORK IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY DONE BY NATIVE DSP PACKING
==================================================================
R1 reusable quarter-square, coefficient-table and full-width hybrid primitives;
R3 selected nine-DSP projection backend; R4 colour/fog memory replacements;
R5 normal-transform/square/root/provider consolidation; R6 coefficient-memory
attributes; and R7 zero-DSP LOD/separable bake remain partly or wholly open in
the roadmap. These are further architectural routes, not banked savings.
The nine-DSP projector is an alternative endpoint to the dual18 eleven-DSP
projector. Never subtract both full reductions from the same baseline.

6. PROOF AND ADOPTION STILL MATTER
=================================
The clean dual18 MapOnly/CDB packet proves one MAC resource has two distinct
live lane routes. Its report explicitly leaves broad encrypted vendor arithmetic
validation on HOLD. Later BIL2 and G8A fits establish their selected physical
DSP counts, not blanket proof of every signed/mixed wide-product construction.

The current frontier is therefore not 'all targets implemented, just update a
spreadsheet'. It contains physically measured candidate endpoints, implemented
but unfitted leaves, deliberate unadopted replacements and genuinely unbuilt
packing/memory endpoints.

Recommended reconciliation, within the existing roadmap:
* Retain historical receipt prices, but label their source/profile and staleness.
* Separately track exact current candidate endpoints and selected adoption.
* Represent a shared physical owner once, with all actual callers.
* Expose required unpriced functions as unknown, not absent or free.
* Price pose/cull/bake at existing suitable subsystem gates and adopt atomically.
* Keep the current G8B timing work intact; packing may change ALM and timing.
* Do not let a late 'CURRENT' label on a selected old ledger row substitute for
  source-closure matching or a composed result.

CONCLUSION
==========
There is no demonstrated post-rescue 173-DSP machine. Large identified savings
have not been cashed into the selected composition or its scoreboard. That is a
materially better starting position than a fully optimized 173-DSP total, but
neither partial arithmetic nor target subtraction proves the complete console
fits. Finish and qualify the known replacements, then account for every missing
physical owner before deciding what capability must be cut.
