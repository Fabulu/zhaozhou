    registered job stimulus
             |
             v

             |
             | full triangle: ax/ay/az, bx/by/bz, cx/cy/cz; source id

The normal consumer is permanently ready in this wrapper. The tessellator is

A replacement which assumes that every job is exactly two square-cell triangles

4. THE CURRENT TESSELLATOR: CONTRACT THAT MUST SURVIVE THE REWRITE
===============================================================================

4.1 Job, geometry and surface ownership

One job identifies one 8x8-cell subpatch of one surface. Job origins are multiples
of eight in 0..24 inside a 32x32-cell patch. Coordinates on its lattice are six
bits. Own and neighboring LOD levels are two bits, with strides 1, 2, 4 or 8.
The morph factor is 17-bit Q16, clamped above 65536. Source identifiers on this
mesh interface are 16 bits, not the 32-bit source field in some command ABIs.
The surface bit distinguishes top from underside. [S06, S07]

Plain-cell emission uses the fixed i00-i11 diagonal:

    top triangle 0: (i00, i11, i10)
    top triangle 1: (i00, i01, i11)

The underside swaps b and c. Preserve the exact triangle order, not merely the
same undirected triangle set. Normal orientation and deterministic traces depend
on it. World x and z arrive with the lattice response; do not silently replace
those placed coordinates with an unrelated origin-plus-step calculation.

4.2 Stitching is topology, not cosmetic vertex snapping

Each edge uses max(own level, neighbor level), hence the coarser shared stride.
With no coarser neighbor, the enumerator walks ordinary run-cells. Otherwise it
emits the inner rectangle followed by an annulus whose outer segments fan onto
the inner ring. At own level 2 beside level 3, the inner rectangle collapses to
its center. Own level 3 cannot have a still-coarser neighbor. [S06, S08]

Do not replace the annulus with independently patched side strips or snapped
boundary vertices. Those alternatives change corner cases and shared vertex
sets. In particular, all 1,024 combinations of own level and four neighbor
levels must continue to agree with the oracle.

4.3 Solidity and empty work

Dual pages read the 64 subpatch cell states through the fixed one-cycle response
port. An ordinary run-cell emits only if every covered base cell is SOLID.
A stitched job containing any non-SOLID cell is rejected before triangle
emission. Precisely, the implementation's reject condition is j_stitch together
with a non-all-solid mask; it is not simply own_level != 0. [S06]

A legacy page has no cell-state scan and is treated as solid. It can still be
stitched. A legacy underside job emits nothing. Preserve clamp counters and
empty-job behavior as well as the emitted geometry.

4.4 Geomorph

Only vertices strictly inside the subpatch morph. Boundary vertices stay at the
lattice height even when the adjoining subpatch has a different morph factor.
A coarse vertex requires one lattice read. A vertex requiring geomorph adds two
coarse-parent reads. Therefore a triangle requires three to nine lattice reads.
Unmodified lattice heights are passed through verbatim. [S06, S07]

The current contract reports 456 cycles for a dual, unmorphed level-0 subpatch
with 128 triangles and 936 cycles at half morph. These are recorded existing
contract measurements, not timings re-run in this review and not guarantees for
the proposed pipeline. The rewrite must publish new end-to-end cycle counts,
including scans, setup, stalls and drain. [S07]


5. THE CURRENT NORMAL GENERATOR: EXACT ARITHMETIC AND REAL SCHEDULING
===============================================================================

For a triangle (a,b,c), compute e1=b-a and e2=c-a with signed 33-bit differences.
The six signed products are 66 bits; the three differences are carried in
signed 67 bits:

    Nx = e1y*e2z - e1z*e2y
    Ny = e1z*e2x - e1x*e2z
    Nz = e1x*e2y - e1y*e2x

Each output lane is:

    sat32((N + 32768) arithmetic_shift_right 16)

The bias is positive for BOTH signs. It is not sign-dependent round-away-from-
zero and it is not truncation. The normal is an unnormalized Q16.16 face normal,
not a unit vector and not an averaged per-vertex normal. Degeneracy is decided
AFTER rounding and saturation: all three emitted lanes must be zero. [S04]

The inspected implementation already shares one multiplier across the six
products, and already registers its product before the subtractors. Source
comments describing an old two-cycle pipeline or equating its latency and
initiation interval are not a reliable cycle specification.

A source-level edge schedule of the inspected implementation is:

    edge 0: accept triangle, latch six edges
    edge 1: capture product 0
    edge 2: consume product 0; capture product 1
    edge 3: consume product 1; capture product 2
    edge 4: consume product 2; capture product 3
    edge 5: consume product 3; capture product 4
    edge 6: consume product 4; capture product 5; finish issuing
    edge 7: consume product 5; publish full-width normal into s1
    edge 8: move s1 through rescale into output s2
    edge 9: earliest next input acceptance; earliest output transfer

The reason for the acceptance gap is tri_ready_o = !m_busy && !s1_valid.
At edge 8, pre-edge s1_valid is still true, so a replacement input cannot be
accepted on the same edge that s1 drains. The independently executed sequential
Python model reproduced accept edges 0,9,18,27,36,45. Thus the no-stall source-
level initiation interval is nine cycles, not the comment's eight. The output
becomes valid after edge 8; distinguishing that from a transfer at edge 9 avoids
an off-by-one argument about latency. [S04; Appendix A]

This observation is not a Verilator measurement. Add an RTL assertion/test that
records the same edge events before changing the schedule.


6. DIAGNOSIS: THREE PLAUSIBLE TIMING LIMITS, ONLY TWO ARE GEOMETRY
===============================================================================

6.1 The measured-version normal multiply/accumulate cone

At the measured revision the path shape in RTL was:

    latched edge -> six-way operand select -> 33x33 multiplication
        -> sign extension -> 67-bit subtraction -> accumulator/result register

That is an obvious pipeline-boundary problem. The current m_p_q change attacks
it directly. It must not be offered as a newly discovered unimplemented fix.
Nevertheless, the source comment diagnosing this as the exact cause of 31.10 is
a hypothesis without the matching raw path report, as the comment itself
acknowledges. It is not evidence that the other half of the pair was fast.

6.2 The still-present tessellation geomorph cone

This one is visible in the inspected source. On the parent-B response, the
combinational chain is: [S06]

    lat_h_i (parent B) minus v_ha (parent A), 34-bit difference
      -> rounded divide by two and saturation, m_half
      -> saturating add of parent A, m_hc
      -> subtract original vertex height, m_d
      -> multiply by job morph factor, m_prod (52-bit destination)
      -> rounded shift by sixteen and saturation, m_step
      -> saturating add of original height, m_y
      -> vertex register OR directly the final emitted triangle register

The last-slot bypass sends m_y directly into the output packet on pend_last.
There is no pipeline register separating these arithmetic operations. Registering
NORMALS' product does nothing to this chain. This is the main remaining
arithmetic target of the rearchitecture.

The correct claim is that this is a concretely observed long arithmetic cone,
not that this review has proven it is exactly the saved 31.10 critical path.

6.3 The characterization wrapper can also be the limiter

The hash sink has a feedback expression combining a XOR, a shift, several
additions, metadata and all three normal lanes. That can itself create a slow
register-to-register path. A core-to-core classifier alone would call it
internal, even though it is not geometry. [S03]

In addition, the synthetic lattice is written from raw stim_valid_i and stim_i,
not exclusively from the registered stimulus. Consequently the statement that
all input behavior is behind registered stimulus is incomplete. A path launched
from such a port must be classified correctly rather than assumed impossible.

Required diagnosis after refit: distinguish DUT-internal, DUT-to-DUT seam,
wrapper-only, boundary, reset recovery/removal, and clocking restrictions.
Finding a harness-limited path is not license to ignore an equally bad DUT path
immediately below it. Capture enough paths in each class.


7. THE PROPOSED ARCHITECTURE
===============================================================================

Keep the existing public TESS and NORMALS ports and arithmetic law. Rebuild the
interior around completed, explicitly owned triangles and registered arithmetic.

    Job registers and one-job lifecycle
              |
    Solidity scan -> registered 64/16/4/1 solidity hierarchy
              |
    Plain / inner / annulus enumerator
              |
    Registered triangle descriptor and registered segment setup
              |
    One-cycle lattice request/response front end
              |
    Two reserved triangle-assembly banks
          |                 ^
          | parent tuple    | completed morphed vertex
          v                 |
    One registered geomorph pipeline
              |
    Oldest completed bank -> registered triangle seam
              |
    Shared-product normal engine
      registered operand selection / existing registered product
      full-width accumulation / registered rescale boundary
              |
    Ready/valid normal output

This is one lane, not three separate morph multipliers and not six parallel
normal multipliers. It does not introduce a world cache, a giant reorder buffer,
another clock domain, an approximate reciprocal or a new rendering law.

The two assembly banks are the specific mechanism allowing additional arithmetic
latency without letting late responses overwrite the next triangle. An ordinary
FIFO pasted after the old pend_last logic would not solve that problem.

Implement in isolated steps and fit between them. The target shape is specified
here; the order of changes in section 16 determines which parts are justified by
the actual timing and cost results.


8. REBUILD THE GEOMORPH UNIT AS A TAGGED, REGISTERED PIPELINE
===============================================================================

8.1 Exact stages

Use the current helper semantics unchanged in the first implementation:

    M0  Capture {h, ha, hb, morph, bank, slot} at parent-B response handling.
        All later stages use this tuple, never live v_ha, pend_slot or j_morph
        unless their lifetime is explicitly proven to cover the whole token.

    M1  Compute and register d_ab = sign_extend(hb) - sign_extend(ha).
        Retain the existing signed 34-bit representation initially.

    M2  Compute and register half = sat32((d_ab + 1) >>> 1).

    M3  Compute and register hc = sat32(ha + half), with a wide signed sum.

    M4  Compute and register d = sign_extend(hc) - sign_extend(h).

    M5  Compute and register product = signed_nonnegative(morph) * d.
        Keep the existing 52-bit product representation for the first pass.
        The register follows the multiply, not its final saturating sum.

    M6  Compute and register step = sat32((product + 32768) >>> 16).

    M7  Compute and register y = sat32(h + step), then commit {bank,slot,y}.

Each stage has an accompanying valid bit and the bank/slot tag. Carry only the
fields still needed. Parent A dies after M3, the factor after M5, the original
height after M7. Do not carry nine coordinates through every arithmetic stage.

If M6 remains the measured critical path, split quotient rounding from saturation.
Do the same for normal rescale only when needed. These are legitimate additional
cycles; do not instead install a false path around the arithmetic.

The initial pipeline can be nonstalling internally: banks are reserved before
requests, and each completion has an assigned register destination. Admission
stops at the bank allocator. This avoids a chain of combinational ready signals
running backward through eight stages. It requires the completion-port contract
in section 9; it must not assume a single-write RAM can absorb two writes.

8.2 Remove the final-vertex combinational bypass

Delete the architectural dependence on:

    parent B arriving now -> m_y now -> final triangle output now

The final read being issued or returned does not mean the final vertex is ready
any more. All three completed vertex values must exist in the assembly bank
before the output seam is filled. Wait an additional cycle after the last write
rather than recreating a long bypass to save that cycle.

8.3 Do not simplify away observable intermediate saturations

The existing reference and RTL use saturation at the half-difference, coarse
height, rounded morph delta and final height. Those boundaries are observable
on the documented whole-word height domain. [S06, S07, S08]

Concrete counterexample, checked in Appendix A:

    ha = -2147483648; hb = 2147483647
    rounded half-difference = 2147483648 before saturation
    saturated half-difference = 2147483647
    current coarse_height = -1
    a single rounded (ha+hb)/2 would instead produce 0

Thus replacing the two-step coarse-height expression with a single averaged sum
changes behavior. Preserve it unless an explicit arithmetic-law change is
approved and propagated to every reference, test and content assumption.

There is a second documentation discrepancy that must be recorded:

    h = -2147483648; hc = 2147483647; morph = 65536
    the current reference/RTL rounded delta saturates to 2147483647
    final height = -1, NOT hc

Therefore the blanket endpoint statement 'full morph always gives hc' is not
true over the entire height word admitted by the contract. This is not merely
an alternative rounding preference: the existing reference itself performs the
intermediate narrow. The timing rewrite must preserve reference behavior while
opening a separate contract-correction issue. Do not silently fix the oracle
and call the changed geometry an optimization. [S07, S08; Appendix A]

8.4 Permissible later arithmetic reductions

A difference of two signed-32 heights needs 33 bits, not 34. Narrowing it may be
possible after proving every intermediate's range, but first establish a passing
staged implementation. Treat the morph factor as nonnegative; 65536 must never
be reinterpreted as negative by a signed 17-bit cast. An 18-bit signed operand
with an explicit zero sign bit preserves the current representation.

For positive rounding bias, the exact integer identity is:

    (x + 2^(k-1)) >>> k = (x >>> k) + x[k-1]

This can move the rounding increment onto the retained quotient instead of a
full-width product word. Preserve signed extension, a carry guard, saturation
thresholds and the legal input range. A Python identity check is not permission
to rely on implicit SystemVerilog expression widths.

Do not merge the coarse interpolation rounding with the morph rounding. They
are two separate operations in the existing reference, not redundant work.


9. TRIANGLE OWNERSHIP, BACKPRESSURE AND MEMORY RESPONSES
===============================================================================

9.1 Reserve before requesting

Allocate one of two triangle banks before issuing that triangle's first lattice
request. A bank contains three vertices, a three-bit final-ready mask, source and
surface metadata, and enough issue/completion state to know it is still owned.
Keep only one triangle's address-fetch sequence active at a time initially;
completions for the previous bank may still be in the arithmetic pipeline.

A new bank is not allocated when both are occupied. This is the primary
backpressure point. Once a lattice request is issued, its fixed-latency response
is accepted regardless of a later consumer stall. The reservation guarantees a
home. No cancellation or variable-latency reinterpretation of the public memory
port is introduced.

The two banks need 2 * 3 * 3 * 32 = 576 coordinate/height payload bits, before
metadata and valid state. That is a logical storage count, not a prediction of
ALMs or a demand to build a wide, deeply buffered FIFO.

9.2 Tag every response by what was requested

The delayed request metadata must include bank, slot and read kind. A response
must not use the CURRENT enumerator slot or newly advanced descriptor.

    own vertex, no morph:
        store x/y/z and set that slot's final-ready bit.

    own vertex, morph required:
        store x/z and original h; do not mark final-ready.

    parent A:
        retain its height for the corresponding parent-B tuple.

    parent B:
        capture an independent M0 tuple, including original h and parent A.
        Mark final-ready only when the tagged M7 result commits.

The existing single parent-A temporary can be reused only if M0 captures its
value before it is overwritten. A bank/slot-indexed parent store is another
option, but it is not an excuse for a large context machine. State the lifetime
proof in the implementation and test it under adjacent morphed vertices.

9.3 Two completion events can occur in one cycle

A nonmorphed own-vertex response and an older morph completion may arrive on the
same cycle. Both must be committed. For register banks, use independent per-slot
writes and merge ready bits explicitly:

    ready_next[bank] = ready_old[bank]
                    OR direct_completion_mask[bank]
                    OR morph_completion_mask[bank]

Do not assign the same ready vector twice with nonblocking assignments and lose
one update. Assert that two writers never target the same bank/slot in one cycle.
There is one final result per vertex, so such a collision indicates bad ownership
or tagging, not a condition to resolve by priority.

A later RAM-backed implementation must supply the required write ports or an
explicit completion queue. The two-bank register implementation is preferred for
this first rearchitecture because the storage is small and the write behavior is
transparent.

9.4 Publish oldest-complete, not whichever finishes first

A later nonmorphed triangle can become complete before an older heavily morphed
one. Keep a two-entry allocation order, or equivalent head/tail state. Only the
oldest bank may publish, and only after its three final-ready bits are set.

The registered seam holds 288 coordinate bits plus 16 source bits and one surface
bit: 305 payload bits and valid state. Fill it only when it is empty or its
previous triangle is accepted. Release the bank only after ownership transfers
to this register. The seam's payload and valid must remain stable while stalled.

No bank-generation field is necessary for this first design if a bank is never
released with pending responses or morph tokens. Prove that invariant. Do not
add generation machinery to hide a lifecycle violation.

9.5 Reset and drain

Reset clears all valid/owned/ready state, delayed request tags and morph valids.
An old memory value appearing after reset is ignored because it has no valid
request tag. Payload reset is not itself a correctness requirement when valid
state proves the data cannot be observed; preserve the public zero-counter reset
semantics and check simulation X behavior.

Tessellation idle requires no active job, no pending memory response, no allocated
bank, no morph token and no seam packet. NORMALS adds its own busy and result
stages. 'The last request was issued' is not an idle condition.

Keep existing counter events explicit. The present tessellation counter increments
when it publishes a triangle into its output register, not on downstream
acceptance. Preserve that publication event at the new seam unless the contract
is deliberately revised. Do not count bank completion as a second emission.
The normal counter likewise counts publication into its rounded output stage.


10. NORMAL ENGINE: KEEP THE EXISTING REPAIR, COMPLETE ITS PIPELINING IF NEEDED
===============================================================================

10.1 Default implementation

Retain one shared 33x33 multiplier and the existing m_p_q product register. The
first refit decides whether the remaining operand mux plus multiplication is
actually a failing cone. If so, add explicit signed operand registers AFTER
selection and BEFORE the multiplication:

    captured edges
       -> registered {operand_a, operand_b, term_tag, valid}
       -> existing registered {product, term_tag, valid}
       -> 67-bit lane accumulation
       -> registered rounding/saturation stages
       -> normal output

Selecting six products does not mean instantiating six multiplication expressions
inside six case arms. There is still one multiply operator. Keep the data path
free of unnecessary busy gating; valid tags determine which products count.

Use the actual term tag on return, not the live issue counter. Keep the engine
busy until the final product is CONSUMED, not merely issued. Preserve the existing
last-lane behavior: publish acc2 minus the returning final product, not acc2's
pre-edge value. A shared explicitly named acc2_next expression can clarify this
without claiming the fitter currently duplicates its subtraction hardware.

If the normal rescale is critical, register a widened rounded quotient before
saturating to 32 bits. The degeneracy flag reads those exact saturated output
lanes. It must not trigger on the unrounded cross product or on an earlier copy
of the rounding expression.

10.2 Do not over-optimize admission first

The conservative one-triangle arithmetic engine has simple ownership and ample
room to add a clock of latency. Retain that discipline initially. Do not admit a
replacement triangle just because the final operand was issued; existing tags
and accumulators still belong to the old triangle.

If a later throughput requirement justifies accepting on the same cycle the
full-width result stage drains, design and test that as a separate change. The
present nine-cycle initiation interval is a starting measurement from the model,
not a target that justifies weakening result ownership.

With an added operand stage and otherwise the same conservative structure, the
natural no-stall issue interval gains a cycle. Record the actual new sequence
rather than copying old latency comments. The point is to trade a small number
of cycles for a shorter physical clock period, not to advertise one triangle per
clock from a six-operation shared multiplier.

10.3 Full precision and underside rounding

Do not narrow 33-bit edges to 32 or 27 to fit a convenient multiplier mode. Do not
replace 67-bit cross-product lanes with 64-bit intermediates. The existing
arithmetic deliberately preserves extreme signed-coordinate differences.

Do not derive an underside normal by negating an ALREADY ROUNDED top normal.
Positive-bias rounding is asymmetric at ties:

    rescale16(+32768) = 1
    rescale16(-32768) = 0
    -rescale16(+32768) = -1

Winding inversion must precede the cross product, as today, or an equivalent
negation must occur on the full-width raw lane before rounding. [S04; Appendix A]

The Cyclone V DSP documentation supports different multiplier modes according
to operand width; it does not guarantee that declaring a register in RTL places
it in the desired hard register. Inspect the mapped DSP modes and register use.
A source comment promising free fabric cost is not a fit report. [S11]


11. REMOVE THE TESSELLATOR'S CONTROL-PATH DEPTH WITHOUT CHANGING TOPOLOGY
===============================================================================

11.1 Replace repeated 64-cell range tests with a solidity hierarchy

The current cell_solid combinational loop tests all 64 base cells against bounds
derived from ea, eb and stride. Its int casts and general arithmetic leave a
large expression for synthesis to simplify. No claim is made here that Quartus
necessarily implements every source multiplication literally. [S06]

Build these registered masks after the scan:

    L0: 64 bits, one per base cell
    L1: 16 bits, each AND of an aligned 2x2 group in L0
    L2:  4 bits, each AND of an aligned 2x2 group in L1
    L3:  1 bit, AND of the four L2 bits

Total 85 logical bits, only 21 beyond the existing 64-cell mask. A run-cell's
solid flag becomes the selected bit at its own level and local ea/eb index.
The same selection works for the inner cells of the stitched enumerator.
For a legacy page initialize the masks to all-solid without a scan.

Constructing one registered reduction level per clock adds three bounded setup
cycles, not a variable per-triangle scan. j_stitch rejection still occurs before
emission and still examines whether the entire underlying subpatch is solid.

Appendix A compared this hierarchy against direct rectangular coverage for
4,098 masks and 348,330 run-cell queries. That checks the proposed algorithm,
not the eventual RTL decoder or cycle timing.

11.2 Register segment preparation and triangle descriptors

The current annulus path combines outer_v, proj_t, fan-range arithmetic, inner_v,
slot selection, morph-case selection and parent-address adjustment. These are
small-width operations, but their combinational composition can still be deep.
The fixed-latency lattice boundary does not make the address-generation cone
free. [S06]

Prepare a segment's two outer endpoints, projected inner-ring endpoints and fan
step count once per segment, in registered setup state. Hold them through that
segment's fan. Register the selected triangle's three (i,j) lattice coordinates
before the per-vertex fetch sequencer uses them. A descriptor needs only 36
coordinate bits, three morph-case fields and small metadata; it is not another
full world-coordinate packet.

Compute and register parent addresses from that stable descriptor when needed.
Do not add this stage blindly into the external response-latency definition:
lat_req still marks the actual request, and the datum still arrives one cycle
later. Additional cycles happen BEFORE issuing or AFTER capturing, internally.

Retain the current annulus enumeration as the oracle for these staged operations.
Avoid a large lookup ROM over all 1,024 level combinations. The calculations are
bounded and can be prepared incrementally. A small lookup for a specifically
measured residual cone is optional, not the initial architecture.

11.3 Retain issue order while separating issue from completion

The old enumerator advances on last-read ISSUE, not capture, to avoid a bubble.
The new descriptor allocator may similarly advance when all of a triangle's
requests are issued, provided the bank and every pending token retain the old
triangle's identity. It may not overwrite a descriptor still needed to issue
parent reads. Completion order is handled by bank readiness and allocation order,
not by stalling the entire arithmetic pipeline until the enumerator catches up.


12. FIX THE MEASUREMENT HARNESS WITHOUT HIDING THE DESIGN
===============================================================================

Preserve the historical wrapper unchanged as a baseline artifact. Add a separate
characterization variant with a stated, inspectable stimulus and sink contract.
Compare implementation changes under the same harness; compare harness changes
with the same DUT. Otherwise two unrelated improvements get credited to one fix.

The current wrapper's limitations are concrete: [S03]

    - A 64-entry synthetic height memory is addressed only by lat_vi, not vj.
      It is not an independent two-dimensional terrain field.
    - lat_surface does not select a second independent height plane.
    - Cell-state responses are permanently SOLID; void/reject logic may fold.
    - Job fields overlap within one 32-bit stimulus word.
    - job_morph is driven with a forced zero top bit, so 65536 and clamp-above-
      65536 cannot be independently stimulated through that connection.
    - The origin mask allows encodings outside the contract's 0..24 domain.
    - Output ready is permanently high.
    - The feedback hash has arithmetic depth unrelated to the geometry result.
    - Synthetic memory writes use raw stimulus inputs.

These limitations make optimization-away plausible; they do not quantify any
missing ALMs or DSPs. Do not resurrect the report's withdrawn stale-leaf-sum
argument as evidence of folding. [S02]

For the replacement physical harness:

A. Register every request-driving and memory-write-driving input. Provide
   independently programmable job fields, legal origins, full morph domain and
   both surface modes. Deliberately include separate malformed-domain tests in
   functional simulation; do not use accidental invalid inputs as the normal
   physical characterization workload.

B. Either use a representative initialized/programmed dual-plane lattice and
   cell-state memory, or a clearly labeled registered-response characterization
   source whose independent dynamic inputs preserve the full arithmetic and
   control logic. A synthetic source is not a functional terrain-memory test.

C. Make downstream ready dynamically exercisable behind a register. Functional
   tests must include both saturation and sustained backpressure; a free-running
   fit alone does not demonstrate handshake correctness.

D. Replace the long hash-add chain with shallow registered observation. For
   example, use separate local registered rotate/XOR accumulators and a later
   registered fold, rather than one expression with several serial additions.
   Include all lanes, source, surface and diagnostics. Check mapped observability;
   a hash is not automatically proof that no lane can be optimized away.

E. Keep synthetic memory and sink resource use separate by hierarchy. A full
   33x33 dual-height array has a very different storage cost from the old 64-word
   probe. Do not attribute that extra RAM to the new tessellator.

F. In functional simulation compare full triangle and normal packets against
   the oracle, not just hashes. Matching hashes are not equality proofs.

Report full clock constraints and setup/hold/recovery/removal results. A higher
Fmax summary alone is not sign-off. The vendor documentation explicitly treats
that summary as a benchmarking report and directs sign-off toward constraints
and slack reports. [S12]


13. WHAT THIS SHOULD COST, AND WHAT IS NOT YET KNOWN
===============================================================================

Known historical total: 1,523 ALMs, 1,389 registers, 9 DSP blocks and one 2,048-bit
synthetic RAM. That is the old pair including its harness, not a fresh estimate
for the proposed design. [S01]

The new architecture intentionally spends registers to shorten logic cones.
The biggest explicit storage additions are the extra assembly bank, carried
morph operands/tags and, if needed, selected normal operands and a rounding
boundary. Counteracting that cost are the removal of last-slot bypass logic,
shared segment setup and the simpler solidity selector. Whether these save
ALMs after placement must be measured; the source cannot settle the balance.

Proposed working guardrails, to be ratified against the project's actual budget:

    Clock: 100 MHz must pass real setup/hold and relevant reset constraints.
    DSP:   pipelining alone should not add multiplication operators. Any increase
           over a matched baseline requires per-entity attribution and approval.
    ALM:   initially flag a matched-wrapper increase above 600 ALMs for review.
    FF:    initially flag a matched-wrapper increase above 1,200 registers.
    RAM:   no full vertex cache or new world-memory subsystem in this task.
    Rate:  publish actual accepted and retired triangles/cycle, not stage count.

Those ALM/FF flags are engineering review thresholds, NOT existing owner rules,
measured costs or a promise to fit within them. The live budget remains
controlling. Compare against a current-source refit under the SAME wrapper,
not against an old leaf row and not across different synthetic memories.

A normal engine needing roughly ten clocks per triangle at 100 MHz would have
an arithmetic capacity around ten million triangles per second before memory,
tessellation, job setup and stalls. That is a dimensional illustration, not a
prediction of game throughput. The current tessellator's three-to-nine reads per
triangle, subpatch scans and production arbitration must be measured together.

Do not spend six parallel normal multipliers to recover one clock of initiation
interval unless a measured workload needs it. Equally, do not serialize geomorph
so aggressively that it destroys the stream rate merely to save a few registers.
The chosen single pipelined morph lane is compatible with one lattice read per
cycle and at least three reads for each newly morphed vertex.

BEGIN PYTHON
#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""Independent arithmetic/control models for the terrain rearchitecture.
Not RTL simulation, synthesis, a formal proof, or a Quartus timing run.
Python 3 standard library only. Reproducible seed: 0x31_10_2026.
"""
from collections import deque
import json
import random

LO, HI = -(1 << 31), (1 << 31) - 1
RNG = random.Random(0x31_10_2026)

def sat32(x):
    return min(HI, max(LO, x))

def rescale(x, k):
    return sat32((x + (1 << (k - 1))) >> k)

def morph_reference(h, ha, hb, m):
    m = min(m, 65536)
    hc = sat32(ha + rescale(hb - ha, 1))
    return sat32(h + rescale(m * (hc - h), 16))

def morph_stages(h, ha, hb, m):
    # Model the specified registered arithmetic boundaries, not clock timing.
    d_ab_q = hb - ha
    half_q = rescale(d_ab_q, 1)
    coarse_q = sat32(ha + half_q)
    delta_q = coarse_q - h
    product_q = min(m, 65536) * delta_q
    step_q = rescale(product_q, 16)
    final_q = sat32(h + step_q)
    return final_q

def normal(tri):
    a, b, c = tri
    u = [b[i] - a[i] for i in range(3)]
    v = [c[i] - a[i] for i in range(3)]
    raw = (u[1]*v[2] - u[2]*v[1],
           u[2]*v[0] - u[0]*v[2],
           u[0]*v[1] - u[1]*v[0])
    n = tuple(rescale(x, 16) for x in raw)
    return n, not any(n)

def edge_pairs(tri):
    a, b, c = tri
    u = [b[i]-a[i] for i in range(3)]
    v = [c[i]-a[i] for i in range(3)]
    return ((u[1],v[2]), (u[2],v[1]),
            (u[2],v[0]), (u[0],v[2]),
            (u[0],v[1]), (u[1],v[0]))

class CurrentNormals:
    """Source-level sequential model of the inspected m_p_q implementation.
    State is read before each edge, matching nonblocking assignments.
    """
    def __init__(self):
        self.busy = self.issue = self.pv = self.s1v = self.s2v = False
        self.seq = self.pstep = self.p = self.src = 0
        self.pairs = [(0, 0)] * 6
        self.acc = [0, 0, 0]
        self.s1 = self.s2 = None
        self.count = 0
    def tick(self, packet, ready):
        b, issue, pv, s1v, s2v = self.busy,self.issue,self.pv,self.s1v,self.s2v
        seq, pstep, p = self.seq,self.pstep,self.p
        acc, pairs, src, s1, s2 = self.acc[:],self.pairs[:],self.src,self.s1,self.s2
        accept = packet is not None and not b and not s1v
        retired = s2 if s2v and ready else None
        free2 = not s2v or ready
        if not b and not s1v:
            if packet is not None:
                self.pairs = edge_pairs(packet[1])
                self.src = packet[0]
                self.seq = 0
                self.busy = self.issue = True
        elif b:
            if issue:
                if seq == 5: self.issue = False
                else: self.seq = seq + 1
            if pv:
                lane = pstep // 2
                self.acc[lane] = p if pstep % 2 == 0 else acc[lane] - p
                if pstep == 5:
                    self.busy = False
                    self.s1v = True
                    self.s1 = (src, (acc[0], acc[1], acc[2]-p))
        self.p = pairs[seq][0]*pairs[seq][1] if seq < 6 else 0
        self.pstep, self.pv = seq, b and issue
        if s1v and free2: self.s1v = False
        if free2:
            self.s2v = s1v
            if s1v:
                n = tuple(rescale(x, 16) for x in s1[1])
                self.s2 = (s1[0], n, not any(n))
                self.count += 1
        return accept, retired
    def idle(self):
        return not self.busy and not self.s1v and not self.s2v

def run():
    results = {}
    cases = [LO,LO+1,-65537,-65536,-32769,-32768,-1,0,1,
             32767,32768,65535,65536,HI-1,HI]
    factors = [0,1,32767,32768,32769,65535,65536,65537,131071]
    n_morph = 0
    for h in cases:
        for ha in cases:
            for hb in cases:
                for m in factors:
                    assert morph_reference(h,ha,hb,m) == morph_stages(h,ha,hb,m)
                    n_morph += 1
    for _ in range(100000):
        vals = [RNG.randint(LO, HI) for _ in range(3)]
        m = RNG.randrange(1 << 17)
        assert morph_reference(*vals, m) == morph_stages(*vals, m)
        n_morph += 1
    results['morph_equivalence_cases'] = n_morph
    results['counterexamples'] = {
        'midpoint_preserve_intermediate_saturation': {
            'ha': LO, 'hb': HI,
            'current_coarse_height': sat32(LO+rescale(HI-LO,1)),
            'single_sum_midpoint': (LO+HI+1)//2},
        'full_morph_is_not_always_coarse_height': {
            'h': LO, 'hc': HI, 'm':65536,
            'current_morph_height':sat32(LO+rescale(65536*(HI-LO),16)),
            'coarse_height':HI},
        'do_not_negate_rounded_normal': {
            'raw_lane':32768, 'round_positive':rescale(32768,16),
            'round_negative':rescale(-32768,16),
            'negate_rounded_positive':-rescale(32768,16)}}

    # Exact quotient/round-bit identity with arbitrary-precision intermediates.
    # Hardware implementations still need the documented width/range proof.
    n_round = 0
    bound = 2*((1 << 32)-1)**2
    for _ in range(100000):
        x = RNG.randint(-bound, bound)
        for k in (1,16):
            assert (x+(1 << (k-1))) >> k == (x >> k)+((x >> (k-1)) & 1)
            n_round += 1
    results['rounding_identity_cases'] = n_round

    # Build the 64+16+4+1 solidity pyramid, independently query rectangle bits.
    queries = 0
    for mask in [0,(1 << 64)-1] + [RNG.getrandbits(64) for _ in range(4096)]:
        levels = [[(mask >> i) & 1 for i in range(64)]]
        width = 8
        for _ in range(3):
            prev = levels[-1]
            levels.append([all(prev[(2*y+dy)*width+2*x+dx]
                               for dy in (0,1) for dx in (0,1))
                           for y in range(width//2) for x in range(width//2)])
            width //= 2
        for level in range(4):
            stride, n = 1 << level, 8 >> level
            for eb in range(n):
                for ea in range(n):
                    expected = all((mask >> (j*8+i)) & 1
                                   for j in range(eb*stride,(eb+1)*stride)
                                   for i in range(ea*stride,(ea+1)*stride))
                    assert levels[level][eb*n+ea] == expected
                    queries += 1

    results['solidity_queries'] = queries

        if out is not None: outputs.append(cycle)

    results['current_normals_output_transfer_edges_no_stall'] = outputs

                pending = (sent,t)

                     schedule == 'random' and RNG.random() < 0.6 or
                     schedule == 'one_in_32' and cycle % 32 == 0)
            accepted, out = model.tick(pending, ready)
            if accepted:

                expected.append((pending[0], n, deg))
                sent += 1
                pending = None
            if out is not None:
                assert expected and out == expected.popleft()
                retired += 1
            cycle += 1
        assert retired == target == model.count

    results['normal_control_model_retired'] = total_out
