## R1 — The FOV floor is ACCEPTED

> "minimum fov you propose is ok"

The question was `reports/OWNER-QUESTION-FOV-FLOOR-20260909.md`: may the
view-projection matrix's vertical perspective coefficient be capped at 2.0,
setting a vertical FOV floor just above **53.13°**?

**Answer: yes.** This unlocks narrowing the projector's nine matrix coefficients
to **signed 18-bit Q16.16**.

* Vertices are **untouched** — they stay full-width s32, ±32,768 world units.
  This was never a world-size question.
* The floor is **video-mode independent**: aspect only ever reduces `m00`, and the
  binding coefficient is `m11 = cot(fov/2)`. One ruling covers all three modes.
* What it forecloses: long-lens work below ~53.2° vertical. 60–90° cameras are
  unaffected.

**Worth −18 DSP taken alone, −10 marginal** after the shared service and
`ROWS_PER_PASS`, because all three levers act on the same eleven products and
**multiply rather than add**. The lattice: 66 → 33 → 15 → **5**.

**Still to do:** the narrowing itself is not implemented. `MATW` is the named
parameter; default stays 32 so nothing ships differently until the gate passes.

## R2 — Rounding: qformats wins, with a stated escape

> "qformats wins unless FORGE has strong visible/content-law reason to be
> exceptional"

`FORGE.PRIM.md` says *"round-half away from zero"*; `spec/qformats.md` §4 says
round-half-up. `zhao_forge_prim_eval.sv` followed qformats and flagged the
conflict rather than resolving it silently. **That was correct and it stands.**

The ruling is not "qformats always" — it is **qformats unless FORGE can show a
visible or content-law reason.** So the burden of proof now sits on anyone who
wants the exception, and it must be a *visible* reason, demonstrated by eye at
final resolution, not a symmetry argument. `FORGE.PRIM.md`'s line is corrected to
point at the ruling.

### R3 correction: the swap is NOT drop-in, and a report said it was

I went to perform this swap and checked the ports first. **`zhao_raster_rcp24_v3`
is not a drop-in replacement for `zhao_raster_rcp24_svc`**, and the archaeology
report that recommended it says it is — *"The v3 file also deliberately keeps the
old ports so it is drop-in."* That is wrong.

Measured by comparing the two port lists: **svc has 16 ports, v3 has 20.**

| | |
|---|---|
| in svc, **absent from v3** | `mul_busy_o` |
| in v3, absent from svc | `mul_jobs_o`, `zero_jobs_o`, `phase_jobs_o`, `negcorr_jobs_o`, `qerr_o` |
| width change | `occupancy_o` is `[3:0]` in svc and **`[5:0]` in v3** |

So the swap is four distinct changes inside `zhao_texture_island_v3_top.sv`, not
a parameter edit:

1. `zhao_raster_rcp24_svc #(.NCTX(8), …)` → `zhao_raster_rcp24_v3 #(.NCTX(12), …)`
2. `logic [3:0] rcp_occ;` must widen to `[5:0]`, or the occupancy count silently
   truncates above 15 — and NCTX=12 makes higher occupancy reachable, which is
   exactly the parameter the ruling changes.
3. `.mul_busy_o(rcp_mul_busy)` must go. Worth noting: `rcp_mul_busy` is declared
   at `:504`, connected at `:635`, and **read nowhere in the island** — a
   dead-end capture, so nothing is lost.
4. v3's four new job counters and its **`qerr_o` sticky error bit** need homes.
   `qerr_o` in particular must not be left open: an error output dropped on the
   floor is the unfired-detector law, and this repo has a `check_counters.py` that
   would flag it.

**Left unconnected, the four counters and `qerr_o` are a `PINMISSING` that only
the next fit discovers** — the exact trap `CLAUDE.md` documents for
`zhao_prod_top` regeneration.

This is the eighth claim checked and refused today, and the pattern is now
unmistakable: **"it is drop-in", "the owner already ruled it", "the measurement
says −6" — the confident one-line summary is where the error lives.** The ruling
stands and the swap should happen; it is simply a real change to the largest block
in the tree, and it goes to an architect rather than a hand edit at speed.

## R4 — The 1-bone-per-clock target is relaxed

> "relax the 1 bone/clock rule. It must have been arbitrary."

`GEOM.POSE.md`'s *"1 decoded bone per clock steady-state on miss"* forced the
12-product decode to be spatial, which is why `zhao_geom_pose_decode` carries
**18 DSP** — quat2mat (9) + mat3x4_mul (9).

Its own contract already contained the demand figure: *"≤128 distinct tuples/frame
× 32 × 12 ≈ 49k multiplies/frame — **noise against the DSP budget**"*. Against
`computeClocksPerFrame = 1,666,666` that is **2.9% of a frame on ONE multiplier
lane**, against 18 provisioned — roughly **34× over**.

**Relaxing to one bone per twelve clocks costs 2.9% of the frame and returns
17 DSP.** The owner's read that the target was arbitrary is supported by the
contract itself, which called the block's own cost "noise" while it consumed 9.5%
of the DSP census — a *utilisation* figure used to dismiss an *area* cost, which is
the exact inversion of `TEXTURE.TMU.md:490`'s ruling that each nonconstant `*`
creates one physical multiplier.

**Still to do:** the sequenced pose decoder is not implemented. `terrain_normals`
and `terrain_lod` are the in-repo pattern.
