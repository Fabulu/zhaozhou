2026-09-09, rescue phase. Read-only analysis through the census's **own**
selector (`build_bill`), not a reimplementation of it — the standing law from
brief §2.7 and from having broken exactly that check twice today.

DSP is the hardest cap in the machine: **192 counted, 112 physical, owner target
<= 94.** ALM is worse in absolute terms but has more places to go; DSP has
eighteen.

**192 - 44 = 148.** That is the honest reach of what is measured today, and it
leaves **54 DSP** to the target.

## Where the remaining 54 has to come from

Not from more small savings — there are none left. The five blocks that hold it:

```
zhao_geom_pose_decode  18      zhao_terrain_bake  17      zhao_shell_top  16
zhao_geom_cull         15      island (after rcp24) 14
```

### `pose_decode`'s 18 is two matrix engines, and that is the shape of the answer

Checked, not assumed:

```
fpga/rtl/geometry/zhao_geom_pose_decode.sv:155   zhao_geom_quat2mat   u_quat   ( 9 DSP )
fpga/rtl/geometry/zhao_geom_pose_decode.sv:190   zhao_geom_mat3x4_mul u_mul    ( 9 DSP )
                                                                         18 = the measured total
```

Both submodules carry their own separately measured 9 DSP. So pose decoding is a
quaternion-to-matrix conversion followed by a matrix multiply — **general
transform arithmetic, instantiated privately**, exactly like the projection core
was.

That is the second structural lever and it is the same lever as the first: the
geometry domain runs `project`, `pose_decode`, `skin`, `setup`, `cull` and `clip`
as separate arithmetic kingdoms, each provisioned for its own peak. The owner's
roadmap calls for a shared transform service inside a bounded, schedulable
geometry domain — not a machine-wide ALU, which would trade an area problem for
a throughput disaster.

**90 DSP of geometry against a roadmap allocation of ~24** is where the target
becomes reachable. The projector proved the pattern is real and that collapsing
it costs no arithmetic; pose/skin/setup is the same pattern with more clients.

## What this analysis does NOT establish

**Not that 94 is reachable.** 148 is measured-or-structural; the remaining 54
depends on a geometry transform service that does not exist yet, and on whatever
`terrain_bake`, `shell_top` and `geom_cull` turn out to be made of. Two of those
five I have not opened.

**Not a fit.** Every number is a ledger row through the census's own selector.
The shared projector's 33 is a structural prediction from the instantiation graph
and the multiplier count; it becomes real at a fit, which the owner has said to
avoid until it is worth spending.

**Not the ALM story.** DSP has eighteen holders; ALM has sixty-six. They overlap
but are not the same problem, and the register breach is a third problem again.

## The immediate consequence

The four standing owner calls are no longer bookkeeping — **two of them are DSP
moves** (`material_combine_v1` -2, `geom_skin MUL_LANES=1` -6) and a third
(`rcp24_v3`) is -3. Together with the projector they are 44 of the 98 needed, and
three of the four need only a word rather than an architecture.

# ADDENDUM: the geometry domain's 90 DSP is one primitive, copied

Opened `zhao_geom_cull` (15 DSP), one of the two holders the section above
admitted were unexamined. Its cost reconstructs exactly:

```
fpga/rtl/geometry/zhao_geom_cull.sv
  193  function mul_pc     (PC_W=33 x 32 signed)
  209  function mul_slack  (32 x LEN_W=34 signed)
  351  assign sq_prod = $signed(sq_operand) * $signed(sq_operand)

  369  dot   = mul_pc(pl_a, ev_cx) + mul_pc(pl_b, ev_cy) + mul_pc(pl_c, ev_cz) + ...
  373  slack = mul_slack(ev_r, len_ceil[...])
```

**Five multiplier sites x 3 DSP = 15.** Reconstructed, not assumed — and the same
function-call-is-not-shared-hardware rule that explained `project_core`'s nine.

## The primitive is the same one, three times over

| block | the arithmetic | sites |
|---|---|---:|
| `zhao_project_core` | `mat[0]*vx + mat[1]*vy + mat[2]*vz`, three rows | 9 |
| `zhao_geom_cull` | `pl_a*cx + pl_b*cy + pl_c*cz`, one plane | 3 (+2) |
| `zhao_geom_pose_decode` | `quat2mat` (9) + `mat3x4_mul` (9) | 18 |

A three-term signed dot product, provisioned privately in every block that wants
one. `project_core` computes three of them per vertex; `geom_cull` computes one
per plane; `pose_decode` computes a matrix's worth twice over.

That is why the geometry domain is **90 DSP against the roadmap's ~24
allocation**, and it is the same finding as the projector at a larger scale: the
duplication is not of a *block*, it is of a *primitive*.

## What follows, and what does not

**Follows:** a bounded, schedulable geometry transform service — a small number of
physical dot-product lanes with resident operands and queued clients — is the
structural answer, exactly as the roadmap says. Not a machine-wide ALU: pixel-rate
work, geometry preparation and FIELD must not compete through one bus, which
trades an area problem for a throughput disaster.

**Does not follow:** that the lanes can be reduced to one. Each client's demand
has to be counted before choosing a lane count, and the projector's own case shows
why — one shared core was only defensible because 903,552 projections fit inside
a ~1,333,333-clock window. **The lane count is a throughput calculation, not a
preference**, and it must be done per client before any RTL.

**Still unopened:** `zhao_shell_top`'s 16 DSP. It is a composed hierarchy rather
than a leaf, so its DSP is distributed across the shell's blocks and is unlikely
to be one primitive. It is the last unexamined holder.

# ADDENDUM 2: pose_decode's 18 DSP serves 2.9% of a frame

The report above said the lane count for a geometry transform service "is a
throughput calculation, not a preference, and it must be done per client before
any RTL." Doing it for the largest remaining holder.

`design/budgets/workloads.yml` lists `zhao_geom_pose_decode` under **`unruled`**
-- no demand figure -- with this note:

> "The pose path has no derived invocation count. It is the block the docket
> predicts hides 14-18 DSPs, so this is the demand figure most worth deriving
> next."

The docket's prediction was exactly right: it hides **18**.

## The derivation was already written, in the contract

`design/contracts/GEOM.POSE.md` carries it:

> "Working-set derivation: <=128 distinct tuples/frame x 32 x 12 ~= 49k
> multiplies/frame -- **noise against the DSP budget**."

Against `computeClocksPerFrame = 1,666,666`, **49,152 multiplies is 2.9% of a
frame.**

And 128 is not a capacity masquerading as a demand -- the trap that made
`terrain_project` look 6,173x over-provisioned. The contract *clamps* it:

> "Cache-full: evict LRU tuple not referenced this frame; a tuple referenced
> this frame is never evicted (**bounded by the 128-tuple budget -- a frame
> demanding more is a content-tier violation, counted and clamped
> deterministically**)."

A ruled, counted, deterministic clamp is a demand figure. It is also
corroborated from the other side: the 120,000-vertex ruling over a median 2,951
vertices is ~40 creatures, and the type-grouped army economy means instances of
one type playing one clip at one tick **share one decoded pose**, so distinct
tuples cannot much exceed the instance count anyway.

## What that means for the 18

| | |
|---|---|
| demand | 49,152 multiplies / frame |
| frame | 1,666,666 clocks |
| **one** physical multiplier lane at II=1 | ~~2.9%~~ **28.4% measured** — see the correction in `POSE-DECODE-SEQUENCED-20260909.md`; the 12-multiplies-per-bone figure undercounts the real 81 by 6.75x |
| provisioned today | `quat2mat` 9 DSP + `mat3x4_mul` 9 DSP = **18** |

The block is provisioned roughly **34x above its own contract's declared
working set.** The reason is the contract's throughput target -- *"1 decoded
bone per clock steady-state on miss"* -- which forces the whole 12-product
decode to be spatial. Relaxing that to one bone per twelve clocks costs 2.9% of
the frame and returns **17 DSP**.

`zhao_terrain_normals` (one multiply muxed across six operand cases) and
`zhao_terrain_lod` (one multiply across square and eval) are the in-repo proof
that this shape synthesises here. This is not a new mechanism, it is the same
one, applied to the block that most needs it.

## Where the arithmetic then stands

    -33  shared projector           (structural, needs the arena first)
     -3  rcp24_svc -> rcp24_v3      (measured)
     -2  retire material_combine_v1 (owner call)
     -6  geom_skin MUL_LANES=1      (owner call, costs 33 MHz)
    ----

`zhao_geom_cull`'s 15 is the next one and it will NOT collapse to one lane: at a
ruled 333,333 evaluations/frame with ~6 products each, demand is ~2,000,000

the answer for the rest of geometry: **compute the client's demand, then buy the
lanes it needs, instead of provisioning every call site for its own peak.**

## Not established

That 17 is bankable. It is a structural prediction from a contract-declared
workload, and it trades a stated throughput target the owner set. **The relaxed
target is an owner call, not mine.** The DSP figure becomes real at the
projection-subsystem fit, alongside the projector and the arena -- one fit, three
questions.

# ADDENDUM 3: I quoted a lever whose own contract says the configuration FAILS

A contract-archaeology sweep refuted a number in the table above. It is right and
I was wrong, and the error is one this repository has a name for.

## The claim I made

> | `geom_skin MUL_LANES=1` | **-6** | already fitted; costs -33 MHz, so it is a
> trade the owner accepts or refuses |

## What `design/contracts/GEOM.SKIN.md:447-462` actually says

| `MUL_LANES` | DSPs | ALMs | registers | Fmax | II | vertices/frame | vs. 120,000 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 3 | 1,530 | 1,449 | 56.11 MHz* | 24 | 38,965 | **fails, 32%** |
| **3** | **9** | **2,225** | **1,696** | **89.65 MHz** | **12** | **124,514** | **passes, 104%** |
| 6 | 18 | 2,595 | 1,683 | 84.61 MHz | 10 | 141,017 | passes, 118% |

> "`MUL_LANES = 3` is the shipping point. **`MUL_LANES = 1` is kept because it
> fails**, and it fails by a mile once the pipeline's fill is spread over only
> one lane's 18 issue slots."

And at `:218`:

> "`MUL_LANES = 1` is kept, built and differentiated **because it fails**: a
> frontier with no failing end does not show where the wall is, and **section 8
> of the directed test asserts that this configuration is below the demand**
> rather than letting it pass quietly."

## Three things wrong, not one

1. **The trade is not "-6 DSP for -33 MHz". It is "-6 DSP for a block that
   delivers 32% of its owner-ruled demand"** -- 38,965 vertices against the
   120,000 ruled on 2026-08-23. There is a committed test that asserts this
   configuration fails. It is not an owner call; it is a disqualified point.
2. **It is not -6 anyway.** The contract says these frontier rows "**must be
   excluded from any DSP total** -- they are the same block". `zhao_geom_skin`
   is counted once, at 9, in its shipping configuration.
3. **The row is stale.** The footnote: "the `MUL_LANES = 1` row was measured on
   the pre-pipeline RTL (`e7591e8`) as the diagnosis experiment; it has not been
   re-fitted since."

## Why I made it, since that is the transferable part

The disqualifying column was **in the same table, two columns to the right of
the one I quoted.** I read the Fmax column, found a number that fit the shape of
a tradeable lever, and stopped. This is the broken-instrument law in its purest
form -- *the defect always makes the answer look better, smaller or simpler than
the truth* -- except the instrument was me, and the "measurement" was a table I
had already opened.

It is also the *deliberately built failing endpoint* trap. Somebody constructed
a configuration whose entire purpose is to fail, wrote down that it fails, and
asserted the failure in a test. Reading its DSP column as an available saving
inverts the author's intent completely.

# ADDENDUM 4: the last unexamined holder is opened, and it is NOT a lever

The report above closed with *"Still unopened: `zhao_shell_top`'s 16 DSP. It is a
composed hierarchy rather than a leaf, so its DSP is distributed across the
shell's blocks and is unlikely to be one primitive. It is the last unexamined
holder."* Opened now.

## What is inside it

Its instantiation closure is **41 modules**, and five of them carry measured DSP:

    zhao_geom_bin_pipe      21
    zhao_geom_binner        12   (stale row -- see below)
    zhao_raster_fragment    10
    zhao_raster_tile_pipe    9
    zhao_raster_edgewalk     2
    ------------------------------
    leaf rows sum to        54

**The shell's own composed row measures 16.** So composition already accounts for
a 38-DSP difference between the children's standalone rows and the thing they
compose into.

## Why that makes it a poor target, which is the useful part

A composed number **already three times smaller than the sum of its parts** is
not where a sharing lever hides — whatever pruning, sharing or virtual-pin
inflation explains the gap has already happened. The prediction in the original
sentence was right: it is distributed, it is not one primitive, and there is no
`project_core`-shaped duplication to collapse.

**I am not claiming to know which mechanism closes the 54-to-16 gap.** Leaf fits
carry virtual-pin inflation, composition prunes unreachable logic, and the shell
may not exercise every child's full parameterisation. Those are three different
explanations with three different consequences and the evidence here separates
none of them. What it does establish is that **the 16 is already a composed
measurement, so it should not be re-hunted as though it were an unopened leaf.**

## One real finding fell out of it

`zhao_geom_binner` carries **two rows**, and the census's selector is right to
prefer the second:

    dsp=12  commit 96c0394a  rtlCleanAtHead FALSE   <- the stale, dirty row
    dsp= 6  commit 534bbd22  rtlCleanAtHead TRUE    <- the 23-bit slope narrowing

The archaeology sweep flagged that the repository states **two disagreeing
post-fix numbers** for this block — `zhao_geom_binner.sv:459` says "12 -> 4" while
`zhao_pair_setup_binner.sv:8` says "12 -> 6 DSPs, mapped". **The ledger settles
it: there is a CLEAN measured row at 6.** The "12 -> 4" sentence in the block's
own header is the one that is wrong, and it is wrong in the flattering direction.

Also confirmed, because it would have been a real accounting fault: none of the
five children appears in the manifest's counted-top list. They are counted
**inside** the shell, so the 54 is not being added to the 16 anywhere. No
double-count.
