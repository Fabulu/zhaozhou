### `pose_decode`'s 18 is two matrix engines, and that is the shape of the answer

Checked, not assumed:

```
fpga/rtl/geometry/zhao_geom_pose_decode.sv:155   zhao_geom_quat2mat   u_quat   ( 9 DSP )
fpga/rtl/geometry/zhao_geom_pose_decode.sv:190   zhao_geom_mat3x4_mul u_mul    ( 9 DSP )
                                                                         18 = the measured total
```

Both submodules carry their own separately measured 9 DSP. So pose decoding is a
quaternion-to-matrix conversion followed by a matrix multiply — **general
transform arithmetic, instantiated privately**, exactly like the projection core
was.

That is the second structural lever and it is the same lever as the first: the
geometry domain runs `project`, `pose_decode`, `skin`, `setup`, `cull` and `clip`
as separate arithmetic kingdoms, each provisioned for its own peak. The owner's
roadmap calls for a shared transform service inside a bounded, schedulable
geometry domain — not a machine-wide ALU, which would trade an area problem for
a throughput disaster.

**90 DSP of geometry against a roadmap allocation of ~24** is where the target
becomes reachable. The projector proved the pattern is real and that collapsing
it costs no arithmetic; pose/skin/setup is the same pattern with more clients.

## What this analysis does NOT establish

**Not that 94 is reachable.** 148 is measured-or-structural; the remaining 54
depends on a geometry transform service that does not exist yet, and on whatever
`terrain_bake`, `shell_top` and `geom_cull` turn out to be made of. Two of those
five I have not opened.

**Not a fit.** Every number is a ledger row through the census's own selector.
The shared projector's 33 is a structural prediction from the instantiation graph
and the multiplier count; it becomes real at a fit, which the owner has said to
avoid until it is worth spending.

**Not the ALM story.** DSP has eighteen holders; ALM has sixty-six. They overlap
but are not the same problem, and the register breach is a third problem again.

## The immediate consequence

The four standing owner calls are no longer bookkeeping — **two of them are DSP
moves** (`material_combine_v1` -2, `geom_skin MUL_LANES=1` -6) and a third
(`rcp24_v3`) is -3. Together with the projector they are 44 of the 98 needed, and
three of the four need only a word rather than an architecture.
