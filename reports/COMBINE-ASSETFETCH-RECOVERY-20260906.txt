0. EXECUTIVE DECISION
====================

COMBINE needs a different execution organization, not different material math.
Preserve all eight recipes. Replace record-by-job priority scans with two
explicit product lanes, a fixed paired-phase schedule, small ready queues,
synchronous operand storage and one whole-context writeback per phase.

ASSETFETCH needs overlap and ownership, not two complete fetch engines.
Preserve the checked footprint planner and corrected guard handshake. Use two
meshlet payload banks, one fill engine, independent consumers and descriptor
lookahead. Share one permitted ENGINE1 memory client between descriptor and
payload requests through a logical-owner adapter.

There are three immediate prerequisites:

  The island's new 64-entry output reorder buffer has no matching admission
  credit covering final output retirement. Its claimed overflow proof does not
  follow from FRAGROB's capacity. Long downstream stalls can wrap the table.

  ASSETFETCH still reads its client identity live after acceptance. MESHFETCH
  reads its descriptor address and client live after acceptance. These must be
  captured before prefetching makes overlapping requests ordinary.

  The readable committed COMBINE timing exports describe the old 29.74 MHz
  experiment, not the new manually recorded 36.28 MHz result. Recover matching
  setup/hold reports before claiming to have identified the new worst cone.

The plan retains normal maps, mipmaps, three logical material samples, alpha,
real memory arbitration and frame-resource protection. Its purpose is to remove
uncontrolled work selection and serialized waiting, not reduce the game to fit
implementation defects. [R1-R11, R15-R18]

completed records is not a held output transaction: a newly completed higher-
priority record must not replace a result already offered while ready is low.

1.5 Corrections to previous assessments
--------------------------------------
The new order-restoration test proves its 64-fragment workload, not sustained
backpressure safety. Calling R6 fully closed was too broad: FRAGROB releases
contexts before final island retirement, so it does not bound the parked output.

The 30-to-360 stall increase does not promise a 12x benefit from buffering.
It exposes costs omitted by the fake memory, not twelvefold avoidable work.

The recipe names are insufficient to infer their math. Current LERP interpolates
alpha; MODULATE2X doubles alpha; DETAIL_MASK multiplies s0/s1 RGB and s0/s2 alpha.
It is NOT a third-sample-weighted colour lerp. These exact semantics govern the
rewrite. [R2]


2. MANDATORY END-TO-END LIFETIME CREDITS
======================================

2.1 Counterexample in the current shape
--------------------------------------
The island admits based on RCP readiness. Final output has 64 completion slots,
a six-bit submission sequence, and permanently ready COMBINE completion. There
is no matching admission count from external acceptance to final sink transfer.
[R3]

Hold the sink not-ready:
  admit and complete sequences 0..63;
  entry 0 still holds sequence 0;
  internal contexts have been released and admit more work;
  sequence 64 completes and overwrites entry 0.

This loses the first result and changes a valid stalled output. A 64-input test
cannot expose the wrap. A local transition model reproduced the failure shape;
this is not a claim of a Verilator reproduction of the full current island.

2.2 Required invariant and minimal implementation
------------------------------------------------
Within one non-aborted epoch, define cumulative external admissions A and final
external output transfers E:

  live = A - E
  0 <= live <= OWNER_DEPTH = 64

Reserve one completion/owner slot at ADMISSION. Return its credit ONLY at final
external output acceptance, not at RCP/PERSPUV completion, FRAGROB retirement,
COMBINE completion or transfer into an output skid.

Conservative seam:

  credit_available = live < OWNER_DEPTH
  rcp_valid         = frag_valid && credit_available
  frag_ready        = rcp_ready && credit_available
  admit             = frag_valid && frag_ready
  emit              = out_valid && out_ready

Gate both caller-ready and RCP-valid. Otherwise RCP can accept a phantom job
that the caller was told did not handshake. Update the live count with the
combined admit/emit case once per clock.

Initially do not bypass a full condition using the same-cycle outgoing result.
A conservative one-cycle bubble at full-to-free avoids cyclic ready paths and
same-slot clear/write hazards. Optimize it only after the invariant is proven.

2.3 Owner and execution context are different lifetimes
------------------------------------------------------
A global owner survives admission through final island retirement. A COMBINE
context survives only while that service holds a material job. Each needs its
own credit. Completing a COMBINE context into its reserved owner may free that
service context without freeing the global slot.

The owner holds immutable fragment identity, submission position and metadata
still needed by delayed services. COMBINE carries its owner handle and captured
operands, then returns handle/result. It must not allocate a second independent
global ordering system.

A low six-bit index identifies a bounded 64-live window only because the credit
invariant holds. Reset/epoch/cancelled responses are separate: validate the
existing generation/epoch law at return or guarantee and test quiescent draining.
Do not assume modulo indexing also excludes a stale response after reset.

2.4 Completion RAM and stable output
-----------------------------------
Use synchronous completion storage plus small reserved/done flags. Store RGBA,
refusal and caller tag together, or synchronize completion and immutable-tag
reads. Use a registered head read and a held output/skid, not an unregistered
64-to-1 result/tag mux. Reset validity/control, not payload RAM.

A result fetched into a skid remains globally reserved until the external sink
accepts it. Do not mark its slot free at RAM read or FIFO pop.

Assert:
  every completion targets a live matching owner/generation;
  a slot receives one completion, never a duplicate overwrite;
  valid && !ready implies stable payload/tag/status;
  final retirement is the next admission sequence;
  live equals all outstanding reservations, including output-held results;
  no service has accepted a fragment absent from external admission.

A lost head fragment is an explicit fault, not permission to skip it on timeout.
Use the declared refused-result/frame-abort policy after draining or cancelling
its obligations. Keep internal out-of-order execution: the missing piece is
correct lifetime reservation, not a demand to serialize every service.


3. EXACT MATERIAL LAWS, INCLUDING ALPHA
======================================

Read directly from the current scalar reference. [R2]

  U(a,b) = (a*b + 128) >> 8
  M2(a,b) = min(255, 2*U(a,b))
  A(a,b) = min(255, a+b)
  L(a,b,w) = clamp8(a + sign(b-a)*((abs(b-a)*w + 128) >> 8))

All inputs are unsigned bytes except the explicitly widened signed difference.
U(255,255)=254. Weight 255 is not mathematically 1.0. Do not substitute division
by 255, asymmetric signed rounding, floating point or a combined three-layer
product with only one final rounding.

Recipe                    RGB                       ALPHA                products
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0 PASSTHRU                s0                        s0.a                     0
1 MODULATE                U(s0,s1)                  U(s0.a,s1.a)            4
2 MODULATE2X              M2(s0,s1)                 M2(s0.a,s1.a)           4
3 LERP                    L(s0,s1,weight)           L(s0.a,s1.a,weight)     4
4 ADD_SAT                 A(s0,s1)                  A(s0.a,s1.a)            0
5 MASK                    s0 if s1.a!=0, else zero  same binary gate         0
6 TERRAIN_DETAIL_LIGHT    U(U(s0,s1),s2)             s0.a                     6
7 TERRAIN_DETAIL_MASK     U(s0,s1)                  U(s0.a,s2.a)            4

ADD and MODULATE2X saturation counts are once per fragment if ANY affected
channel saturates, not once per channel. MASK tests alpha: black opaque masks
keep s0. Required sample counts are [1,2,2,2,2,2,3,3].

count==0 has precedence and returns BASE RGBA before recipe refusal. No dummy
sample is required. Insufficient samples produce the oracle's refused result
and preserve identity. Their executed product count is zero.

Unknown software recipes with nonzero samples are refused. A three-bit hardware
field cannot encode recipe 8; do not test 8 by truncating it into 0 and claim
unknown-encoding coverage. Wider command fields are validated at their real
boundary.

TAGW is opaque. The current C++ helper has a 16-bit caller tag while the island
carries additional sequencing bits. Check full hardware transport separately
so the C++ helper's type does not truncate the wider handle.


4. PAIRED-PHASE COMBINE ARCHITECTURE
===================================

4.1 Selected shape
------------------
Build zhao_texture_material_combine_v2 beside V1. Use TWO explicit unsigned
8x8 product lanes, initially EIGHT bounded execution contexts, and one fixed-
latency phase pipeline. Test four/eight contexts; the count is a latency-hiding
choice, not the global fragment capacity.

  immutable payload/tag capture
            |
         NEW FIFO ------+
                        +--> select one head --> sync operand/scratch read
         CONT FIFO -----+                            |
             ^                                  operand registers
             |                                       |
             |                                TWO product lanes
             |                                       |
             |                                rounded/post result
             |                                       |
             +------- continuation <---------- one writeback
                                                     |
                                             completion RAM/FIFO
                                                     |
                                                held output
                                                     |
                                             reserved owner slot

No record-by-job scan, per-record 20-bit issued/done network, arbitrary
cross-record lane selection or separate hidden alpha multiplier.

4.2 Paired schedule
--------------------
Every product-bearing phase uses both lanes for ONE context.

PASSTHRU / ADD / MASK / zero-sample / refused:
  One zero-product phase through the same pipeline.

MODULATE / MODULATE2X / LERP:
  phase 0: R and G;
  phase 1: B and A, preserving R/G.

DETAIL_MASK:
  phase 0: U(s0.r,s1.r), U(s0.g,s1.g);
  phase 1: U(s0.b,s1.b), U(s0.a,s2.a).

DETAIL_LIGHT:
  phase 0: R1=U(s0.r,s1.r), G1=U(s0.g,s1.g);
  phase 1: B1=U(s0.b,s1.b), R2=U(R1,s2.r);
  phase 2: G2=U(G1,s2.g), B2=U(B1,s2.b).
  alpha: s0.a, no multiplication.

A 32-bit scratch word holds:
  after phase 0: {R1,G1,unused,s0.a};
  after phase 1: {R2,G1,B1,s0.a};
  final:        {R2,G2,B2,s0.a}.

This preserves all six products and both rounding layers without needing a
three-lane first stage or a general dependency scheduler.

4.3 Why pair within one context
-------------------------------
Arbitrary lane selection from two records demands two record reads and two
potentially scattered writebacks. Pairing within one record gives one address,
one phase tag and one whole-row write. Every product recipe has an even job
count, and the fixed DETAIL_LIGHT schedule handles its only special dependency.

Use separate NEW and CONT FIFOs because both may enqueue in one cycle: input
acceptance and a returning nonfinal phase. A single RAM FIFO with two writers
would silently introduce another memory-port problem.

Arbitrate ready heads fairly, for example registered round-robin preference
when both are ready. Do not let continuous new work starve continuations or
vice versa. Queue entries are context indices/phase bits, not wide payloads.

4.4 One phase in flight per context
-----------------------------------
A context exists in exactly one of NEW, CONT, phase pipeline, DONE/output hold
or FREE. Its next phase becomes eligible only after the previous scratch row
has been written and its continuation becomes visible. The initial version
avoids same-edge scratch write/read bypasses and undefined RAM read-during-write.

Zero-product phases use the common pipeline with multipliers disabled. An
immediate bypass could collide with a returning arithmetic job and create two
completion writes. The uniform path guarantees at most one writeback per clock.
It costs one ticket for simple recipes; capacity accounting includes it.


5. COMBINE STORAGE AND PORT SCHEDULE
====================================

Suggested per-context stores:
  payload: s0/s1/s2 RGBA (96 bits), weight, recipe/count/control;
           112- or 128-bit row;
  scratch: 32 channel bits plus earlier-saturation flag where needed;
  completion: RGBA + refusal/status;
  immutable tag: TAGW bits, read when completion is output.

For count==0, copy BASE into s0 and select a bypass operation. This avoids
carrying another permanent base-colour field for every textured context.
Preserve validation precedence before canonicalizing the input.

Carry the small execution-context ID through stages rather than repeating the
full opaque tag everywhere. Do not recycle that context before its final tag
read and output acceptance.

Store          single write source       single read source
------------------------------------------------------------
Payload        admitted input            selected phase
Scratch        nonfinal writeback        selected continuation
Completion     final writeback           output reader
Opaque tag     admitted input            output reader
NEW FIFO       admitted input            scheduler
CONT FIFO      nonfinal writeback        scheduler
DONE FIFO      final writeback           output reader
FREE FIFO      output accepted           admitted input

The schedule deliberately gives each memory at most one read and one write
per cycle. Scratch and completion are separate because an output read can occur
while another context reads continuation operands. Payload and scratch are
separate because admission and writeback can happen together.

Do NOT initialize scratch on admission: that creates a second writer. Phase 0
ignores old scratch and writes every field its continuation needs. Control and
validity reset; payload RAM does not.

Use clock-only RAM processes, registered memory outputs before selection, and
registered selected operands before products. Inspect actual RAM Summary names,
ports and depths. Declared arrays/attributes/lint do not establish inference.
Small narrow FIFO heads may be registers; do not turn them into circulating
128-bit packets.

Every admitted context reserves its completion row and bounded DONE capacity.
Thus already-issued phases can always write back even if the output stops.
Popping a DONE index does not release the context; release occurs when its held
COMBINE output is accepted. Inside the island, that output then occupies a
separately reserved global owner until final external retirement.


6. COMBINE PIPELINE AND FLOW CONTROL
====================================

6.1 Sequential boundaries
-------------------------
Recommended stages, specified edge-by-edge in RTL:
  Q: selected FIFO ticket/address;
  R: synchronous payload/scratch read;
  O: selected operands, lerp magnitude/sign/base, registered;
  M: two raw products, registered;
  F: rounding, add/subtract, doubling/saturation, registered;
  W: one whole scratch/final write, continuation/completion event.

The abstract model uses five issue-to-return clocks. Depending on RAM output
configuration, implementation may need another clock; update the contract,
context-depth check and tests honestly. Latency may increase without reducing
the intended one-ticket-per-clock initiation rate.

Prohibit these one-cycle cones:
  queue selection -> indexed payload mux -> multiply -> accumulation;
  DETAIL_LIGHT first-layer multiply -> second-layer multiply;
  RAM output -> another asynchronous indexed table -> arithmetic;
  operand selection -> wide diagnostic accumulation.

6.2 Arithmetic implementation
-----------------------------
For LERP, difference/sign preparation precedes multiplication. abs(b-a) fits
in eight bits but the initial signed difference must be widened. Round the
magnitude, then reapply sign to the held base value.

Declare raw product width and rounding-add width explicitly. MODULATE2X uses
a nine-bit doubled rounded result before clamp, including alpha. DETAIL_LIGHT
stores an 8-bit first layer; it is not a fused product.

Both lanes write one whole context word. Carry an earlier saturation flag in
scratch and emit the one fragment-level event at final writeback. No output
or product return consults live input recipe, weight or the current FIFO head.

6.3 Stalls and output holding
-----------------------------
Input ready falls when no context/NEW capacity remains. After handshake the
producer is free to change its pins. The core uses only captured data.

No ready ticket inserts a pipeline bubble; issued tickets continue with their
metadata. A downstream pause stores completed contexts and eventually blocks
new admissions. It must not stop an instruction halfway between multiply issue
and the fixed-latency product return.

A held output register/skid reserves space before completion/tag RAM reads.
Its tag, RGBA and refusal remain stable until accepted. A later completed
higher-priority record cannot displace it.

The two multipliers are dedicated here. Introducing an externally shared
multiply arbiter with independent refusals would require a different phase
acceptance protocol and is out of scope.

6.4 Registered events
----------------------
Suggested core events:
  issue {valid, recipe, products_this_phase=0 or 2};
  final {valid, refusal_kind, saturated_add, saturated_mul2x};
  occupancy/error witnesses.

Events are registered at defined points, not reduced combinationally over all
records. Their conversion into externally visible counters is part of the
resource/observability contract below.


7. INSTRUMENTATION, DSP MAPPING AND RESOURCE BUDGETS
==================================================

7.1 The counter floor is real
-----------------------------
Eight 32-bit jobs-by-recipe counters and four 32-bit refusal/saturation counters
already hold 384 bits of state. A 500-register limit for the whole legacy-shaped
block leaves 116 registers for all pipelines, queues, output and control.
A carefully pipelined replacement may not fit that scope. [R1,R2]

Do not hide this by removing counters or reporting a stripped core as a pass.
Keep three explicit scopes:
  CCORE: functional core and registered events;
  CWRAP: core plus legacy live counter interface;
  CISLAND: actual production island, charging counters wherever they live.

The first migration can retain CWRAP. A later explicitly versioned telemetry
change may send events to a shared counter/snapshot bank. That is not free:
charge the bank in production, preserve observability and test event latency.
It must not be a bookkeeping maneuver that removes costs from every total.

7.2 Keep counter updates out of the issue cone
----------------------------------------------
One selected phase emits a recipe and zero/two products. Register the event,
then update the selected counter. Preserve the current counter wrap/saturation
law. Tests drain the bounded event pipeline; do not require combinationally
visible counts unless the old interface genuinely specified that cycle.

Do not recreate blocking 32-bit additions in nested scheduling loops. If a
counter itself limits timing, measure and stage it explicitly rather than
waiving an actively sampled path with a multicycle exception.

7.3 Make logic mapping a measurement
-------------------------------------
LOGIC2 is the proposed initial variant: exactly two named product operators
mapped to general logic, with max_dsp=0 for that variant. Put style controls on
persistent direct product nets or a small multiplication module; inspect the
actual assignments/netlist. Vendor documentation explains the scope/direct-
result requirement, but only the pinned tool's mapping settles it. [R1,V3]

Do NOT force all island/console multipliers to logic. RCP, perspective and
geometry intentionally use DSPs. A global option in a leaf-only experiment is
not safe to copy into the production QSF.

If LOGIC2 has a poor result, compare the SAME phase pipeline as an explicitly
named DSP2 variant. Two DSPs may be the sensible measured trade. They may not
be reported as a zero-DSP success simply because an inherited max_dsp=2 passes.

7.4 Targets and reservations
-----------------------------
Keep the existing 125 MHz leaf headroom and approximately 800-ALM/500-register
small-variant thresholds as acceptance references, not achieved results. Do
not move a redline after seeing a failure. Measure CCORE/CWRAP scopes clearly.
[R20]

Reserve approximately 6-8 M10Ks for separate payload, scratch, completion, tag
and optional queue stores in the initial RAM-first core. This is a structural
reservation, not a fit. Small scratch/tag queues may be cheaper in MLAB or
narrow registers; decide from actual mapping with the same registered ports.

If the compatibility wrapper cannot meet the inherited register/ALM envelope,
leave the result red and request an explicit telemetry change or measured
whole-island rebudget. This brief does not authorize silent threshold changes.

Pipeline registers may increase while distributed payload and selection logic
fall. Judge total timing/resources, not register count alone. The global owner
RAM also replaces the new muxed completion table; do not add another table and
ignore the old one's cost.


8. COMBINE CAPACITY MODEL
=========================

Count both products and phase tickets:

  products = 4*(Nmod+Nmod2+Nlerp+Ndetailmask) + 6*Ndetail-light
  tickets  = Nzero + 2*(Nmod+Nmod2+Nlerp+Ndetailmask) + 3*Ndetail-light

Nzero includes actual bypass/add/mask/refused/zero-sample work. Categories must
be disjoint: an untextured MODULATE is not also a textured MODULATE job.

There are at most two products and one ticket per clock. Thus intended
asymptotic service ceilings with ready independent work are:
  zero-product recipes: one fragment/clock;
  four-product recipes: one fragment/two clocks;
  DETAIL_LIGHT: one fragment/three clocks.

These are service ceilings, not frame-rate guarantees. Upstream samples,
continuation latency, finite contexts and output stalls can reduce them.
The eight-context/five-cycle abstract queue model reaches these limits on long
homogeneous streams; actual RTL must reproduce them with real RAM visibility.

At 100 MHz/60 Hz there are about 1,666,667 periods per frame. With a 20% reserve,
the work envelope is about 1,333,333. Charge observed bubbles and recovery traffic;
do not spend that reserve again in each subsystem.

Mipmap/palette/bilinear/normal work is upstream sampling cost. COMBINE accepts
logical sample results. Three logical samples can represent many texture taps;
this core does not supply a free extra mip-blend engine by sharing its lanes.


9. ASSETFETCH DIAGNOSIS AND OVERLAP LIMITS
========================================

Preserve the useful existing machinery: wide checked footprint arithmetic,
count/alignment validation, line requests, synchronous storage, two replicated
index RAMs and the repaired delayed guard verdict. Do not restart the geometry
format or build a general speculative cache. [R4-R6]

Maximum useful payload:
  64 vertices x 32 bytes = 2,048 bytes;
  126 triangles x 3 u8 indices = 378 bytes;
  total 2,426 bytes.

Current lifetime:
  accept -> fill index -> fill vertex -> handoff -> serve -> release.
The next job cannot fill while this one is consumed.

Let F be fill time under a stated memory workload and C be consumption/release
time. Single banking pays roughly F+C per meshlet. Proper two-bank lookahead
approaches max(F,C) after startup, plus transition/arbitration overhead.

Overlap-only ideal speedup is (F+C)/max(F,C), between 1 and 2 for repeated equal
jobs, NOT 12. A cold first meshlet still pays memory latency. If F dominates,
the next bank often is not ready when consumption ends. If C dominates,
fetch can hide but consumption remains the limit.

A second bank is useless without a next accepted descriptor and a release rule
that waits for both consumers. The 360-stall experiment justifies measuring
those behaviors; it does not prove a specific game-frame speedup.


10. FOOTPRINTS, COMPACTION AND CONCRETE RAM PACKING
=================================================

10.1 Alignment changes the traffic maximum
------------------------------------------
Vertex offsets are 32-byte aligned and index offsets are 8-byte aligned;
external lines are 64 bytes. The code already derives the worst prefixes:

  index: 56-byte prefix + 378 bytes = 434 -> SEVEN lines;
  vertex: 32-byte prefix + 2,048 bytes = 2,080 -> THIRTY-THREE lines;
  combined: 40 lines = 2,560 fetched bytes.

A fully line-aligned maximum-size job takes 6+32=38 lines, or 2,432 bytes.
Neither traffic value is universal. Zero-length streams fetch zero lines.
Preserve the reference's count/alignment/bounds refusal order, including
validation of unused offsets where it is currently required. [R4,R5]

Form absolute addresses in widened arithmetic, adding the pool base exactly
once. Validate the full issued line against the permitted arena, not only useful
payload bytes followed by an unauthorized rounded overfetch. The current aligned
pool helps; that fact must remain checked if the allocation changes.

10.2 Compact useful words during fill
-------------------------------------
The old buffers keep full line prefixes/suffixes. The new writer can discard
whole unused words and store the logical index/vertex streams beginning at
bank offset zero. Both allowed alignments are multiples of eight bytes, so
prefix removal does not need a general byte-shift network.

For each returning 64-bit word at absolute address B:
  keep it if it belongs to the useful stream's word range;
  write at (B-stream_start)/8 in the reserved bank;
  discard whole prefix/suffix words;
  keep the exact useful byte count for a final partial index word.

Vertices are full 32-byte records. Indices may end partway through a word;
unused bytes are never indices merely because they were transferred. Bounds
and counts, not resetting all RAM payload, prevent exposure of stale padding.

Per compact bank:
  vertex = 256 x 64-bit words = 2,048 bytes;
  index = 48 x 64-bit words = 384 bytes, at most 378 meaningful.

Allocate 64 index words/bank to simplify bank/address concatenation and bounded
adjacent reads. That allocation does not raise the 126-triangle format limit.

10.3 Two-bank M10K-first prototype
---------------------------------

  vertex_ram[512] of 64 bits
    address {bank, vertex_word[7:0]}
    one fill write and one consumer read
    candidate packing: FOUR M10Ks

  index_ram_A[128] of 64 bits
  index_ram_B[128] of 64 bits
    address {bank, index_word[5:0]}
    identical writes; independent reads for triplet word and next word
    candidate packing: TWO M10Ks per copy, FOUR total

Total payload-store candidate: EIGHT M10Ks. Metadata, adapter and output staging
are additional. This is a supported width/depth/port packing calculation, not
an inferred/fitted count. Simple-dual-port and true-dual-port have different
available widths and must not be interchanged in a budget. [V1,V2]

Why index copies remain: two independent reads plus simultaneous filling is
three memory operations. Replication gives each physical RAM one write/one
read. The old code already uses this technique; preserve it when adding banks.

Why not immediately read whole 256-bit vertices in one clock: the width may
force more physical slices and output registers despite a small total bit count.
The four-word reader has a clear bandwidth bound and a smaller port. Measure
whether a wider reader buys useful throughput before paying for it.

10.4 Possible low incremental M10K cost
---------------------------------------
The old raw vertex buffer has 264 words because alignment crosses the 256-word
threshold. A straightforward 64-bit-wide M10K implementation can already need
four blocks. Two compact banks total 512 words and can also need four.

Each old 56-word index replica can need two blocks. Each combined 128-word
two-bank replica can still need two. Thus ordinary packing of these old/new
payload stores can be eight blocks in BOTH cases.

This is a promising hypothesis, not a measured zero-cost promise. Quartus may
split tails into MLABs, choose different shapes or add staging. Compare named
RAM summaries. The defensible statement is that doubling logical banks does
not necessarily double physical RAM when padding crossed a depth boundary.

10.5 Handoff is a pointer, not a copy
------------------------------------
Fill directly into the reserved bank. READY-to-SERVING changes a bank handle
and generation. Do not implement ping-pong by copying a complete meshlet from
staging into a consumer buffer; that adds the traffic/ports we are trying to
avoid.


11. TWO-BANK ASSETFETCH ARCHITECTURE
===================================

11.1 Bank state machine
-----------------------
  FREE -> RESERVED -> FILLING -> READY -> SERVING -> DRAINING -> FREE
                          \-> FAULT/DRAINING ------------------/

RESERVED owns the captured descriptor and destination. FILLING owns every
outstanding return obligation. READY means all required useful data is present
and validated. SERVING means downstream accepted that bank's descriptor.
DRAINING covers pending reads/returns after completion or cancellation.

Use a two-entry ordered bank-descriptor queue. Prepare B while consuming A,
but do not let a later smaller meshlet bypass an older one if submission order
has visible raster/blend effects. An occupied READY bank cannot be overwritten.
When both banks are owned, m_ready falls.

11.2 Capture at admission
-------------------------
Capture source/caller ID, vertex BYTE offset, index BYTE offset, counts,
validated absolute line bases/counts/prefixes, bank/generation, and the actual
frame/resource identity required by the production caller.

Prefer a trusted fixed ENGINE1 identity at the production adapter. A generic
leaf-test m_client input can remain, but it must be latched once, never read
live later.

Keep byte units separate from ASSEMBLE's base VERTEX ID. The assembler adds a
local u8 index to a base ID; this is not a 32-byte-record address offset. Name
internal signals accordingly and do not repair a units bug by adding the pool
base at both ends.

11.3 One fill engine, one logical request in flight
---------------------------------------------------
The fill sequencer chooses the oldest reserved bank needing data and reserves
its exact destination before requesting a line. It records stream kind, line
number, useful word range and expected packed beat count. Index-then-vertex
fill order can remain for first equivalence.

Start with ONE logical geometry memory request in flight. A queue of future
requests or a second reserved bank is not multiple outstanding response state.
Do not add more outstanding requests until measurement justifies the extra
routing/credit complexity.

The engine switches banks only between complete logical requests. Scanout can
interrupt physical burst service through the existing arbiter, but the logical
line's destination and remaining return count do not change.

11.4 Exact beat acceptance
--------------------------
A 64-byte asset line needs eight packed 64-bit words. Assert:
  no beat without an owned request;
  no extra beat after completion;
  last matches the expected count;
  no READY on an early/truncated last;
  write index remains inside the reserved bank;
  request and bank generations match.

Do not make beat_last alone authority for arbitrary received length. Early last
is truncation; late/extra data is a protocol fault. Old RAM contents cannot
supply missing words of a supposedly complete vertex.

Raw return data cannot wait for an arbitrary downstream consumer. Reserve the
bank/elastic destination before issue. If an input skid is added, prove its
capacity against the actual non-backpressurable burst, not average traffic.

11.5 Concurrent serving and fill
--------------------------------
Handoff descriptor/bank/generation stays stable until accepted. Readers then
use that captured serving identity, not the newest READY or currently filling
bank. Normal operation writes B while reading A; assert no fill writes to a
SERVING bank rather than relying on read-during-write settings to mask errors.

The second bank only buys useful work when the next descriptor is available
and the current bank is not held longer than required. Sections 13/14 close
those two ends of the pipeline.


12. SHARED ENGINE1 ADAPTER AND ACTUAL MEMORY PROTOCOL
====================================================

12.1 Keep the physical client law
---------------------------------
Arbiter slot 3 is ENGINE1; slot 4 is DEBUG because the slot index is cast to the
client enum. The guard permits the asset pool for ENGINE1, not DEBUG. A spare
slot is not a spare geometry privilege. [R9,R10]

Recommended production route:

  MESHFETCH descriptors ----+
                            +--> logical geometry adapter --> one ENGINE1 guard
  ASSETFETCH payloads ------+                                    |
                                                         arbiter slot 3
                                                               |
                                                         SDRAM controller
                                                               |
                                                  physical-owner return routing
                                                               |
                                                    logical-owner return routing
                                                       /                \
                                                descriptor sink      bank fill

This retains the existing physical client/scanout/refresh laws. It adds modest
upstream sharing and explicit logical ownership, not another controller or
unbounded crossbar.

12.2 Three acceptance boundaries
---------------------------------
Distinguish adapter acceptance, guard acceptance/verdict, and arbiter/controller
grant/data completion. Guard ready is a level; ok/violation is a delayed pulse.
Preserve S_VERD rather than requiring ready&&ok simultaneously. [R4,R7,R9]

A guard OK is not returned data. An arbiter grant is not a completed line.
The adapter retains the logical owner through the full return or explicit
drain/fault. Its selected request remains stable while the guard is blocked.

12.3 Two burst scales, not one
------------------------------
Geometry requests use BYTES:
  ASSETFETCH: 64 bytes -> eight packed 64-bit words;
  MESHFETCH: 32 bytes -> four packed 64-bit words.

Physical SDRAM req.len uses 16-bit WORDS, up to eight per physical burst:
  one full physical burst = 16 bytes;
  one 64-byte logical line = four full physical bursts;
  one 32-byte descriptor = two physical bursts.

The arbiter performs this splitting and may interleave other clients between
bursts. Do not release a geometry owner after the first physical credit return.
Do not count SCANOUT words as part of the pending geometry line. [R10,R11]

Keep MESHFETCH's existing 32-byte checked footprint. Do not normalize it to
an unchecked 64-byte read just to simplify the adapter. Store expected return
count so both lengths work. Any existing geometry beat/last generator assuming
eight words must be generalized and tested for four/eight, not bypassed by
fetching a neighboring descriptor.

12.4 Physical owner versus logical owner
----------------------------------------
The shell routes packed read data using physical ownership captured at the
controller grant. The geometry adapter separately records whether ENGINE1's
current logical request belongs to MESHFETCH or a particular ASSETFETCH bank.

Record BEFORE issue:
  descriptor/payload kind;
  transaction/source ID;
  bank/generation if payload;
  stream/line/write offset;
  expected and received packed word count;
  cancellation/fault state.

A physical scanout burst between two asset bursts must not reset the logical
asset record. A later queued geometry request must not capture the earlier
request's return. One logical request in flight makes this proof simple.

12.5 Fairness and timing
-------------------------
Use small request queues and round-robin arbitration at logical-request
boundaries. A bounded descriptor preference may maintain lookahead, but cannot
starve payload filling. A long asset job cannot monopolize the adapter until
all of its lines finish if the next descriptor is needed.

Preserve the physical arbiter's scanout/aging law and controller refresh.
The conservative controller's full-read grant-to-grant spans are 12/15/18 clocks
for row hit/miss/conflict, before additional contention. These are simulation
profile laws, not board performance. Four physical bursts are genuine work for
a 64-byte line; a 360-cycle multi-line fetch is not 360 removable control cycles.
[R11]


13. MESHFETCH LOOKAHEAD AND PRODUCTION HIERARCHY
==============================================

13.1 Fix late metadata first
----------------------------
SOURCE: ASSETFETCH's later guard request reads m_client_i. MESHFETCH's later
request reads m_client_i AND m_addr_i after its earlier admission/LOD/cull work.
[R4,R7]

Counterexample: accept A, then producer moves to B while A is delayed. A's
later guard operation can fetch B's address or use B's client. A bench holding
all input pins constant hides it; prefetching exposes it.

Capture accepted fields, or use a fixed trusted physical client where that is
the production contract. Poison address/client immediately after acceptance
in the targeted regression. The memory request must still describe A.

13.2 Bounded lookahead
-----------------------
Queue eligible meshlet descriptors before the current bank is released.
At least one next prepared descriptor is necessary; two pending descriptors
are a reasonable starting bound if resource lifetimes support it.

Preserve LOD/cull behavior. Do not fetch discarded payloads merely to keep the
port busy. A descriptor can be examined ahead of payload-bank admission, but
queued work keeps its frame/configuration/resource ownership.

Measure simultaneous consumer work and opposite-bank fill. Two arrays and a
hand-drawn overlap diagram do not establish that the next descriptor actually
arrives in time.

13.3 Internalize the production route
--------------------------------------
D22 currently puts MESHFETCH, ASSETFETCH, VDECODE and ASSEMBLE beside the shell
in the bench, using exposed ports to reach real memory. That is useful test
composition, not the final production hierarchy. [R12,R24]

The final step moves the selected chain and adapter inside zhao_shell_top or
its selected production subhierarchy. Retire harness-only memory/payload
bypasses from production; keep them only in named diagnostic wrappers.

Update prod_manifest and generated resource top: newly nested blocks must not
also remain separately counted roots. Conversely, do not exclude them from
the census while their supposed replacement is disconnected. Count selected
implementations exactly once. [R21]

Do this in an isolated source snapshot, not by editing transitive files of a
running fit. Independent implementation is possible while a fit runs; starving
or changing the experiment underneath Quartus is not.

13.4 Configuration units and versions
--------------------------------------
Transforms/LOD parameters may be captured per job or referenced through an
immutable versioned frame configuration, according to the existing scope.
No queued request may consult configuration that can change behind it.

Bank tags and logical request generations can be internal sideband. Do not
invent a new public asset format or game behavior as part of a fetch optimization.
If a necessary resource version is genuinely absent from the current ABI,
record the gap explicitly instead of inferring it from an address.


14. INDEX/VERTEX CONSUMERS AND BANK RELEASE
==========================================

14.1 ASSEMBLE's request is not automatically a queue
----------------------------------------------------
The assembler asserts an index request while waiting in its fetch state.
Increasing response latency without adapting the protocol can admit a held
request repeatedly and deliver extra stale responses into later triangles.
[R8]

Preferred internal interface: request valid/ready and response valid/ready,
carrying triangle index/bank generation, one outstanding initially.

Compatibility shim: accept one legacy request episode, capture its index,
issue RAM reads once, return one response pulse, and do not reaccept that held
episode until the requester advances/deasserts according to the contract.

Do not stretch signals in a fake bench until one waveform happens to work.
A release barrier includes all outstanding index reads and responses.

14.2 Index reads
-----------------
For triangle t, compact byte offset b=3*t. Read word floor(b/8) from replica A
and, when straddling, the following word from B. Capture b's low selector with
the RAM addresses; extract the bytes after those synchronous responses arrive.

If the triplet does not straddle, mask the irrelevant second word to zero.
Do not require initialized padding or read into the next bank. Validate t
against triangle_count; preserve ASSEMBLE's local-index versus vertex-count
checks. Correct fetching does not legalize an invalid triangle.

14.3 Vertex reads
------------------
A vertex is four consecutive 64-bit words. A pipelined reader can approach one
record per four read clocks after startup with a ready consumer. Retain the
old reader rate first if that isolates the bank-overlap improvement.

For overlap between assembling the next vertex and holding the previous one,
reserve an assembly register and separate held output/skid. This can cost about
512 staging bits before tags/control. Charge them. One 256-bit register cannot
simultaneously retain a stalled vertex and collect the next one.

Read tickets carry bank generation, vertex index and word number. Complete
record/tag stays stable under output stall. Never select the output source using
the fill engine's current bank.

14.4 Release needs both consumers
---------------------------------
Vertex production may end before index assembly, or vice versa. Keep the bank
until all consumers that may read it are done and all pending reader responses
have drained. Treat last data ACCEPTED, not merely presented, as progress.

Initially preserve the external release operation with a coordinator guaranteeing
that it means both consumers are finished. A later derived done-ack mechanism
is possible, but cannot be guessed from v_ready or a single counter.
Zero-count jobs need explicit correct termination rather than waiting for a
last vertex/triangle that will never exist.


15. CANCELLATION, RESOURCE VERSIONS AND FAULTS
=============================================

15.1 Early teardown
--------------------
If early release is allowed, distinguish cancellation from ordinary completion:
  stop issuing new work for that bank;
  mark pending outputs cancelled at the agreed boundary;
  retain ownership while accepted memory returns and RAM reads drain;
  discard those old-generation returns;
  reuse the bank only when no old write can target it.

A generation bit is not permission to route untagged old bus data according
to the new bank selection. The adapter keeps the old logical owner until the
whole return stream finishes. Generation checking supplements ownership.

15.2 Resource pins
-------------------
Copying bytes into a bank does not remove source-lifetime obligations for queued
and outstanding external reads. Preserve frame/resource residency pins. A bank
release does not by itself authorize global asset-page recycling.

The conservative compatible policy retains the frame's resource ownership until
its required reads/consumers finish. Earlier per-resource release needs explicit
outstanding-reference accounting and its own tests.

15.3 Fault taxonomy
--------------------
Keep separate:
  malformed count/alignment/footprint;
  guard violation;
  capacity backpressure;
  unexpected/truncated/extra data;
  stale generation or cancellation;
  out-of-range index;
  internal duplicate completion/impossible ownership.

Preserve existing refusal precedence. Do not return zero-filled vertices as
success or conflate a full bank with malformed input. A failed job needs a
defined terminal event so its owner can clean up.

A physical timeout is not proof that no more untagged data can arrive. Follow
the platform drain/abort/reset law; no timer may immediately recycle the bank
while an old burst can still write it.

15.4 Reference versus cycle model
----------------------------------
Material equations remain in zref_material.hpp; byte/footprint truth stays in
zref_assetfetch.hpp. Small scheduler/bank models specify conservation and order.
RTL tests prove timing/handshake behavior; composed memory tests prove the actual
return protocol.

Do not force the software renderer to simulate every clock merely to remain
an arithmetic oracle. Conversely, a scalar helper is not proof of a correct
ready/valid protocol or production frame rate.


16. ASSETFETCH PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT
======================================

Preserve prefetch_stall_o for comparison, but document its exact predicate.
Measure distinct quantities rather than one misleading stall total:

  descriptor_wait: next useful work lacks its descriptor;
  guard_wait: captured request awaits acceptance/verdict;
  memory_wait: accepted logical request lacks returned data;
  consumer_starved: an actual requested item cannot be supplied;
  consumer_blocked: item exists but downstream is not ready;
  overlap_cycles: useful A consumption overlaps B fill;
  ready_bank_wait and no_free_bank;
  useful bytes versus transferred bytes;
  descriptor bytes separately from payload bytes;
  maximum occupancy and appropriately sampled wait distributions.

Do not allocate a huge new diagnostic block in the critical path. Use registered
events and a supported existing measurement interface where possible; otherwise
charge the explicit counter wrapper.

Keep the one-triangle D22 test, but compare old/new paths with hundreds of
meshlets, multiple bank handoffs and identical byte/memory workloads:
  balanced F/C, fill-dominated and consume-dominated cases;
  all legal alignment classes and varying sizes;
  alternating views/instances/material tags;
  output pauses and cancellation;
  continuous scanout, row hits/conflicts and normal refresh;
  enough bank reuse to wrap narrow indices repeatedly.

Report cold-start separately from steady state. Require exact bytes, no
cross-bank/unauthorized traffic, exact drain and preserved scanout/refresh.
Require actual overlap witnesses and compare cycles/meshlet, useful throughput
and traffic. A warmer/smaller test is not a buffering speedup.

A synthetic controlled F/C trace should approach max(F,C) under the assumptions;
a production-like mixed trace must demonstrate benefit or state why it does not.
The optional four-word reader must actually sustain one record/four clocks when
resident and unblocked, while preserving identity on every pause.

18. COMBINE TEST AND MUTATION PLAN
==================================

18.1 Arithmetic
----------------
Compile the real current zref_material.hpp into the differential. Test every
recipe, zero/insufficient/required samples, RGB and alpha independently, with
0,1,127,128,254,255, both LERP directions, weights 0/255, rounding boundaries,
alpha-only saturation, black opaque masks and varied third-sample alpha.

Exhaustive software-kernel checks are cheap enough for all products and all
LERP input triples. RTL can use broad random coverage plus carefully selected
exhaustive subdomains if the full product is too slow. State actual RTL coverage;
do not borrow the software exhaustive count for the hardware.

Named arithmetic mutations:
  /255 instead of /256;
  biased negative signed rounding;
  LERP alpha multiplied instead of interpolated;
  MODULATE2X alpha not doubled;
  DETAIL_LIGHT losing its intermediate rounding;
  DETAIL_MASK alpha using s1 instead of s2;
  MASK testing RGB instead of alpha;
  saturation counted per channel;
  phase 1 overwriting a scratch value still needed in phase 2;
  one lane associated with the wrong context.

Each must fail a targeted, nonvacuous check. "Nonblack" is not an arithmetic
oracle, and testing alpha only at 255 cannot close the alpha law.

18.2 Transaction pressure
--------------------------
Drive thousands of fragments, repeated sequence wrap and sink stalls longer
than all pipeline latencies. Mix simple and dependent recipes, distinct samples,
changing weights, delayed services and invalid-input poison after acceptance.

Require the old 65th-completion failure to be prevented by admission backpressure,
then drain every accepted fragment exactly in order. Assert stable offered
output, no phantom RCP admissions, no double completion and conservation across
NEW/CONT/pipeline/DONE/output/FREE.

Include duplicate caller tags intentionally. Unless the contract requires unique
caller tags, the scoreboard must use admission order/owner identity rather than
a map which merges equal user tags.

Reset/abort tests must state drain versus cancellation. A stale old-generation
return must not corrupt new work. If safety instead relies on quiescence, prove
that boundary rather than having both sides assume the other drains.

18.3 Exact composed samples
----------------------------
The current test's accepting either of two palette colours is not proof that
EACH fragment chose its own correct address byte. Four equal texels cannot
reveal incorrect bilinear weights. Preserve these simple directed cases but
add an address-sensitive oracle. [R13]

Use the real texture/TMU reference with texels varying by x, y, mip level,
channel and binding. Compute exact expected addresses, sample values and final
material RGBA per admission. Include odd/even CLUT addresses, nonuniform
bilinear values, varying fu/fv, cache stalls, distinct logical samples, AUX,
and nonzero mips where implemented.

A still-unimplemented path is explicitly pending/red, not quietly excluded
from a claim that the complete island is exact.

18.4 Structure and observability
---------------------------------
Guard against more than two product sites, truncated handles, record-by-job
priority scans returning, reset-written payloads, unintended extra RAM ports,
unreserved always-ready completion, and unheld output selection.

Mutation-test static guardrails too. A style checker passing is still not the
mapped memory/multiplier report. Counters need event-latency and once-per-fragment
checks as well as total arithmetic.


19. ASSETFETCH TEST AND MUTATION PLAN
====================================

19.1 Exact byte oracle
----------------------
Fill memory with address/resource-dependent data, not identical records in both
banks. Compute descriptor bytes, index triples and vertex records from the
reference layout. Compare every item and identity, not only painted pixels.

Cover every permitted line-prefix class, maximum/zero counts, straddling index
triples and arena ends. Preserve the 40-line worst case and check compaction
without changing useful bytes or external addresses.

Malformed cases: oversize counts, misalignment, widened-address overflow,
base added twice, illegal client/resource, early/late/missing/extra words,
response with no owner. Preserve old refusal precedence for old fault classes.

19.2 Actual memory protocol
----------------------------
Test both requesters against actual MEM.GUARD, then the shared adapter against
actual arbiter/controller/model plus scanout. A reasoned MESHFETCH handshake
repair is not a real-memory MESHFETCH test until it actually reads descriptors.

Drive simultaneous logical clients; alternate 32-byte descriptors and 64-byte
payload lines; block guard acceptance; deliver a later OK or violation; interleave
scanout between physical bursts; exercise refresh/row conflicts. Require logical
owners to survive whole requests and forbid cross-client return contamination.

Poison address/client after request A is accepted while B is offered. The later
request and data must still belong to A. A bench preserving A's pins indefinitely
has not tested this contract.

19.3 Bank stress and release
-----------------------------
Fill B while A serves. Pause vertex output at every word/record boundary and
pause ASSEMBLE on straddling indices. Fill both banks, block consumers, then
verify READY waiting and upstream backpressure without overwrite.

Finish one consumer before the other; the bank remains owned. Cancel at first/
last line, pending guard verdict, active physical burst, pending RAM response
and held vertex. Reuse only after the documented drain and reject stale events.

Named mutations:
  live client/address instead of captured;
  return routed by current fill bank;
  release on presented rather than accepted final vertex;
  release while indices remain outstanding;
  early last accepted as complete;
  index prefix retained or vertex base added twice;
  held index request admitted repeatedly;
  adjacent-word read crosses bank boundary;
  logical owner released at first physical credit return;
  descriptor silently treated as 64 bytes;
  fake poisoned pool used instead of SDRAM.

Require actual witnesses: both banks reused, both request classes granted,
both consumers stalled, overlap occurred, a denied request really hit the guard,
and narrow indices wrapped. These accompany exact outputs; they do not replace
them.

characterization, alongside the true production leaf. Do not remove boundary
paths solely to advertise a higher number or blame math for artificial virtual-
pin constraints without classification.

Classify BOTH endpoints: input/register/output, internal registers, clock/CDC/
reset. Then trace the actual worst internal nodes. An endpoint named counter
or queue is not a complete explanation of how its path got long.

The leaf's 125 MHz is headroom; the product is 100 MHz. Preserve the existing
V2 complete-composition 105 MHz floor/110 MHz objective where applicable.
A passing isolated leaf is not a whole-console frequency or capacity proof.

20.3 Hold cannot be ignored
----------------------------
The reported -5.284 ns hold shortfall is not fixed just by lowering the clock.
Examine launch/capture edges, clock relations, I/O constraints and minimum-delay
routes. Boundary artifacts need demonstrated classification and validation of
the real registered boundary. Internal hold needs physical closure.

No blanket false paths, no timing pass based only on setup, and no "Fitter
successful" headline substituting for setup/hold acceptance.

20.4 Fail cheaply before placement
-----------------------------------
Elaborate and map first. Reject reset-flop payloads, extra RAM ports, missing
banks or unexpected DSP inference before an expensive fit. Then run one clean
meaningful variant, read its path, and select the next change for a reason.
LOGIC2/DSP2 is a bounded decision experiment, not permission for endless seeds.

20.5 Operational isolation
---------------------------
One expensive fit at a time on the constrained host. No nine creature-video
renders, full-shell simulation swarm or concurrent full build. Source review
and small isolated model work can continue.

Fit immutable snapshots. Preserve logs/workspaces on timeout. Distinguish
watchdog interruption, process failure, completed timing failure and resource
failure. Check the actual build exit status before executing a binary; a filtered
log pipeline is not proof that linking succeeded.

Mutations run in isolated copies or committed/backed-up state. Restoring a test
must not erase the uncommitted fix being assessed.

22. REJECTED SHORTCUTS AND FALLBACK CHOICES
==========================================

Reject two whole fetchers instead of two payload banks, a new memory controller,
or treating DEBUG as another geometry client.

Reject a single context which waits serially through every pipeline stage while
advertising two-products-per-clock because it contains two multipliers.

Reject finite order restoration without admission credits, constant 1x1/equal-
texel tests as the only colour evidence, or counters instead of exact bytes.

Reject changed alpha/rounding/sample semantics to ease fitting, blanket multiplier
mapping changes, weakened scanout/refresh, relaxed clocks or waived hold.

Reject passing an old counter-inclusive budget by measuring a counter-free core.
An explicit telemetry redesign is possible, but it must retain observable events
and charge physical cost somewhere real.

Reasonable measured fallbacks:
  DSP2 instead of LOGIC2 with identical arithmetic/schedule;
  existing vertex reader if bank overlap already solves useful starvation;
  compact single-bank low-area mode for workloads that do not benefit;
  small registered MLAB scratch/tag storage if actually better;
  explicit local rebudget based on measured whole-island totals and approval.

Stop when services are correct, bounded, integrated and meet measured capacity/
resource/timing acceptance. Do not spend another week polishing optional leaf
variants after the path is good enough for terrain development.


23. EXECUTED LOCAL VALIDATION
==============================

The following were run during preparation. They are proposal-level tests, NOT
repository RTL simulation, Quartus, physical memory or a full renderer run.
The workspace reset during packaging; the source/results are rebuilt in the
companion bundle and rerun where provided. The recorded conclusions remain
bounded to the following models.

23.1 Arithmetic
----------------
A C++17 program transcribed the pinned scalar equations and independently
expressed the paired-phase calculation. It reported:
  65,536 byte products;
  16,777,216 LERP triples;
  800,000 randomized recipe/count/refusal cases;
  zero mismatches.

It includes correct alpha, saturation flags, count-zero precedence and two
software-only invalid recipe values. This checks the proposal against a
transcription, not the original header; the implementation gate MUST compile
the actual current zref_material.hpp.

23.2 Owner credits
-------------------
A transition model reproduced the uncredited shape with 65 completions and
zero sink transfers: held head changed from sequence 0 to 64.

The credited model accepted/emitted 8,192 items, under random completion delay,
long repeated sink stalls and 128 index wraps, with zero loss/duplication/order
errors, max live=64 and stable held output. The initial run took 17,479 model
cycles and blocked admission for 9,173 cycles. These are abstract model cycles,
not the current island's measured runtime.

23.3 Queue feasibility
------------------------
Eight contexts, five-cycle phase return, fair NEW/CONT selection, one completion
consumer, 12,000 fragments per homogeneous test:
  PASSTHRU:     12,007 cycles, 12,000 tickets,      0 products;
  MODULATE:     24,009 cycles, 24,000 tickets, 48,000 products;
  DETAIL_LIGHT: 36,009 cycles, 36,000 tickets, 72,000 products;
  mixed:        20,980 cycles, 20,969 tickets, 32,888 products.

Thus the phase graph itself permits nearly one ticket/clock and two products/
clock for product-heavy streams. Real RAM timing, queue implementation, input
availability, output stalls and Fmax remain RTL/physical acceptance questions.

23.4 Footprint compaction
--------------------------
The model checked 1,146 per-stream count/alignment combinations: all vertex
counts with both prefixes, all triangle counts with all eight prefixes, every
useful record/triplet against address-dependent bytes. Zero mismatches.
Maximums were 7 index lines, 33 vertex lines, 2,560 transferred and 2,426 useful
bytes. Eight M10Ks remains a packing candidate, not an inference result.

23.5 Reproduce
---------------
Python uses the standard library/fixed seeds; C++ needs a C++17 compiler:
  python validate_models.py
  g++ -std=c++17 -O2 -Wall -Wextra -Werror check_math.cpp -o check_math
  ./check_math
Only run the binary after successful compilation. No binary is required in the
bundle; source and recorded output suffice.


24. ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST AND ASSESSMENT
=======================================

The implementing agent should return source/parameters, reproduced defects,
exact tested inputs, mutations detected, pressure/wrap/cancel coverage, useful
throughput, named RAM ports, complete resources including diagnostics, setup/
hold/unconstrained-path status, raw reports and remaining decisions.

The non-negotiable checklist:
  [ ] 65th completion cannot overwrite an unretired first result.
  [ ] No phantom RCP admission; global credits release only at final sink accept.
  [ ] Internal reorder allowed; external order exact; output held stable.
  [ ] Eight current material laws including alpha remain byte-exact.
  [ ] Two product sites, selected mapping verified, counters honestly charged.
  [ ] No issue-path record/job scan or unimplemented RAM port.
  [ ] Both fetchers preserve accepted address/client/configuration.
  [ ] Both descriptor/payload requesters traverse actual guarded memory.
  [ ] ENGINE1 sharing preserves SCANOUT and logical multi-burst ownership.
  [ ] Exact 32/64-byte returns; no early bank publication or stale overwrite.
  [ ] Reader bank identity held; index request not duplicated by a held signal.
  [ ] Both consumers and outstanding responses finish before release/reuse.
  [ ] Compaction covers all legal alignments; real A-consume/B-fill measured.
  [ ] Source-matched setup/hold/resources; production roots counted once.

COMBINE's expected gain is regularity: one queue-head decision, one context read,
two products and one whole writeback. Its tiny mathematical kernels do not need
the current general job-selection structure. A much more credible 100 MHz
implementation is plausible with this organization; 100/125 MHz and a particular
ALM total are NOT guaranteed before mapping/placement.

ASSETFETCH's gain is overlap and packing. Two 2.4 KiB-class payload banks are a
reasonable bounded mechanism, with a plausible path to low incremental M10K use.
They do not erase external traffic, burst timing or scanout/refresh costs.

Budget expectations are proposals:
  COMBINE: bounded RAM and pipeline registers replace broad scan/state logic;
           the inherited 500-register counter-inclusive target may need an
           explicit telemetry decision or measured rebudget, never a silent pass.
  Owner:   replace the muxed table with reserved synchronous storage, rather
           than adding another duplicate table.
  Fetch:   eight-M10K candidate payload layout plus explicit adapter/control/
           output staging; no extra arithmetic DSP is inherently necessary.

No new complete-console budget was established here. Preserve the reserve-
conscious whole-machine budget and do not add overlapping stale leaf numbers.

The work is concrete and bounded: close credits and metadata, replace the
combiner's scans with paired phases, give geometry two owned banks and one
properly shared memory client, test exact results under pressure, then measure.
It does not require discarding the visual features or inventing another console.
