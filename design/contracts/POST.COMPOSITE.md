# Contract — POST.COMPOSITE (Compositor)

> Ledger: `design/blocks.yml` · owner ZH-070 · phase 11 · maturity SPECIFIED

## Purpose and exclusions

Bloom, haze, shockwave, refraction, grading, palette and flash composition of 3D frame + planes + sprites into the scanout framebuffer.

## Clock and reset semantics
Single `gpu_clk`, synchronous active-low `rst_n`. Reset abandons work in flight
and clears the effect buffers; they are rebuilt every frame, so nothing durable
is lost.

## Input and output packet layouts
### The compositor order — FROZEN, owner ruling 2026-08-31 §4

    1. resolve the 3D world; composite the two restricted world-plane slots
    2. build ONE bounded displacement field from refraction + shockwave + heat
       haze — contributions combine BEFORE sampling, and are clamped
    3. sample world colour, glow source AND the exterior-ink mask through the
       SAME displaced coordinates
    4. atmospheric haze / fog-sheet composition
    5. blur and add bloom/glow
    6. palette phase/remap, then the generated colour-grading transform
       (may be fused into one generated lookup if the result is exact)
    7. full-screen flash/tint
    8. exterior ink, LAST on the world image
    9. HUD sprites, text, debug overlays

**Order is visible.** Grading before bloom and grading after bloom are different
pictures, and no amount of reading the block list would reveal which was
intended. This section is therefore the contract's most load-bearing paragraph.

Three details in it are decisions, not sequencing:

* **ONE displacement field, sampled once** (step 2–3). The ruling says
  explicitly: *do not repeatedly resample the framebuffer once per effect.* Three
  effects each resampling would cost three framebuffer reads and would let them
  disagree about where a pixel came from.
* **Glow and ink are sampled through the SAME displaced coordinates** as the
  world (step 3). So refraction bends the world, its glow and its outline
  **coherently** — an outline that stayed put while the creature bent would read
  as a printing error.
* **Ink is overlaid last, after the flash** (step 8). *"The default flash does
  not wash out the line; changing that later is an explicit artistic mode, not
  an accidental consequence of stage order."*

| mode | effect buffer |
|---|---|
| Z60 | 96 × 60 |
| Storm | 80 × 60 |
| Duo | 128 × 60 |

**Quarter linear resolution**, frozen by the ruling.

## Backpressure rules
Ready/valid to the framebuffer writer. The chain is a fixed sequence of
full-screen passes; a stall holds the whole chain, which is safe because no
stage holds state that expires.

## Memory ownership
Reads the resolved framebuffer and `POST.GATHER`'s three planes; writes the
scanout framebuffer.

The upscale from quarter-resolution planes to full resolution happens **here**,
at sample time, not in `POST.GATHER` — keeping the planes small is the point of
their being quarter resolution, and upscaling early would discard the saving.

## Q formats and rounding
Blend arithmetic reuses the frozen blend law; this block introduces no new
colour mathematics.

Palette remap and colour grading **may be fused into one generated lookup, if
the result is exact** — the ruling permits the fusion and requires the
exactness, so the table is generated by `fixgen` and proved against the
unfused evaluation rather than assumed equivalent.

Displacement sampling is **nearest**, with the same truncation as `TWOD.PLANE`,
so a displaced sample cannot invent a colour that was not in the frame.

## Latency (fixed or variable)
Fixed per pixel within each pass; the frame cost is the sum of the passes.

## Target throughput
One pixel per clock per pass. The chain is **five full-screen passes** over the
world image (displaced sample, haze, bloom, grade+flash, ink).

At Z60's 92,160 pixels that is ~461,000 clocks — **~35 % of a 1,333,333-clock
frame.** That is the largest single consumer named in this contract set, and it
is the number that decides whether passes must be fused. It should be measured
before anyone assumes the renderer owns the frame.

## Overflow and malformed-input behaviour
| condition | behaviour |
|---|---|
| displacement points outside the frame | clamp to the edge, count. Wrapping would sample the opposite side of the screen, which is a spectacular and very confusing artefact |
| a gather plane is absent | treat as zero and count; the frame still composites |
| grading table missing | pass through ungraded, count. A wrong grade is worse than no grade |

* `bloom_cells_contributing`

## Scalar reference function
`zref::PostComposite` (ledger). Owns the nine-step order, the single displacement
field, the coherent glow/ink sampling and the fusion-exactness requirement.

## Directed tests
`tests/post/post_composite_directed.cpp`.

* **the order is asserted**, not assumed: a frame with both grading and bloom,
  compared against a reference that applies them in the opposite order, must
  differ — proving the test can actually see the ordering it claims to check;
* **coherent sampling**: a refracting surface over a glowing outlined creature —
  world, glow and ink all displace together. Assert the ink follows the world,
  which is the case that would look like a printing error if it broke;
* **flash does not wash out ink**: a full-intensity flash frame still shows the
  line, per the ruling;
* three displacement contributions combine before sampling and are clamped once,
  not three times;
* fused grading+palette equals unfused evaluation, **exactly**;
* displacement off the frame edge clamps, does not wrap.

## Randomized differential tests
`tests/post/post_composite_random.cpp`, RTL against `zref::PostComposite`.

Random effect combinations at random intensities, biased toward **simultaneous**
effects — the single-effect cases are easy and the interactions are where order
and the single-field rule matter.

## Formal properties
`tests/formal/post_composite_order.sby`:

* **the framebuffer is sampled once per pixel per pass** — the ruling's
  no-repeated-resample rule, as a property;
* displacement is clamped before use, always;
* ink is applied after flash, for all inputs;
* handshake hygiene; reset clears staging.

## Synthesis / resource ceiling
Unbuilt. **Ceiling: 3,500 ALMs, 12 DSPs, ≤ 8 M10K.**

The largest of the post blocks, and the one whose *throughput* matters more than
its area — see the ~35 %-of-frame figure above.

## Integration capture cases
* **a Level-9 spell frame** — refraction, shockwave, bloom, flash and ink at
  once. The interaction case, and the ruling's stage order visible in a single
  image.
* **the ~35 % measurement** — five passes at Z60, measured rather than computed,
  because it decides whether passes must be fused.
* **Duo** — both views composited independently at 128 × 60 planes.
* **a flash frame with HUD** — HUD after everything, legible, per §3.2.

## Notes

Palette/grading tables are generated assets, never runtime-computed.
