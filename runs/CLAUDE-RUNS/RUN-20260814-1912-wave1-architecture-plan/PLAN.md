## 6. Risks and mitigations

| # | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | SV packed-struct field order (MSB-first packing) silently breaks byte-identity vs C/TS | The tri-language golden-binary matrix (W4) is the gate, not layout reasoning; emitter inverts order once, goldens catch any regression; `assert ($bits(...)==8*M)` in SV. |
