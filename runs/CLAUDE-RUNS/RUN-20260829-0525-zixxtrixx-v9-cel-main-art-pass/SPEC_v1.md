## Objective

Deliver and publish a refined cel-main Zixxtrixx whose outline remains proportional across real camera distances, whose lighting reads as smooth authored bands rather than polygons, whose cel texture is calmer but still handmade, whose head/neck/eyes match the concept more closely, and whose revised animations remain loose, forceful and stable. Preserve prior normal and experimental generations in an organized archive.

## Don't Retry

- Fixed five-pixel outline at every camera distance: it becomes a black mass far away.
- Per-triangle faceted cel as main: owner rejects the polygon discontinuities.
- Quantize vertex colours and interpolate them: interpolation rebuilds gradients while retaining triangle boundaries.
- Remove all cel texture: subtle handmade grain is still wanted.
- Inflate the nose to make a head: the nose must be smaller than the neck stretch behind it.
- Add an overlapping head/neck shell: it previously self-intersected and disconnected the tube.
- Draw a black line at the neck joint: only the exterior silhouette should receive outline.
- Tail-tip balance: supporting tail must lie flat.
- Replace the accepted old taunt: preserve it and add a new clip.
- Add high-frequency noise to fall or balance: that is twitch, not wobble.
- Salto impact against terrain alone: the owner explicitly wants target penetration and a stable hold.
- Dump every archive generation into one expanded panel.
