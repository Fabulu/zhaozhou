HOW TO READ THIS, AND WHAT NOT TO QUOTE FROM IT
-----------------------------------------------
BEFORE is the trustworthy block and it is the whole diagnosis: creature -0.684
px/frame, background -0.750, RELATIVE +0.066 (|mean| 0.147). The two operands
were measured independently and they AGREE -- the signature of a pure camera
pan, which is exactly what the tool's selftest leg A reproduces synthetically.
Screen motion with no speed in it.

AFTER: the creature figures are sound -- +0.425 px/frame of drift, 113.7 px of
span, 45/93 px edge margins, 0 frames touching an edge.

! DO NOT QUOTE THE AFTER BLOCK'S bg_dx OR relative_dx. It reports +0.133 for a
background that must be scrolling LEFT while the creature travels right, i.e.
the wrong SIGN. The cause is in the subject, not the tool: this staging's
terrain is a near-featureless brown gradient whose only strong feature is a
SCREEN-ANCHORED ordered dither, and an integer band correlator cannot track a
smooth ramp carrying a pattern that does not move. The BEFORE block survives it
because there the terrain and the creature move together, so the estimate lands
in the same place either way.

THE GROUND-RELATIVE RATE IS AUTHORED, NOT INFERRED, and does not need this
instrument. The root traverses 8.4 m of world along the screen-lateral axis.
Measured directly at follow 0 -- the configuration where the ground IS static
and bg_dx read +0.001 px/frame, which is the positive control proving the band
correlator was reading the ground and not the creature -- the creature crosses
at +2.177 px/frame at cam_k 200000, hence +3.05 px/frame at the shipping
cam_k 280000.
