   56| ## 4. Particles through the antenna: bounded attempt, shipped small
   57| 
   58| **Pre-layer route (plan's A/B): declined by construction, as the prep predicted.** Pre splats draw while the depth buffer holds only terrain, and then the creature composites over them (`creature_hook`), so every mote inside the silhouette is hidden, including motes genuinely in front. That flattens depth, the plan's own stop condition. `glow_splat` is confirmed to depth-test per pixel at the centre depth and never to write depth.
   59| 
   60| **Narrow candidate taken instead, using existing depth (no centreline model, collision or repulsion).** The "through" read is the one-frame flip that happens when a flat mote disc's *centre* crosses a stick's front surface. Antenna pixels come from depth the renderer already has: creature-covered (`depth != pre_depth`) but not body-covered (`u02_body_cover`). For fold and surge mote bodies only (`ManaSplat::surface_fade`), visibility ramps to zero with a smoothstep as the centre's view depth approaches that surface, within `kMoteSurfaceFadeMm`.
   61| 
   62| - **First cut rejected by reasoning:** a centre-pixel read would drop a mote in *one* frame as it slides sideways onto a stick at matching depth. The shipped form averages over the disc footprint instead (`kMoteSurfaceFadeTaps` = 5×5).
   63| - **Fade operands:** additive halo by gain; soft body by opacity; hard-opaque surge core drawn soft at opacity f (its gain would darken, not clear).
   64| - **Knobs:** `kMoteSurfaceFadeDefault = true`, `kMoteSurfaceFadeMm = 120`, `ZHAO_U02_MOTE_SURFACE_FADE=off|on`, `ZHAO_U02_MOTE_SURFACE_FADE_MM=1..2000`, all strict.
   65| - **Exact-off:** `off` equals the no-fade binary on 23/23 live+legacy subjects and 13/13 non-live subjects.
   66| - **Scope:** mana-bearing non-live diagnostics (fogprobe-mana, mist-parked, trio, mana-aqua/stack, manalab) now render under the fade. Archived media are stored bytes and are not re-rendered.
   67| - **Cost:** 25 depth reads per mote splat.
