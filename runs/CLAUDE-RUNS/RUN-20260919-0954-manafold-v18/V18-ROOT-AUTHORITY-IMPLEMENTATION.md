## Architecture fire-test and correction

The ratified first draft said to make the complete nominal Front/End swell half-widths rigid JunctionF/RearSocket cores. The gate arithmetic disproved that before the change shipped:

- nominal root half-widths `270/280 mm` would reduce the F–A/C–End translation runs to `270/680 mm`;
- legal compaction would leave `53/-27 mm`, violating the unchanged 80 mm floor and inverting C–End;
- End's nominal support reaches beyond the final sampled tail, leaving no outside-support ReturnTip blend.

No compaction bound, 80 mm floor, closure tolerance or profile value moved. The corrected mechanism derives sampled support from the exact production predicate `abs(station-at) < half`, gives every non-terminal supported ring one semantic rotation while keeping the exact version-17 translation gradient, and treats terminal rear ring 63 as the explicit buried ReturnTip-only exception.

## Production mechanism
