RECONCILIATION: the stated 5.2%/4.7% is reachable only if pixels that are
scanned but then rejected by the depth test are counted as free. The outer
aura is depth-tested against the conduit centre, and the body (~64 px across)
plus the terrain occlude a large share of its box -- reject ~35% of the outer
aura and you land almost exactly on the quoted 4.7% idle. That is a fair
BLEND-cost number and an optimistic FILL-cost number: the loop still visits
every pixel in the box and performs the compare.

VERDICT: the arithmetic is internally consistent for blend cost but understates
the work per conduit by roughly 1.4x (blended, before depth rejection) to 2x
(scanned). Honest figures:
  one conduit    ~7.4%% blended / ~10.3%% scanned   (claimed 5.2%%)
  three conduits ~22%%  blended / ~31%%   scanned   (claimed 15.5%%)
                 + one SHARED sky bloom + ONE glow-ramp rebuild per frame
                   (both of those claims are correct and verified in code:
                    the bloom is scene-level, the 64-entry ramp is per frame,
                    not per instance).

This does not by itself fail 'cheap enough for several on screen' -- ~31% of the
frame in a simple add-with-early-out loop may well be affordable -- but the
owner asked for the cost to be STATED, and the stated number is optimistic.
The knobs that move it are kCentreGlowRadiusPx (quadratic: 46 -> 36 would cut
the outer box by 39%) and kCentreGlowCorePx.
