FAULT EVIDENCE: the four-pointed pupil star renders BLACK, not cyan.
QA pass, 2026-09-04. Lossless .rgb frames read with tools/reel/rgbframe.py.

THE SHEET: all three concept sheets (Side.png, Front.png, and the oblique eye
detail on Description.png) draw the pupil as a CYAN four-pointed star on a
white rim inside a purple almond. It is the single most distinctive graphic
element of a creature that has neither nose nor mouth.

THE AUTHORED VALUE IS CORRECT:
  unnamed02_art.h:111  kStarR = 64, kStarG = 220, kStarB = 240   // cyan
  unnamed02_model.h    make_star_blade() leaves p.page at its default 255
                       (= untextured, flat r/g/b), so no texture is involved.

SO IT IS THE LIGHTING, NOT THE COLOUR OR THE TEXTURE:

  subject                     cyan-family px    near-black px
  u02-s4-unlit (flat colour)       194                0
  u02-s4-front (shipped lit path)    0              288

THE SAME PIXELS, BEFORE AND AFTER. The implementer's own evidence file
evidence/form-front.png contains 288 cyan pixels (72 at native 384x240).
Taking those 72 coordinates and reading them out of the shipped lit render
of the same view:

  n = 72,  mean RGB = (0.0, 1.6, 2.1),  max RGB = (0, 4, 8)
  fraction near-black = 100%

Every pixel that is cyan in the evidence the creature was judged by is pure
black in the render that shipped. This is not a darkened cyan (a shaded cyan
keeps G,B above R); it is zero light.

CONFIRMED IN EVERY SHIPPED CLIP: see look-eyes-allclips.png -- eye crops from
hover, channel, crackle, drift, curious, startle, rest, pirouette and fx-tour.
The star is black in all of them. The teal specks visible nearby are separate
cyan mana motes, not the pupil.

A partial exception: at some orientations part of the star does light (hover
frame 0102 shows star pixels at (33,121,148) beside others at (0,0,0)), so the
star is roughly half-lit-half-black rather than uniformly dead. The dominant
read is still black.

WHY IT SURVIVED TO PUBLISH: the form evidence the eyes were judged against was
rendered on a flat/unlit path, where the star IS cyan. The fault is invisible
in that evidence and visible in every shipped frame. This is the house law's
own failure mode -- a component check passing is not likeness evidence.

OWNER KNOBS THAT MOVE IT (all in unnamed02_art.h):
  kStarR/kStarG/kStarB   the colour -- already correct, changing it will not help
  kPupilStarArmMm  78    star arm half-length
  kPupilStarWideMm 30    blade width      } a larger star presents more lit area
  kPupilStarThinMm 16    blade thinness   } and is the cheapest partial mitigation
  kEyeBulgeMm      88    how proud the star stands off the lens
A proper fix is a lighting/emissive treatment for the star part; zref::creature
RingPart has no emission field today, so that is an implementation change and
not a knob.
