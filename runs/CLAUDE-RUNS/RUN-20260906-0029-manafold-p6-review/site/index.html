Manafold folds its mana.

Every creature is a continuous skinned surface bound to at most 32 bones, compiled by the same reference implementation that defines what the console’s silicon must produce, and rendered one frame at a time at 384 by 240 through the real RGB565 path. Pass 4 names the second creature: MANAFOLD, and the name is a mechanic — its fully boned antenna folds the mana in its ring into recognisable shapes, holds them long enough to read, and kneads them into new ones, continuously, in every clip. The shapes are stencils of glow motes at fixed barycentric weights over the posed antenna joints, so the mana folds because the rig folds — at a distance, never by contact, the way a magnet shapes iron filings. The eyes are rebuilt as all-polygon teardrops (pointy top, round bottom) whose white rings ride the pupil bone — the whites trace the pupils by construction — the smear is depth-correct (the creature occludes its own trail), the interior glow is gone, the mist is one rung more see-through, and directional hits land as an airborne recoil that shatters the held shape.

Manafold

mana conduit

MANAFOLD, pass 6 — a floating mana-storing companion that folds the mana it carries. This pass rebuilt the creature from the concept sheets rather than tuning it. The antenna is no longer a string of beads: the five separate ball parts are gone and their swell lives in the antenna's own skin, so it is ONE continuous surface from the body to the re-entry, with four gentle knuckles where the side sheet draws them — and the free-floating dongle is resolved structurally, because it was a knuckle parented to the body. The eyes are rebuilt from the front sheet: a symmetric lens pointed at both ends, and a single star unit whose white outer star is GENERATED from the cyan inner one, so the two can never disagree. The hinges gained the out-of-plane axis they never had — “up and down separately” was geometrically impossible before — and each now reads the fold envelope at its own lag, so the grip travels up the antenna as a wave instead of every joint closing on the same frame. The channel's mana is now the house treatment on every clip, lightning included. Every clip renders under the many-colour rig at a closer camera. Every shape, colour and timing value is a named, editable knob.

THE MANA MENU, pass 3 — six variants, all alive, every body FILLED (an opaque core under the glow) and anchored in the middle of the ring. The two smear rungs double as the decay/glitch picker.

The drip is cut (it was dead in 579 of 600 frames). The smear is a decaying, deliberately glitchy persistence plane — longer than a normal game trail, never a smooth fade.

Direction 4 opened the eye system for redesign. X1 — the all-polygon teardrop lens with the white annulus riding the PUPIL bone — wins: whites trace the pupils by construction, and the per-ring profile gives the sheet’s pointy-top round-bottom teardrop no two-constant almond could express. X2 (the almond kept, white moved to geometry) is shown beside it; X3, making the texture page track, is refused by the renderer itself — vertex UVs bake once at compile and the sampler has no offset, so a painted ring can never follow a moving star.

EXPERIMENTS, NOT THE SHIPPING LOOK. Ten mechanisms tried against one question: why has the folded mana never read as a nameable shape? The answer turned out not to be the drawing.

The shape was never unreadable because it was badly drawn — it had NOWHERE TO BE READ, competing with a bright pink antenna against a bright pink body inside a 40 px window. Two ingredients fix it and neither is sufficient alone: separation makes it nameable, the edge makes it crisp. None of this is in the clips above yet; it is the next pass's integration.

Zixxtrixx

snake

The first creature, from two concept sheets by S. Hofer. One continuous subtly tapered tube swells into its face rather than wearing a separate skull, then runs through a weight-bearing middle into a thin forked tail. Twenty-eight bones carry the bend, coordinated pupils and boundary-reaching orange stripes; an identity-default fixed-point sidecar adds real positive-volume radial squash only to the authored spring brace. The accepted neutral body and Cool Cross remain fixed while the spring now ARMS BY PEELING: the contact leaves the ground at the front of the grounded stretch and rolls backward along the body until the animal stands on the end of its tail, then compresses hard on it — the face blue resolves into a centred point, and restrained eye and impact language gives each performance its own intention.
