## Objective

Produce exactly ten new, named and editable lighting-only alternatives whose dominant key is physically above the floating-island creature, and expose them through one slow continuous world-space inspection orbit for owner choice without promoting any option.

---

## Scope

**In Scope:**

- Ten new world-space overhead light rigs authored by eye from High Open/Crossfill as directional parents.
- Meaningful variation in elevation, azimuth, open-sky authority, side/top balance, fill softness/tint, ambient floor and sun/moon character.
- One deterministic held-signature-S 360-degree camera orbit with constant target/framing and no cuts.
- Identical native 384x240 orbit renders for all ten; High Open/Crossfill references may be secondary.
- Compact noindex bestiary section, one deployment, bounded production verification and stopped-job closeout.

**Out of Scope:**

- Geometry, proportions, pose or animation changes.
- Texture, pigment, cel threshold, outline or selected-standard changes.
- Catalogue regeneration, spring work, broad regression or release-grade polishing.
- Reopening the closed stale `creature_core` issue absent a new signal.

---

## Constraints

- Exactly ten new options; references do not count.
- Every dominant key arrives from above in fixed world space and visibly illuminates dorsal/top surfaces.
- Fill cannot become a below-light or camera-relative pseudo-key; underside may not exceed top authority.
- Shared deterministic slow orbit, constant target/framing, no cuts.
- Direct build only; recompile all dependent TUs after layout change.
- Fresh isolated clones only; exact staging paths; preserve every archive/v11 asset and exact noindex.
- Publish exactly once after safe dual-main integration.

---

## Don't Retry

- Do not infer camera-facing light direction from one fixed shot. V11 established that a rig can look brighter while still being physically wrong around the creature.
- Do not mechanically sweep angles or gains; author and compare complete native renders.
- Do not use ambient/fill to disguise a key whose vertical authority is wrong.
- Do not run `cmake --build`, Sacengine, the catalogue, or stale compositor diagnostics.
