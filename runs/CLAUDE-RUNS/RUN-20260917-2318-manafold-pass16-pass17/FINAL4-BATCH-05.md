**Subjects:** Mana Blue, Mana Boil, Mana Cyan, Mana Green
**Method:** strict by-eye traversal of every tile in the four supplied every-frame contact sheets. Mana Cyan frames 0325–0399 were also inspected in the enlarged crop `FINAL4-BATCH-05-cyan-phrases.png`; no other full sheet was opened.

## Verdicts

### Mana Blue — PASS

Across 0000–0599 the deep-blue menu mechanism remains a local, pulsing head/inner-shell treatment rather than a copied screen history. Quiet, brightening and fading phrases repeat continuously while the creature stays compact and fully framed. No horizontal trail, duplicate silhouette, empty/grey tile or frame-to-frame tear appears. The pink round body, eye/head read, internal body line and continuous antenna remain attached and legible throughout the full camera cycle; the blue treatment does not grow into a bar or stray polygon. The ending returns to the same stable pose/effect family as the opening without a seam.

### Mana Boil — PASS

The boil is visibly its own alternate menu mechanism: a centred blue-white core expands into a broad circular/annular bloom around the head, holds, then contracts in several complete phrases across 0000–0599. Radius and brightness change progressively rather than popping, dragging a prior frame, or smearing across the screen. At peak boil the luminous disc deliberately dominates the head region, but it remains round, centred on the creature and bounded; the lower body silhouette and internal contour stay readable and the effect clears cleanly afterward. The antenna and rear attachment never detach, framing and ground contact remain stable, and no white-bar collapse, loose triangle or loop discontinuity appears.

### Mana Cyan — PASS

The long sheet contains repeated connected white/cyan figures—angular loops, crossed/diamond-like states and rounded turns—separated by quieter phrases. Their edges change progressively with no wrong held topology or midpoint pop. The enlarged 0325–0399 sequence confirms a continuous angular-figure phrase: orientation and edge emphasis evolve frame by frame while loose cyan/white motes remain scattered around it rather than occupying rigid figure vertices. Nearer antenna/body strokes interrupt portions of the bright figure, and the eyes plus internal body ink remain readable instead of being painted over wholesale. There is no frame-history smear, duplicated body, empty frame, framing loss, stray geometry or end seam.

### Mana Green — PASS

Across all 600 frames the green menu treatment stays a compact local pulse in the head/inner-shell region, with smooth brighten/hold/fade phrases. It does not masquerade as the standard connected-lightning candidate, which is acceptable for this deliberately alternate mechanism, and it does not inherit or introduce a screen-space trail. The creature's round pink mass, eyes, dark internal contour, continuous antenna and rear socket remain stable through the camera cycle. No collapse, white bar, detached geometry, accidental ground clip, framing excursion or loop discontinuity is visible.

## Batch verdict

**PASS — 4/4 subjects.** The two local-fill variants and the boil remain coherent alternate menu mechanisms without smear or structural regression. Mana Cyan preserves connected-shape continuity, depth and a visually independent particle field. No reproducible defect in this batch warrants holding Manafold Pass 16.
