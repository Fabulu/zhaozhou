## By-eye verdict

**The old dense “corrugated vacuum hose” finish does not reproduce.** The band reads as one continuous softly textured surface with five broad swell stations and long smooth spans. The visible light/dark variation is crayon/toon grain over broad forms, not a train of repeated narrow ribs. At both body attachments the black contour resolves as the authored socket/junction silhouette; no detached colour seam or repeated ribbed base is visible across the four views.

The rear-facing f450 view makes the End/socket read most explicit: it remains a single body-attached swell, not a loose bead. The wider f0/f300 views preserve the straight return and continuous opening.

## Decision

**CLOSED for Pass 17. No antenna texture, topology, swell-width or seam edit is authorized.** Direction 14 remains open only on independent public carrier motion/proof. Reopening surface finish would spend risk on a fault absent from the current picture and could regress the accepted continuous non-beaded skin.
