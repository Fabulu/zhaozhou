# Manafold Pass 17 base-eye form ladder

**Binary:** one eye-infrastructure renderer; form supplied only through same-binary diagnostics
**Subjects:** 600-frame Hover orbit and 140-frame fixed-camera Hit

## Evidence

- `PASS17-EYE-FORM-ORBIT.png`: current/A/B/C at f0/f150/f300/f450.
- `PASS17-EYE-FORM-HIT-CROPS.png`: current/A/B/C at fixed-camera Hit f16/f28/f36, 3× crop.

Every rung rendered both complete clips at RC 0.

| Rung | Long × wide × deep (mm) | By-eye read |
|---|---|---|
| Current/legacy | 270 × 84 × 40 | Long dagger; empty violet tip and white/cyan splinter dominate at oblique impact poses. Rejected for Pass 17. |
| A | 250 × 100 × 40 | **Selected.** Wider almond with enough length to keep the pointed identity; star sits inside a readable purple lens at front/three-quarter/oblique views. |
| B | 240 × 105 × 40 | Clearest departure from the dagger, but reads slightly too short/stubby before any large/small acting is added. |
| C | 255 × 110 × 40 | Robust support around the star, but too broad/heavy as the neutral base and leaves less room for authored enlargement. |

## Decision

Select **250 × 100 × 40 mm** as the neutral Pass-17 base lens. The value is authored from the rendered ladder, not generated from the old aspect ratio. Preserve the exact 270 × 84 × 40 legacy tuple as the same-binary rejected control.

The base form only removes the inherited dagger/splinter bias. Public size curves are a separate step: they must make eyes plainly larger and smaller, including asymmetry, without eye-eye contact, disappearance into the body, star/lens deregistration or loss of blink/travel/roll/gaze.
