Root cause
----------
The ring zipper was already outward-wound. Two historical assertions called it
inward. generate_smooth_normals negated its already-correct cross product, and
compose_creatures independently reversed B/C before flat Lambert. Smooth and
flat agreed only because both had been made inward.

World/view trace
----------------
1. compose_creatures constructs each instance world matrix from orientation,
   tilt/bulk and translation, then forms worldm[b] = world * pose[b].
2. Both skin_vertex and skin_normal_lambert consume worldm. The normal therefore
   follows bone/model/instance rotation into world space; translation is absent
   from the normal rows.
3. The selected rig is read once. key_* and fill_* are passed unchanged directly
   to smooth and flat world-space Lambert. No model or view matrix touches them.
4. vp is passed separately only to project_vertex after world-space position and
   Lambert are already known.
5. The inspection orbit changes only SceneView.view_projection by multiplying
   it by rot_world_yaw(theta). It never mutates the pose, instance world matrix,
   or g_creature_light_rig.
6. The 600-frame all-frame sheet shows a stationary held pose and a continuous
   camera turn; illumination stays attached to the physical dorsal/top and fixed
   world azimuth rather than following the camera.

High-dorsal material-response trace
-----------------------------------
For a +Y dorsal normal, the candidate produces key Lambert 59000 and fill
Lambert 56000. creature_light applies ambient + key_gain*Lambert + channel
fill*Lambert, then the existing 1/16 quantizer. The three resulting gains are
(65536,65536,65536), mean 65536. The frozen smooth-cel-main thresholds
(43000,57000) select the bright authored level 82000, or 1.2512 gain.
rast.cpp applies that toon gain to the sampled texture channels and saturates
only after multiplication. In rendered frame 0000, the visibly dorsal pink
stripe at native pixel (240,96) is RGB (255,105,206): the high dorsal Lambert
survives gain, cel quantisation, texture response and output clamping as a bright
pixel. It is not inverted, lost to a wrong channel, or replaced by stale state.
