WHY THAT MATTERS — the "regression" is largely an artifact.
  The crossed arm was shortened deliberately. HEAD manafold_model.h says:
    "the crossed (short) arm must FIT the lens half-width (the reviewer's
     185-vs-125 arithmetic ends here)"
  And dd85b719's own commit subject is:
    "Creature 02 pass 3: reviewer verdict — the star still leaves the lens"

  So pass 3's 164 mm protrusion was PRODUCED BY a star arm poking outside the
  lens — precisely the fault pass 4 was directed to remove. The probe, which
  maxes over all face-region vertices, was partly measuring the fault.

  Re-lengthening the crossed arm to restore 166 mm would RE-BREAK
  "the star stays inside the lens". The correct repair is the one the probe's
  own message names — raise the LENS's own stand-off (kEyeDeepMm / kEyeXMm) —
  and the gate should be re-baselined against lens geometry, not a max over
  every face vertex including a star that is now correctly contained.

  The reviewer's PRESCRIPTION (raise kEyeDeepMm/kEyeXMm) is right.
  Its CAUSE, its severity, and its "one acceptance item broke another" pairing
  are wrong: the pairing is star-containment vs protrusion, not eye-separation
  vs protrusion.
