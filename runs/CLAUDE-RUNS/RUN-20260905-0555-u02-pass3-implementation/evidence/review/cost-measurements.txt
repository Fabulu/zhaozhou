Mana cost -- the reviewer's own numbers. Reference renderer, one target at a
time, back to back on the same machine, ZIXX_EXP=celmain
ZIXX_LIGHT=diagonal-cool-cross.

WALL CLOCK (whole frame, so scene and camera are in it)

  unnamed02-rest       401 frames  no mana,      fixed camera    99 ms/frame
  unnamed02-channel    421 frames  strands+smear+bloom, fixed   130 ms/frame
  u02-trio             421 frames  "three conduits",    fixed   115 ms/frame
  unnamed02-hover      601 frames  no mana,      ORBIT camera   126 ms/frame
  unnamed02-mana-aqua  601 frames  full smeared plasma, fixed    90 ms/frame

Read those last two carefully. Wall clock cannot isolate the mana here: the
ORBIT camera costs more than the entire mana stack. hover, with NO mana at
all, runs 126 ms/frame; mana-aqua, with the full filled plasma AND the
persistence plane, runs 90. Cross-subject timing is not an instrument for
this question, and I am recording that so the next agent does not repeat it.

PIXEL VISITS (the QA's own unit; one full-screen pass = 92,160 visits)

  kStrandCount 3, kBoltSegs 16 segments per strand
  stamps/segment = Manhattan(segment)/kBoltStampMm(22), capped at 24
                 ~3.5 at the ~51 mm segment kStrandSpanMm(820)/16 implies
  per stamp: halo disc r = kBoltHaloRPx 7 -> ~154 px
             core disc r = kBoltCoreRPx 3 -> ~28 px
  3 x 16 x 3.5 x 182 = ~30,600 visits = 33% of one full-screen pass.

  The QA's ~38% is the right order of magnitude. The plan's 0.8% estimate was
  wrong by roughly 40x. FLAG 2 IS CONFIRMED, and the implementer deserves
  credit for surfacing it rather than quietly restating the plan.

WHAT THE COST NOTE LEAVES OUT

  * Every splat with gain_pm != 1000 rebuilds a 64-entry x 3-channel palette
    before it is drawn (both u02_mana_splats loops in zhao_reel.cpp): 192
    writes per splat, and TWICE per splat when the smear plane is on, because
    smear_feed runs its own copy of that loop. At ~340 strand splats that is
    ~65k additional operations per frame -- the same order as the pixel
    visits, and absent from the arithmetic.
  * smear_feed is a whole second traversal of every non-white splat.

WHY IT IS THIS EXPENSIVE: OVERLAP, NOT COVERAGE. kStrandSpanMm 820 lands as
roughly 60-80 px at the shipping camera and carries ~56 stamps. That is about
1.2 px between stamp centres, each stamping a 14 px-diameter disc: every
filament pixel is visited on the order of TEN times. That is the ribbon
argument made concrete -- a FORGE.PRIM ribbon evaluator visits each strand
pixel once and would cut this by about an order of magnitude, with no change
to the look.

"SEVERAL CONDUITS" HAS NEVER BEEN MEASURED BY ANYONE

  zhao_reel.cpp:  if (sub.u02_mana != 0 && ii == 0) { ... mana_fill(...) }

  Mana is filled for INSTANCE 0 ONLY. u02-trio -- the subject whose own note
  reads "THREE phase-offset conduits, one scene sun + bloom, all effects" --
  draws ONE conduit's mana and two plain bodies. That is why it times faster
  than the single-conduit channel (it also pulls the camera back to cam_k
  170000). No subject in the bank has ever rendered several conduits' mana.
  The QA's cost note is headed "three-conduit stack case"; the renderer does
  not run that case.

THE PLAIN ANSWER ASKED FOR

  As shipped, the mana is affordable for ONE conduit in the reference
  renderer -- measured, not argued. For several conduits it is UNMEASURED,
  and the stamped-disc construction is the wrong shape for it: the ~10x
  overdraw is intrinsic to stamping, not to the look the owner picked. It
  becomes affordable at scale once the ribbon block exists. The honest
  statement today is "one conduit, measured; many conduits, unknown, and the
  subject that claims to test it does not".
