B. INSTRUMENT FAULT: trajplot.creature_mask SELECTS THE HORIZON

  unnamed02-fall frame 0000 contains NO creature -- it is above the frame.
  The committed mask still returns 2034 pixels, in a band at y = 175..195
  spanning x = 0..383. That is the terrain horizon line, not the creature.
  Plate 21 shows the selection painted yellow over the frame.

  Row-median background subtraction flags the row where sky meets ground,
  because that row's median is not either of the two things in it. The
  consequence is that every trajplot series on these clips carries a constant
  ~2000 px of horizon, and the frames where the subject is absent still look
  alive. The QA's own headline -- "hover cx/cy p2p 14/13 px with area
  breathing 2054 px -- no flat line on any channel of any clip" -- is stated
  in the same units as the contamination (2054 vs 2034), so the "no flat
  line" conclusion is not established by that instrument.

  This is the FIFTH wrong diagnostic in the family rgbframe.py's own
  docstring catalogues (a contact tracker that classified sky as ground, an
  unsound 2D contact measure, a segmentation that masked in terrain because
  the camera moves, and two channel-rotating readers). It is the same failure
  as the third one on that list.

  FIX: mask by connected component -- take the largest blob and drop
  full-width bands -- or gate on a saturation/hue rule the terrace cannot
  satisfy, and assert a plausible bounding box. Whatever is chosen, the tool
  must report "subject not found" rather than a number.
